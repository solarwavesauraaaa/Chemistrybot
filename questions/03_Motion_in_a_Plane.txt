# 03_Motion_in_a_Plane

Q: The position vector of a particle is r = 3t i + 4t^2 j. The acceleration at t=2s is:
A) 8 j
B) 3 i + 8 j
C) 8 i
D) 3 i + 4 j
Answer: A [v = dr/dt = 3 i + 8t j. a = dv/dt = 8 j (constant).]

Q: A particle moves in a circular path of radius 5 m with uniform speed 10 m/s. The magnitude of centripetal acceleration is:
A) 20 m/s^2
B) 10 m/s^2
C) 50 m/s^2
D) 2 m/s^2
Answer: A [a_c = v^2 / R = 100 / 5 = 20 m/s^2.]

Q: If two vectors A and B are perpendicular, then their scalar product is:
A) AB
B) 0
C) A/B
D) -AB
Answer: B [A.B = |A||B|cos(90) = 0.]

Q: The angle between two vectors A = 3i + 4j and B = 6i + 8j is:
A) 30 degrees
B) 45 degrees
C) 0 degrees
D) 90 degrees
Answer: C [B = 2A. Vectors are parallel, angle = 0.]

Q: The magnitude of resultant of two vectors of magnitudes 3 and 4 with angle 90 degrees is:
A) 7
B) 1
C) 5
D) 12
Answer: C [R = sqrt(3^2 + 4^2) = 5.]

Q: A projectile is fired with velocity u at angle theta. The maximum height reached is:
A) u^2 / 2g
B) u^2 sin^2(theta) / 2g
C) u sin(theta) / g
D) u^2 cos^2(theta) / 2g
Answer: B [H = u^2 sin^2(theta) / 2g.]

Q: The horizontal range of a projectile is maximum for an angle of projection equal to:
A) 30 degrees
B) 45 degrees
C) 60 degrees
D) 90 degrees
Answer: B [R = u^2 sin(2theta) / g. Max when sin(2theta)=1 -> theta=45.]

Q: A body is thrown at an angle 30 degrees to horizontal with velocity 20 m/s. The time of flight is (g=10):
A) 2 s
B) 1 s
C) 4 s
D) 3 s
Answer: A [T = 2u sin(theta) / g = 2*20*0.5 / 10 = 2 s.]

Q: In horizontal projection, the horizontal component of velocity:
A) Increases with time
B) Decreases with time
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero at maximum height
Answer: C [In projectile motion, horizontal acceleration is zero, so horizontal velocity remains constant.]

Q: A particle is moving in a circle of radius 10 m with constant speed 5 m/s. The time period is:
A) 2pi s
B) 4pi s
C) pi s
D) 6pi s
Answer: B [T = 2pi R / v = 2*pi*10 / 5 = 4pi s.]

Q: For a body moving in a circle, the direction of angular velocity vector is:
A) Along the radius
B) Along the tangent
C) Perpendicular to the plane of motion
D) Opposite to the linear velocity
Answer: C [Angular velocity vector is perpendicular to the plane of circular motion.]

Q: Two vectors A and B are such that A + B = A - B. Then:
A) A = 0
B) B = 0
C) A = B
D) A is perpendicular to B
Answer: B [A + B = A - B -> 2B = 0 -> B = 0.]

Q: A projectile has maximum range of 100 m. Its maximum height is:
A) 50 m
B) 25 m
C) 75 m
D) 100 m
Answer: B [Rmax = 4Hmax. H = R/4 = 25 m.]

Q: A particle moves along x-axis with velocity v = 3t^2. The acceleration at t=2s is:
A) 12 m/s^2
B) 6 m/s^2
C) 18 m/s^2
D) 24 m/s^2
Answer: A [a = dv/dt = 6t. At t=2, a = 12 m/s^2.]

Q: The angle between two vectors A and B is 60 degrees. If |A| = 2 and |B| = 3, the magnitude of their scalar product is:
A) 6
B) 3
C) 3 sqrt(3)
D) 6 sqrt(3)
Answer: B [A.B = 2*3*cos(60) = 6*0.5 = 3.]

Q: The velocity of a projectile at the highest point is:
A) Zero
B) u cos(theta)
C) u sin(theta)
D) u
Answer: B [At highest point, vertical velocity is zero. Only horizontal component u cos(theta) remains.]

Q: A particle is moving in a circle with uniform speed. The acceleration is:
A) Directed towards center
B) Directed along tangent
C) Zero
D) Directed outward
Answer: A [Centripetal acceleration is directed toward the center.]

Q: A stone thrown horizontally from the top of a tower with velocity 10 m/s. The horizontal distance covered in 2 seconds (g=10):
A) 20 m
B) 10 m
C) 40 m
D) 5 m
Answer: A [Horizontal distance = velocity * time = 10*2 = 20 m.]

Q: The magnitude of cross product of two unit vectors at 90 degrees is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) sqrt(2)
Answer: B [|A x B| = |A||B|sin(90) = 1*1*1 = 1.]

Q: A projectile is fired at 45 degrees with velocity 20 m/s. The horizontal range is (g=10):
A) 40 m
B) 20 m
C) 80 m
D) 10 m
Answer: A [R = u^2 sin(90) / g = 400/10 = 40 m.]

Q: In uniform circular motion, the work done by centripetal force is:
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Maximum
Answer: A [Force is perpendicular to displacement, work done = 0.]

Q: The direction of velocity vector at any point on the circular path is:
A) Along radius
B) Along tangent
C) Perpendicular to both radius and tangent
D) Along the circumference
Answer: B [Velocity vector is always along the tangent to the path.]

Q: A ball is projected horizontally from a height 20 m with velocity 15 m/s. The time taken to reach ground is (g=10):
A) 2 s
B) 3 s
C) 1 s
D) 4 s
Answer: A [Vertical motion: h = 0.5 g t^2 -> 20 = 5 t^2 -> t = 2 s.]

Q: For a particle moving in a circle of radius 2 m with angular velocity 3 rad/s, the linear velocity is:
A) 6 m/s
B) 2 m/s
C) 9 m/s
D) 3 m/s
Answer: A [v = omega * R = 3*2 = 6 m/s.]

Q: If the angle between two vectors is 120 degrees and their magnitudes are 3 and 4, the magnitude of their resultant is:
A) sqrt(37)
B) sqrt(13)
C) sqrt(25)
D) sqrt(49)
Answer: B [R = sqrt(3^2 + 4^2 + 2*3*4*cos(120)) = sqrt(9 + 16 - 12) = sqrt(13).]

Q: A particle starts from origin with velocity 2i + 3j and acceleration 4i + 2j. The position vector at t=2s is:
A) 12i + 10j
B) 8i + 6j
C) 6i + 8j
D) 10i + 12j
Answer: A [r = ut + 0.5 a t^2 = (2i+3j)*2 + 0.5*(4i+2j)*4 = 4i+6j + 8i+4j = 12i+10j.]

Q: The relation between linear velocity and angular velocity is:
A) v = omega / R
B) v = omega * R
C) v = R / omega
D) v = omega^2 R
Answer: B [v = omega * R.]

Q: If two vectors A and B are such that |A x B| = A.B, then the angle between them is:
A) 30 degrees
B) 45 degrees
C) 60 degrees
D) 90 degrees
Answer: B [AB sin(theta) = AB cos(theta) -> sin(theta)=cos(theta) -> theta=45.]

Q: For a projectile, the angle between velocity and acceleration at the highest point is:
A) 0 degrees
B) 90 degrees
C) 180 degrees
D) 45 degrees
Answer: B [At highest point, velocity is horizontal, acceleration is vertical downward. Angle = 90.]

Q: In circular motion, if the speed is doubled, the centripetal acceleration becomes:
A) 4 times
B) 2 times
C) 8 times
D) 16 times
Answer: A [a_c = v^2 / R. If v is doubled, a becomes 4 times.]

Q: The time period of a particle moving in a circle of radius R with speed v is:
A) 2pi R / v
B) pi R / v
C) v / 2pi R
D) 2pi v / R
Answer: A [T = 2pi R / v.]

Q: A particle is moving with position r = 2t^2 i + 3t j. The velocity at t=1s is:
A) 4i + 3j
B) 2i + 3j
C) 4i + 6j
D) 2i + 6j
Answer: A [v = dr/dt = 4t i + 3j. At t=1, v = 4i + 3j.]

Q: A stone is projected from ground with velocity 25 m/s at 37 degrees. The time of flight is (sin37=0.6, g=10):
A) 3 s
B) 4 s
C) 2 s
D) 5 s
Answer: A [T = 2u sin(theta)/g = 2*25*0.6/10 = 3 s.]

Q: Which of the following is a vector?
A) Energy
B) Work
C) Torque
D) Power
Answer: C [Torque is a vector quantity (axial vector). Energy, Work, and Power are scalars.]

Q: Two vectors A and B are perpendicular. Their resultant magnitude is 5. If A = 3, then B is:
A) 4
B) 5
C) 3
D) 7
Answer: A [sqrt(3^2 + B^2) = 5 -> B^2 = 16 -> B = 4.]

Q: A body is moving in a circle with constant speed. The acceleration vector:
A) Is constant in magnitude and direction
B) Is constant in direction but not magnitude
C) Is constant in magnitude but changes direction
D) Changes both magnitude and direction
Answer: C [In uniform circular motion, magnitude of acceleration is constant but direction continuously changes toward center.]

Q: A projectile is fired with velocity u at angle 60 degrees to horizontal. The time of flight is:
A) sqrt(3) u / g
B) u / g
C) 2u / g
D) u sqrt(3) / 2g
Answer: A [T = 2u sin(60)/g = 2u * sqrt(3)/2 / g = sqrt(3) u / g.]

Q: The horizontal component of velocity of a projectile is 10 m/s. The time of flight is 4 s. The horizontal range is:
A) 40 m
B) 20 m
C) 80 m
D) 10 m
Answer: A [Range = horizontal velocity * time of flight = 10*4 = 40 m.]

Q: A particle moves such that its displacement x = 3t^2 - 2t + 5. The acceleration is:
A) 6 m/s^2
B) 3 m/s^2
C) 4 m/s^2
D) 2 m/s^2
Answer: A [v = 6t - 2. a = 6 m/s^2 (constant).]

Q: At the topmost point of a projectile, the acceleration is:
A) Zero
B) g downward
C) g horizontal
D) 2g downward
Answer: B [Acceleration due to gravity is always g downward, even at the top.]

Q: Which physical quantity remains constant in projectile motion?
A) Horizontal velocity
B) Vertical velocity
C) Total velocity
D) Acceleration
Answer: A [Horizontal velocity is constant; vertical changes due to gravity.]

Q: The angle made by the resultant of two vectors A and B (with magnitudes equal to A) with vector A when angle between them is 60 degrees is:
A) 30 degrees
B) 60 degrees
C) 90 degrees
D) 45 degrees
Answer: A [If |A|=|B| and angle=60, resultant bisects the angle, making 30 with A.]

Q: A particle is moving in a plane with position vector r = 2t i + 4t^2 j. The velocity vector at t=1s is:
A) 2i + 8j
B) 2i + 4j
C) 4i + 8j
D) 4i + 4j
Answer: A [v = dr/dt = 2 i + 8t j. At t=1, v = 2i + 8j.]

Q: The time of flight of a projectile is 6 s. The maximum height reached is (g=10):
A) 45 m
B) 90 m
C) 30 m
D) 60 m
Answer: A [T = 2u sin(theta)/g = 6 -> u sin(theta) = 30. H = (u sin(theta))^2 / 2g = 900/20 = 45 m.]
