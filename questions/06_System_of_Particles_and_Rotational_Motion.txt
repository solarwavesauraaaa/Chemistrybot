# 06_System_of_Particles_and_Rotational_Motion

Q: The center of mass of a uniform rod lies at its:
A) One end
B) Midpoint
C) Center of gravity
D) Both B and C
Answer: D [For a uniform rod, center of mass coincides with center of gravity and lies at its midpoint.]

Q: The velocity of center of mass of a system of particles is given by:
A) (m1v1 + m2v2 + ...) / (m1 + m2 + ...)
B) (m1v1 - m2v2) / (m1 + m2)
C) (v1 + v2) / 2
D) (m1 + m2) / (v1 + v2)
Answer: A [Velocity of COM = Total momentum / Total mass = (sum mi vi) / (sum mi).]

Q: The center of mass of a system of particles moves with constant velocity if:
A) Net external force is zero
B) Net internal force is zero
C) Total kinetic energy is zero
D) Total momentum is variable
Answer: A [If net external force is zero, acceleration of COM is zero, so it moves with constant velocity.]

Q: Moment of inertia depends on:
A) Mass of the body
B) Distribution of mass
C) Position of axis of rotation
D) All of these
Answer: D [I = sum mi ri^2, so it depends on mass, distribution, and axis position.]

Q: The SI unit of moment of inertia is:
A) kg m^2
B) kg m
C) kg m^-2
D) kg m/s^2
Answer: A [Moment of inertia = mass * (distance)^2. Unit = kg m^2.]

Q: The moment of inertia of a solid sphere about its diameter is:
A) 0.4 MR^2
B) 0.5 MR^2
C) 0.6 MR^2
D) 0.8 MR^2
Answer: A [I_solid_sphere = (2/5) MR^2 = 0.4 MR^2.]

Q: The radius of gyration (K) of a body is related to moment of inertia I and mass M by:
A) I = M K^2
B) I = M / K^2
C) I = M K
D) I = K / M
Answer: A [I = M K^2, where K is the radius of gyration.]

Q: The angular momentum (L) of a particle is given by:
A) r x p
B) p x r
C) r . p
D) m v r
Answer: A [L = r x p, where r is position vector and p is linear momentum.]

Q: The SI unit of angular momentum is:
A) kg m^2/s
B) kg m/s
C) kg m^2/s^2
D) kg m/s^2
Answer: A [Angular momentum = mvr. Unit = kg * m^2 / s.]

Q: The torque (tau) acting on a particle is given by:
A) r x F
B) F x r
C) r . F
D) F / r
Answer: A [Torque = r x F (cross product of position vector and force).]

Q: The relation between torque and angular momentum is:
A) tau = dL / dt
B) tau = L / t
C) tau = L * t
D) tau = dL / dr
Answer: A [tau = dL/dt, which is the rotational analogue of F = dp/dt.]

Q: The law of conservation of angular momentum states that if net external torque is zero:
A) Angular momentum is constant
B) Linear momentum is constant
C) Angular velocity is constant
D) Torque is constant
Answer: A [If tau_ext = 0, then L = constant.]

Q: A particle of mass m is moving in a circular path of radius r with speed v. The angular momentum about the center is:
A) m v r
B) m v / r
C) m v^2 r
D) m v r^2
Answer: A [L = m v r.]

Q: The rotational kinetic energy of a body is given by:
A) 0.5 I omega^2
B) 0.5 m v^2
C) I omega
D) 0.5 I omega
Answer: A [K_rot = 0.5 I omega^2.]

Q: Which theorem relates moment of inertia about a parallel axis?
A) Parallel axis theorem
B) Perpendicular axis theorem
C) Work-energy theorem
D) Bernoulli's theorem
Answer: A [Parallel axis theorem: I = I_cm + M d^2.]

Q: The moment of inertia of a uniform disk about its central axis is:
A) 0.5 MR^2
B) 0.25 MR^2
C) 0.75 MR^2
D) MR^2
Answer: A [I_disk = 0.5 MR^2.]

Q: The moment of inertia of a hollow sphere about its diameter is:
A) (2/3) MR^2
B) (2/5) MR^2
C) 0.5 MR^2
D) 0.4 MR^2
Answer: A [I_hollow_sphere = (2/3) MR^2.]

Q: Which quantity is conserved in pure rolling motion?
A) Angular momentum
B) Linear momentum
C) Mechanical energy
D) All of these
Answer: D [In pure rolling, mechanical energy is conserved if no friction does work, angular momentum and linear momentum are conserved if no external force/torque.]

Q: The ratio of rotational kinetic energy to translational kinetic energy for a rolling solid sphere is:
A) 2/5
B) 2/7
C) 5/2
D) 7/2
Answer: A [K_rot / K_trans = 0.5 I omega^2 / 0.5 m v^2. For sphere I = 0.4 mR^2 and omega = v/R. Ratio = 0.4 = 2/5.]

Q: A solid sphere and a hollow sphere of same mass and radius roll down an inclined plane. Which reaches the bottom first?
A) Solid sphere
B) Hollow sphere
C) Both reach together
D) Depends on mass
Answer: A [Solid sphere has smaller moment of inertia, so it has greater acceleration and reaches first.]

Q: The center of mass of a system of three particles of equal mass at the vertices of an equilateral triangle is:
A) At the centroid
B) At the center of the triangle
C) At the circumcenter
D) At one vertex
Answer: A [For equal masses at vertices, COM lies at the centroid of the triangle.]

Q: The velocity of a point on a rolling wheel is zero at:
A) The topmost point
B) The bottommost point
C) The center of the wheel
D) The horizontal diameter ends
Answer: B [In pure rolling, the point of contact (bottommost) has zero instantaneous velocity.]

Q: The angular momentum of a rotating body about its axis is conserved if:
A) No external torque acts
B) No external force acts
C) The body is in translational equilibrium
D) The net force is zero
Answer: A [Angular momentum is conserved when net external torque is zero.]

Q: A wheel of radius R rolls without slipping. The velocity of its center is v. The angular velocity of the wheel is:
A) v / R
B) v * R
C) v^2 / R
D) R / v
Answer: A [For pure rolling, v = omega * R, so omega = v / R.]

Q: The work done by a torque in rotating a body through angle theta is:
A) tau * theta
B) 0.5 tau * theta
C) tau / theta
D) tau + theta
Answer: A [Work done = torque * angular displacement = tau * theta.]

Q: The power delivered by a torque is:
A) tau * omega
B) tau / omega
C) tau * theta
D) tau / t
Answer: A [Power = torque * angular velocity = tau * omega.]

Q: A force of 10 N is applied tangentially to a wheel of radius 0.5 m. The torque produced is:
A) 5 N m
B) 10 N m
C) 2.5 N m
D) 20 N m
Answer: A [tau = F * r = 10 * 0.5 = 5 N m.]

Q: A particle of mass 2 kg moves in a circle of radius 3 m with speed 4 m/s. The angular momentum about the center is:
A) 24 kg m^2/s
B) 12 kg m^2/s
C) 6 kg m^2/s
D) 48 kg m^2/s
Answer: A [L = m v r = 2 * 4 * 3 = 24 kg m^2/s.]

Q: For a rigid body, the relation between linear velocity and angular velocity for a point at distance r from the axis is:
A) v = omega * r
B) v = omega / r
C) v = r / omega
D) v = omega^2 r
Answer: A [v = omega * r.]

Q: The moment of inertia of a thin uniform rod of mass M and length L about an axis passing through its center and perpendicular to its length is:
A) ML^2 / 12
B) ML^2 / 3
C) ML^2 / 6
D) ML^2 / 2
Answer: A [I_center = ML^2 / 12.]

Q: The moment of inertia of the same rod about an axis through one end perpendicular to its length is:
A) ML^2 / 3
B) ML^2 / 12
C) ML^2 / 6
D) ML^2 / 2
Answer: A [Using parallel axis theorem: I_end = I_cm + M(L/2)^2 = ML^2/12 + ML^2/4 = ML^2/3.]

Q: A disc and a ring of equal mass and radius roll down an incline. Which has more rotational kinetic energy?
A) Ring
B) Disc
C) Both equal
D) Depends on height
Answer: A [Ring has larger moment of inertia, so it has greater rotational kinetic energy for the same total kinetic energy.]

Q: The angular momentum of a body changes from 10 to 20 kg m^2/s in 2 seconds. The average torque acting is:
A) 5 N m
B) 10 N m
C) 15 N m
D) 20 N m
Answer: A [tau = delta_L / delta_t = (20-10)/2 = 5 N m.]

Q: A particle of mass m is moving with constant velocity. The angular momentum about a fixed point:
A) Is zero if the point is on the path
B) Is constant if the point is not on the path
C) Varies with time
D) Is always zero
Answer: B [If the particle moves with constant velocity, its angular momentum about any fixed point is constant.]

Q: A wheel of moment of inertia 2 kg m^2 is rotating with angular velocity 10 rad/s. The kinetic energy is:
A) 100 J
B) 200 J
C) 50 J
D) 20 J
Answer: A [K = 0.5 I omega^2 = 0.5 * 2 * 100 = 100 J.]

Q: The radius of gyration of a uniform rod of length L about its center is:
A) L / sqrt(12)
B) L / 2
C) L / sqrt(3)
D) L / 6
Answer: A [K = sqrt(I/M) = sqrt(ML^2/12 / M) = L / sqrt(12).]

Q: A solid cylinder rolls without slipping down an incline. The acceleration of its center of mass is:
A) g sin(theta) / (1 + I/MR^2)
B) g sin(theta)
C) g cos(theta)
D) g tan(theta)
Answer: A [a = g sin(theta) / (1 + I/MR^2).]

Q: For a system of particles, the center of mass is:
A) The point where total mass is concentrated
B) The weighted average of positions
C) The geometric center of the system
D) Both A and B
Answer: D [COM is the weighted average of positions where total mass is assumed concentrated.]

Q: A diver performing a somersault pulls his legs and arms to:
A) Increase angular velocity
B) Decrease angular velocity
C) Increase angular momentum
D) Decrease angular momentum
Answer: A [Pulling limbs decreases moment of inertia, so angular velocity increases to conserve angular momentum.]

Q: The ratio of moments of inertia of a solid sphere and a hollow sphere of same mass and radius is:
A) 3/5
B) 2/5
C) 5/3
D) 4/5
Answer: A [Solid = 2/5 MR^2, Hollow = 2/3 MR^2. Ratio = (2/5)/(2/3) = 3/5.]

Q: The condition for pure rolling is:
A) v = omega * R
B) v > omega * R
C) v < omega * R
D) v = 0 at contact
Answer: A [For pure rolling without slipping, v_center = omega * R.]

Q: Which of the following is not a vector quantity?
A) Angular velocity
B) Angular acceleration
C) Torque
D) Moment of inertia
Answer: D [Moment of inertia is a scalar (or tensor) quantity, not a vector.]

Q: The angular velocity of a rotating body is 5 rad/s. If the radius of gyration is 2 m, the linear velocity of a point at that distance is:
A) 10 m/s
B) 5 m/s
C) 2 m/s
D) 20 m/s
Answer: A [v = omega * K = 5 * 2 = 10 m/s.]

Q: A couple consists of two equal and opposite forces separated by distance d. The torque is:
A) F * d
B) F / d
C) F + d
D) F - d
Answer: A [Torque of a couple = magnitude of one force * perpendicular distance.]

Q: The angular momentum of a rigid body is given by:
A) I * omega
B) 0.5 I omega^2
C) m v r
D) I * alpha
Answer: A [L = I * omega for a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis.]

Q: A thin hoop of radius R rolls down an incline. The ratio of its rotational to translational kinetic energy is:
A) 1
B) 0.5
C) 0.67
D) 0.4
Answer: A [For a hoop, I = MR^2. K_rot/K_trans = (0.5 MR^2 omega^2)/(0.5 M v^2) = 1.]
