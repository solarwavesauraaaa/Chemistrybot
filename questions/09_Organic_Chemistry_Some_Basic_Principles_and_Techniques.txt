Q: The correct order of bond length of the C–C bonds in the following compounds is
A) CH₃–CH₃ > CH₂=CH₂ > HC≡CH
B) HC≡CH > CH₂=CH₂ > CH₃–CH₃
C) CH₂=CH₂ > CH₃–CH₃ > HC≡CH
D) CH₃–CH₃ > HC≡CH > CH₂=CH₂
Answer: A [Explanation: Higher s-character shortens the bond. sp³ (25% s) > sp² (33% s) > sp (50% s).]

Q: In which of the following molecules is the carbon atom most electronegative?
A) CH₃–CH₃
B) CH₂=CH₂
C) HC≡CH
D) CH₃Cl
Answer: C [Explanation: sp hybridised carbon has maximum s-character (50%) and is most electronegative.]
