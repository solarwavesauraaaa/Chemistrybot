Q: According to Werner's theory, the primary valence of cobalt in [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃ is
A) 3
B) 6
C) 9
D) 0
Answer: A [Explanation: Primary valence is ionisable and equal to the oxidation number of the metal.]

Q: The secondary valence of Co in [CoCl₂(NH₃)₄]Cl is
A) 4
B) 6
C) 3
D) 2
Answer: B [Explanation: Secondary valence = coordination number = 6.]

Q: Which of the following is a homoleptic complex?

B) [Ni(CO)₄]
C) [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]²⁺
D) [Fe(CN)₅(NO)]²⁻
Answer: B [Explanation: Homoleptic complexes contain only one type of ligand.]

C) pentaamminechloridocobalt(II) chloride

Q: The complex [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ shows
A) geometrical isomerism only
B) optical isomerism only
C) both geometrical and optical isomerism

Answer: A [Explanation: cis-trans (geometrical) isomers; both are optically inactive.]

Q: Which of the following complexes will show optical isomerism?

B) [Co(en)₃]³⁺
C) [Co(NH₃)₃Cl₃]

Q: The hybridisation of Ni in [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ is
A) sp³
B) dsp²
C) sp³d²

Q: The magnetic moment of [NiCl₄]²⁻ is

Q: According to crystal field theory, the splitting energy (Δ₀) is highest for
A) I⁻
B) Cl⁻
C) H₂O
D) CN⁻
Answer: D [Explanation: CN⁻ is a strong field ligand and produces maximum splitting.]

Q: The correct order of spectrochemical series is

Q: [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ is coloured due to
A) d–d transition

Q: The number of geometrical isomers of [Ma₃b₃] type complex is
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 1
Answer: A [Explanation: facial and meridional isomers.]

Q: Which of the following is an outer orbital complex?
A) [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺
B) [CoF₆]³⁻
C) [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻
D) [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻

Q: The oxidation number of Co in [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]Cl₂ is
A) +2
B) +3
C) +1
D) 0
Answer: B [Explanation: NH₃ neutral, Cl⁻ inside = -1, two Cl⁻ outside = -2 → Co = +3.]

Q: Assertion (A): [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ is diamagnetic while [NiCl₄]²⁻ is paramagnetic.

A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Strong field CN⁻ causes pairing; weak field Cl⁻ does not.]

Q: The coordination number of Cr in [Cr(H₂O)₄Cl₂]Cl is

Q: Which of the following shows linkage isomerism?
