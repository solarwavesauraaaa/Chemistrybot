# 16_Electrostatic_Potential_and_Capacitance

Q: The electric potential at a point is defined as:
A) Work done per unit positive charge
B) Force per unit charge
C) Energy per unit volume
D) Charge per unit area
Answer: A [Electric potential V = Work/Charge = W/q.]

Q: The SI unit of electric potential is:
A) Volt
B) Coulomb
C) Joule
D) Watt
Answer: A [Volt (V) is SI unit of electric potential.]

Q: The electric potential at a distance r from a point charge q is:
A) V = k q / r
B) V = k q / r^2
C) V = k q r
D) V = k q / r^3
Answer: A [V = k q / r.]

Q: The electric potential due to a system of charges is:
A) The scalar sum of potentials due to each charge
B) The vector sum of potentials
C) The product of potentials
D) The average of potentials
Answer: A [Potential is a scalar, so we add algebraically.]

Q: The potential at a point due to a dipole is:
A) Zero at the equatorial plane
B) Maximum at the equatorial plane
C) Zero on the axis
D) Maximum on the axis
Answer: A [V_equatorial = 0.]

Q: The work done to move a charge q from infinity to a point where potential is V is:
A) W = qV
B) W = V/q
C) W = q/V
D) W = qV^2
Answer: A [W = qV.]

Q: The potential energy of a system of two charges q1 and q2 separated by r is:
A) U = k q1 q2 / r
B) U = - k q1 q2 / r
C) U = k q1 q2 / r^2
D) U = - k q1 q2 / r^2
Answer: A [U = k q1 q2 / r.]

Q: The capacitance of a conductor is defined as:
A) C = Q / V
B) C = Q * V
C) C = V / Q
D) C = Q / V^2
Answer: A [C = Q / V.]

Q: The SI unit of capacitance is:
A) Farad
B) Coulomb
C) Volt
D) Ohm
Answer: A [Farad (F) is SI unit of capacitance.]

Q: The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is:
A) C = epsilon0 A / d
B) C = epsilon0 d / A
C) C = A / (epsilon0 d)
D) C = epsilon0 A d
Answer: A [C = epsilon0 A / d.]

Q: The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor with a dielectric of dielectric constant K is:
A) C = K epsilon0 A / d
B) C = epsilon0 A / (K d)
C) C = K epsilon0 d / A
D) C = epsilon0 / (K A d)
Answer: A [C = K epsilon0 A / d.]

Q: The equivalent capacitance of capacitors in parallel is:
A) C_eq = C1 + C2 + ...
B) 1/C_eq = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + ...
C) C_eq = C1 * C2 / (C1 + C2)
D) C_eq = C1 / C2
Answer: A [Parallel: C_eq = sum of individual capacitances.]

Q: The equivalent capacitance of capacitors in series is:
A) 1/C_eq = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + ...
B) C_eq = C1 + C2 + ...
C) C_eq = C1 * C2 / (C1 + C2)
D) C_eq = C1 / C2
Answer: A [Series: 1/C_eq = sum of reciprocals.]

Q: The energy stored in a capacitor is:
A) U = 0.5 C V^2
B) U = C V^2
C) U = 0.5 C V
D) U = C V
Answer: A [U = 0.5 C V^2.]

Q: The energy density of a capacitor is:
A) u = 0.5 epsilon0 E^2
B) u = epsilon0 E^2
C) u = 0.5 epsilon0 E
D) u = epsilon0 E
Answer: A [Energy density u = 0.5 epsilon0 E^2.]

Q: The capacitance of a spherical conductor of radius R is:
A) C = 4 pi epsilon0 R
B) C = 2 pi epsilon0 R
C) C = 4 pi epsilon0 R^2
D) C = epsilon0 R
Answer: A [C = 4 pi epsilon0 R.]

Q: The capacitance of an isolated sphere is:
A) 4 pi epsilon0 R
B) 2 pi epsilon0 R
C) 4 pi epsilon0 R^2
D) pi epsilon0 R
Answer: A [C = 4 pi epsilon0 R.]

Q: The potential of a charged conductor is:
A) Constant throughout the conductor
B) Maximum at the surface
C) Maximum at the center
D) Zero at the center
Answer: A [In electrostatic equilibrium, potential is constant throughout a conductor.]

Q: The electric potential inside a charged conductor is:
A) Same as on the surface
B) Greater than on the surface
C) Less than on the surface
D) Zero
Answer: A [Potential is constant and equal to surface potential.]

Q: The electric field at the surface of a charged conductor is:
A) Perpendicular to the surface
B) Parallel to the surface
C) Zero
D) At 45 degrees to the surface
Answer: A [Field is perpendicular to the surface.]

Q: The potential difference between two points is defined as:
A) Work done per unit charge to move between them
B) Force per unit charge
C) Energy per unit volume
D) Charge per unit area
Answer: A [V_AB = W/q.]

Q: The amount of charge stored on a capacitor of 2 µF when connected to a 10 V battery is:
A) 20 µC
B) 2 µC
C) 10 µC
D) 5 µC
Answer: A [Q = C * V = 2 * 10 = 20 µC.]

Q: A capacitor of 4 µF is charged to 100 V. The energy stored is:
A) 0.02 J
B) 0.04 J
C) 0.01 J
D) 0.08 J
Answer: A [U = 0.5 * 4e-6 * 10000 = 0.02 J.]

Q: The energy stored in a capacitor is proportional to:
A) C V^2
B) C V
C) V / C
D) V^2 / C
Answer: A [U ∝ C V^2.]

Q: When a dielectric slab is inserted between the plates of a capacitor, its capacitance:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [Inserting dielectric increases capacitance by factor K.]

Q: The electric field in a dielectric is reduced by a factor of:
A) K
B) 1/K
C) K^2
D) 1/K^2
Answer: A [E = E0 / K.]

Q: The polarization of a dielectric is proportional to:
A) The applied electric field
B) The capacitance
C) The voltage
D) The area
Answer: A [P = chi_e epsilon0 E.]

Q: The dielectric strength of a material is the maximum:
A) Electric field it can withstand
B) Voltage it can withstand
C) Charge it can store
D) Current it can carry
Answer: A [Dielectric strength is max E-field before breakdown.]

Q: If the plate area of a parallel plate capacitor is doubled, its capacitance:
A) Doubles
B) Halves
C) Remains constant
D) Quadruples
Answer: A [C ∝ A. So, if A doubles, C doubles.]

Q: If the distance between plates of a parallel plate capacitor is halved, its capacitance:
A) Doubles
B) Halves
C) Remains constant
D) Quadruples
Answer: A [C ∝ 1/d. If d halves, C doubles.]

Q: The equivalent capacitance of two 6 µF capacitors in series is:
A) 3 µF
B) 12 µF
C) 6 µF
D) 2 µF
Answer: A [1/C = 1/6 + 1/6 = 1/3 -> C = 3 µF.]

Q: The equivalent capacitance of two 6 µF capacitors in parallel is:
A) 12 µF
B) 3 µF
C) 6 µF
D) 2 µF
Answer: A [C = 6 + 6 = 12 µF.]

Q: A capacitor stores charge Q at voltage V. If voltage is doubled, the charge becomes:
A) 2Q
B) Q/2
C) 4Q
D) Q/4
Answer: A [Q = C V. If V doubles, Q doubles.]

Q: The potential energy of a capacitor is stored in:
A) The electric field between the plates
B) The plates
C) The dielectric
D) The connecting wires
Answer: A [Energy is stored in the electric field.]

Q: The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor with air between plates is C. If a dielectric of K = 4 is inserted, the new capacitance is:
A) 4C
B) C/4
C) 2C
D) C
Answer: A [C' = K C = 4C.]

Q: The capacitance of a capacitor depends on:
A) Its physical dimensions and dielectric
B) The charge stored
C) The voltage applied
D) Both B and C
Answer: A [Capacitance depends on geometry and dielectric.]

Q: The electric potential at the surface of a charged sphere of radius R is:
A) V = k Q / R
B) V = k Q / R^2
C) V = k Q R
D) V = k Q / R^3
Answer: A [V = k Q / R.]

Q: The potential due to a point charge at a distance r is V. If the distance is doubled, the potential becomes:
A) V / 2
B) V / 4
C) 2V
D) 4V
Answer: A [V ∝ 1/r. So V' = V/2.]

Q: A charge q is moved in an electric field. The work done is zero if the path is:
A) On an equipotential surface
B) Along a field line
C) Perpendicular to field lines
D) At an angle to field lines
Answer: A [On equipotential surface, V is constant, so W = 0.]

Q: A capacitor of 10 µF is connected to a 12 V battery. The charge on the capacitor is:
A) 120 µC
B) 12 µC
C) 1.2 µC
D) 0.12 µC
Answer: A [Q = C V = 10 * 12 = 120 µC.]

Q: The force between the plates of a charged parallel plate capacitor is:
A) Q^2 / (2 epsilon0 A)
B) Q^2 / (epsilon0 A)
C) Q / (2 epsilon0 A)
D) Q / (epsilon0 A)
Answer: A [F = Q^2 / (2 epsilon0 A).]

Q: When a dielectric is inserted in a charged capacitor (battery disconnected), the electric field:
A) Decreases
B) Increases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [With battery disconnected, Q is constant. E = E0 / K, so decreases.]

Q: When a dielectric is inserted in a capacitor connected to a battery, the charge:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [With battery connected, V is constant. Q = K Q0, so increases.]

Q: A capacitor is charged to 100 V and then isolated. A dielectric of K = 2 is inserted. The new potential difference is:
A) 50 V
B) 100 V
C) 200 V
D) 25 V
Answer: A [Q constant. V = V0 / K = 100 / 2 = 50 V.]

Q: The capacitance of a cylindrical capacitor is:
A) 2 pi epsilon0 L / ln(b/a)
B) 4 pi epsilon0 L / ln(b/a)
C) 2 pi epsilon0 / ln(b/a)
D) pi epsilon0 L / ln(b/a)
Answer: A [C = 2 pi epsilon0 L / ln(b/a).]

Q: The energy stored in a capacitor is 1 J. If the voltage is doubled, the energy becomes:
A) 4 J
B) 2 J
C) 1 J
D) 0.5 J
Answer: A [U ∝ V^2. If V doubles, U becomes 4 times.]

Q: The equivalent capacitance of three capacitors 2 µF, 3 µF, and 6 µF in series is:
A) 1 µF
B) 2 µF
C) 3 µF
D) 6 µF
Answer: A [1/C = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 = 1 -> C = 1 µF.]
