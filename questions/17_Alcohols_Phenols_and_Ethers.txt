Q: Which of the following is a tertiary alcohol?
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂OH
B) (CH₃)₂CHOH
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₂=CH–CH₂OH
Answer: C [Explanation: –OH attached to a tertiary carbon.]

Q: The correct IUPAC name of (CH₃)₂C(OH)CH₂CH₃ is
A) 2-methylbutan-2-ol
B) 2-methylbutan-3-ol
C) 3-methylbutan-2-ol
D) 2-ethylpropan-2-ol
Answer: A [Explanation: Longest chain of 4 carbons with –OH at C-2.]

Q: Which of the following alcohols is most acidic?
A) CH₃OH
B) CH₃CH₂OH
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CF₃CH₂OH
Answer: D [Explanation: Strong –I effect of three fluorine atoms stabilises the alkoxide ion.]

Q: Phenol is more acidic than ethanol because
A) phenoxide ion is stabilised by resonance
B) ethoxide ion is stabilised by resonance
C) phenol has higher molecular weight
D) ethanol has intermolecular H-bonding
Answer: A [Explanation: Delocalisation of negative charge in phenoxide ion.]

Q: The correct order of acidic strength is
A) p-nitrophenol > phenol > o-cresol
B) phenol > p-nitrophenol > o-cresol
C) o-cresol > phenol > p-nitrophenol
D) p-nitrophenol > o-cresol > phenol
Answer: A [Explanation: –NO₂ is electron-withdrawing (increases acidity); –CH₃ is electron-releasing (decreases acidity).]

Q: Which of the following reagents is used for the industrial preparation of phenol from cumene?
A) O₂ followed by dilute acid
B) H₂SO₄
C) NaOH
D) Cl₂/Fe
Answer: A [Explanation: Cumene is oxidised by air and then cleaved by acid to give phenol and acetone.]

Q: The major product obtained when 3-methylbutan-2-ol is heated with concentrated H₂SO₄ is
A) 2-methylbut-1-ene
B) 2-methylbut-2-ene
C) 3-methylbut-1-ene
D) 2-methylbut-2-ene and 2-methylbut-1-ene in equal amounts
Answer: B [Explanation: Saytzeff's rule – more substituted alkene is preferred.]

Q: Which of the following alcohols will give a positive Lucas test within 5 minutes at room temperature?
A) CH₃CH₂OH
B) (CH₃)₂CHOH
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₃OH
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary alcohols react fastest with Lucas reagent (ZnCl₂ + HCl).]

Q: The product formed when phenol is treated with bromine water is
A) o-bromophenol
B) p-bromophenol
C) 2,4,6-tribromophenol
D) m-bromophenol
Answer: C [Explanation: Phenol is highly activated; polysubstitution occurs in aqueous medium.]

Q: Which of the following is the correct order of reactivity of alcohols towards dehydration?
A) 1° > 2° > 3°
B) 3° > 2° > 1°
C) 2° > 1° > 3°
D) 1° > 3° > 2°
Answer: B [Explanation: Stability of carbocation intermediate increases from 1° to 3°.]

Q: Williamson's synthesis involves
A) nucleophilic substitution
B) electrophilic substitution
C) free radical substitution
D) addition reaction
Answer: A [Explanation: SN2 attack of alkoxide on alkyl halide.]

Q: Which of the following ethers cannot be prepared by Williamson's synthesis?
A) CH₃OCH₂CH₃
B) (CH₃)₃COCH₃
C) C₆H₅OCH₃
D) (CH₃)₃COC(CH₃)₃
Answer: D [Explanation: Tertiary-tertiary ether fails due to elimination preference.]

Q: The reaction of phenol with chloroform in presence of NaOH is known as
A) Reimer-Tiemann reaction
B) Kolbe's reaction
C) Gattermann reaction
D) Friedel-Crafts reaction
Answer: A [Explanation: Introduction of –CHO group at ortho position.]

Q: Which of the following compounds will not form a yellow precipitate with I₂/NaOH?
A) CH₃CH₂OH
B) CH₃CH(OH)CH₃
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₃COCH₃
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary alcohols do not have CH₃CH(OH)– group required for iodoform test.]

Q: Assertion (A): Phenol is more reactive than benzene towards electrophilic substitution.
Reason (R): The –OH group is strongly activating and ortho-para directing.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Resonance donation by oxygen increases electron density on the ring.]

Q: The product obtained when ethanol is heated with concentrated H₂SO₄ at 443 K is
A) ethene
B) diethyl ether
C) ethanal
D) ethanoic acid
Answer: A [Explanation: At higher temperature (443 K) dehydration gives alkene; at 413 K ether is formed.]

Q: Which of the following is the most suitable method for the preparation of pure diethyl ether?
A) Heating excess ethanol with concentrated H₂SO₄ at 413 K
B) Heating ethanol with concentrated H₂SO₄ at 443 K
C) Reaction of sodium ethoxide with ethyl iodide
D) Both A and C
Answer: D [Explanation: Both continuous etherification and Williamson synthesis can give pure diethyl ether.]

Q: The correct order of boiling points is
A) CH₃OH < CH₃CH₂OH < CH₃CH₂CH₂OH
B) CH₃CH₂CH₂OH < CH₃CH₂OH < CH₃OH
C) CH₃CH₂OH < CH₃OH < CH₃CH₂CH₂OH
D) CH₃OH < CH₃CH₂CH₂OH < CH₃CH₂OH
Answer: A [Explanation: Boiling point increases with increase in molecular mass and H-bonding.]

Q: Which of the following phenols has the lowest pKa value?
A) Phenol
B) o-Nitrophenol
C) p-Nitrophenol
D) m-Nitrophenol
Answer: C [Explanation: p-Nitrophenoxide is most stabilised by resonance (–NO₂ at para).]

Q: The intermediate formed in the hydroboration-oxidation of alkenes is
A) carbocation
B) carbanion
C) free radical
D) organoborane
Answer: D [Explanation: BH₃ adds to alkene forming alkylborane intermediate.]

Q: Which of the following reagents converts a primary alcohol into an aldehyde without further oxidation?
A) KMnO₄
B) K₂Cr₂O₇/H⁺
C) PCC
D) CrO₃
Answer: C [Explanation: Pyridinium chlorochromate (PCC) is a mild oxidising agent.]

Q: The major product formed when phenol is treated with CO₂ under pressure in presence of sodium hydroxide followed by acidification is
A) salicylic acid
B) benzoic acid
C) salicylaldehyde
D) picric acid
Answer: A [Explanation: Kolbe-Schmitt reaction.]

Q: Which of the following alcohols will give the fastest reaction with Lucas reagent?
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂OH
B) CH₃CH(OH)CH₃
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₂=CH–CH₂OH
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary alcohol reacts almost instantaneously.]

Q: The product of the reaction of phenol with zinc dust is
A) benzene
B) toluene
C) biphenyl
D) cyclohexanol
Answer: A [Explanation: Zinc dust distillation reduces phenol to benzene.]

Q: Which of the following is not a method of preparation of alcohols?
A) Hydroboration-oxidation of alkenes
B) Reduction of aldehydes and ketones
C) Hydrolysis of alkyl halides
D) Friedel-Crafts alkylation
Answer: D [Explanation: Friedel-Crafts is for aromatic substitution, not alcohol preparation.]

Q: Assertion (A): Ethers are less soluble in water than alcohols of comparable molecular mass.
Reason (R): Ethers cannot form hydrogen bonds with water.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: C [Explanation: Ethers can accept H-bonds from water but cannot donate; solubility is lower than alcohols.]

Q: The correct order of reactivity of the following towards HBr is
A) CH₃OH > (CH₃)₂CHOH > (CH₃)₃COH
B) (CH₃)₃COH > (CH₃)₂CHOH > CH₃OH
C) (CH₃)₂CHOH > CH₃OH > (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₃OH > (CH₃)₃COH > (CH₃)₂CHOH
Answer: B [Explanation: Reactivity follows the order of carbocation stability (3° > 2° > 1°).]

Q: Which of the following compounds will give a white precipitate with bromine water?
A) Phenol
B) Anisole
C) both A and B
D) Ethanol
Answer: A [Explanation: Phenol gives 2,4,6-tribromophenol (white ppt); anisole does not under ordinary conditions.]

Q: The reagent used for the conversion of phenol to anisole is
A) CH₃I / NaOH
B) CH₃Cl / AlCl₃
C) (CH₃)₂SO₄ / NaOH
D) both A and C
Answer: D [Explanation: Both methyl iodide and dimethyl sulphate in alkaline medium give anisole.]

Q: Which of the following is the strongest acid?
A) Phenol
B) p-Cresol
C) p-Nitrophenol
D) m-Nitrophenol
Answer: C [Explanation: Strong –R and –I effect of para-nitro group.]

Q: The intermediate in the acid-catalysed dehydration of alcohols is
A) free radical
B) carbocation
C) carbanion
D) carbene
Answer: B [Explanation: Protonation followed by loss of water gives carbocation.]

Q: Which of the following ethers will undergo cleavage with HI most readily?
A) CH₃OCH₃
B) CH₃OC₂H₅
C) (CH₃)₃COCH₃
D) C₆H₅OCH₃
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary alkyl group forms stable carbocation, facilitating cleavage.]

Q: The product formed when anisole is treated with HI is
A) phenol + methyl iodide
B) methanol + iodobenzene
C) both A and B depending on conditions
D) no reaction
Answer: A [Explanation: Preferential cleavage of alkyl-oxygen bond under ordinary conditions.]

Q: Which of the following alcohols can be prepared by the action of Grignard reagent on methanal?
A) Primary
B) Secondary
C) Tertiary
D) All of these
Answer: A [Explanation: HCHO + RMgX → primary alcohol after hydrolysis.]

Q: The correct statement about the reaction of phenol with dilute HNO₃ is
A) only o-nitrophenol is formed
B) only p-nitrophenol is formed
C) both o- and p-nitrophenol are formed
D) 2,4,6-trinitrophenol is formed
Answer: C [Explanation: Dilute HNO₃ gives mixture of ortho and para isomers.]

Q: Which of the following is used as an antiseptic?
A) Phenol
B) Anisole
C) Diethyl ether
D) Ethanol only
Answer: A [Explanation: 0.2% solution of phenol is used as antiseptic.]

Q: The major product obtained on heating 2-methylpropan-2-ol with concentrated H₂SO₄ is
A) 2-methylpropene

Q: Which of the following reagents is used to distinguish phenol from alcohols?
A) Neutral FeCl₃

Answer: A [Explanation: Phenol gives violet colour with neutral FeCl₃; alcohols do not.]

Q: The reaction of sodium ethoxide with ethyl bromide is an example of
A) SN1
B) SN2
C) E1
D) E2
Answer: B [Explanation: Primary halide + strong nucleophile → SN2 (Williamson synthesis).]

Q: Which of the following compounds has the highest boiling point?
A) CH₃OCH₃
B) CH₃CH₂OH
C) CH₃CH₂CH₃

Q: Assertion (A): o-Nitrophenol is more volatile than p-nitrophenol.

A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A

Q: Which of the following alcohols is most reactive towards esterification?
A) CH₃OH
B) CH₃CH₂OH
C) (CH₃)₂CHOH

Answer: A [Explanation: Least steric hindrance in methanol.]

Q: The intermediate in Kolbe's reaction is
A) phenoxide ion
B) carbocation
C) free radical
D) benzyne
Answer: A [Explanation: Phenoxide ion attacks CO₂.]

Q: Which of the following statements is correct about ethers?
A) They are highly reactive towards nucleophiles
