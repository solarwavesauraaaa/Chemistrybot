Q: The electronic configuration of Cr is 3d⁵4s¹ instead of 3d⁴4s² because
A) 3d orbitals have lower energy than 4s
B) half-filled configuration is more stable
C) 4s electrons are more penetrating
D) exchange energy of 3d⁴ is higher
Answer: B [Explanation: Extra stability of half-filled d⁵ configuration.]

Q: Which of the following is not regarded as a transition element according to IUPAC?
A) Fe
B) Zn
C) Cu
D) Mn
Answer: B [Explanation: Zn has completely filled d¹⁰ configuration in ground state and common oxidation state.]

Q: The correct order of atomic radii of the first transition series is
A) Sc < Ti < V < Cr < Mn
B) Sc > Ti > V > Cr > Mn
C) Sc > Ti ≈ V ≈ Cr ≈ Mn
D) Sc < Ti ≈ V ≈ Cr < Mn
Answer: C [Explanation: Atomic radii decrease initially then become almost constant due to poor shielding of d-electrons.]

Q: The maximum oxidation state shown by Mn is
A) +5
B) +6
C) +7
D) +4
Answer: C [Explanation: Mn shows +7 in KMnO₄ (all 4s and 3d electrons removed).]

Q: Which of the following ions has the maximum magnetic moment?
A) Mn²⁺
B) Fe³⁺
C) Cr³⁺
D) V³⁺
Answer: A [Explanation: Mn²⁺ has 5 unpaired electrons (d⁵); magnetic moment = √35 BM.]

Q: The colour of KMnO₄ is due to
A) d–d transition
B) charge transfer spectrum
C) f–f transition
D) ligand field transition
Answer: B [Explanation: Intense purple colour arises from ligand-to-metal charge transfer.]

Q: In the acidic medium, KMnO₄ oxidises oxalic acid. The number of electrons involved per MnO₄⁻ is
A) 3
B) 5
C) 1
D) 7
Answer: B [Explanation: MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 4H₂O]

Q: The correct order of acidic strength of the oxides is
A) Mn₂O₇ > CrO₃ > V₂O₅
B) V₂O₅ > CrO₃ > Mn₂O₇
C) CrO₃ > Mn₂O₇ > V₂O₅
D) Mn₂O₇ > V₂O₅ > CrO₃
Answer: A [Explanation: Higher oxidation state → more acidic oxide.]

Q: Which of the following statements is correct about lanthanoids?
A) All lanthanoids show +2 oxidation state
B) Lanthanoid contraction is due to poor shielding of 4f electrons
C) They form coloured ions only in +3 state
D) Their basic character increases with atomic number
Answer: B [Explanation: Lanthanoid contraction arises from imperfect shielding of 4f electrons.]

Q: The electronic configuration of Eu²⁺ is
A) 4f⁷
B) 4f⁶
C) 4f⁵
D) 4f⁸
Answer: A [Explanation: Eu (4f⁷6s²) loses two 6s electrons → 4f⁷.]

Q: Which of the following is used as an oxidising agent in acidic medium?
A) K₂Cr₂O₇
B) KMnO₄
C) Both A and B
D) Neither
Answer: C [Explanation: Both are strong oxidising agents in acidic medium.]

Q: The number of unpaired electrons in [Cr(H₂O)₆]³⁺ is
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Answer: D [Explanation: Cr³⁺ is d³; all three electrons remain unpaired in t₂g set.]

Q: The correct order of ionic radii of the following is
A) La³⁺ > Eu³⁺ > Lu³⁺
B) Lu³⁺ > Eu³⁺ > La³⁺
C) Eu³⁺ > La³⁺ > Lu³⁺
D) La³⁺ > Lu³⁺ > Eu³⁺
Answer: A [Explanation: Due to lanthanoid contraction, ionic radii decrease from La³⁺ to Lu³⁺.]

Q: Assertion (A): Zn is not considered a transition element.
Reason (R): Zn has completely filled d-orbitals in ground state as well as in +2 oxidation state.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: IUPAC definition requires incomplete d-subshell.]

Q: The catalytic activity of transition metals is due to
A) variable oxidation states
B) large surface area
C) ability to form complexes
D) All of these
Answer: D [Explanation: All factors contribute to catalytic behaviour.]

Q: In the preparation of K₂Cr₂O₇ from chromite ore, the first step is
A) fusion with Na₂CO₃ in air
B) acidification
C) crystallisation
D) reduction
Answer: A [Explanation: 4FeCr₂O₄ + 8Na₂CO₃ + 7O₂ → 8Na₂CrO₄ + 2Fe₂O₃ + 8CO₂]

Q: The structure of dichromate ion is
A) two tetrahedra sharing one oxygen
B) two tetrahedra sharing two oxygens
C) octahedral
D) planar
Answer: A [Explanation: Cr₂O₇²⁻ consists of two CrO₄ tetrahedra sharing one oxygen atom.]

Q: Which of the following has the highest melting point?
A) Cr
B) Mn
C) Fe
D) Zn
Answer: A [Explanation: Cr has maximum number of unpaired electrons → strongest metallic bonding.]

Q: The +2 oxidation state is more stable than +3 for
A) Fe
B) Mn
C) both Fe and Mn in aqueous solution under certain conditions
D) Cr
Answer: C [Explanation: Fe²⁺ and Mn²⁺ are more stable in many cases due to higher hydration enthalpy or half-filled configuration.]

Q: The magnetic moment of Mn²⁺ (in BM) is
A) 5.92
B) 4.90
C) 3.87
D) 2.84
Answer: A [Explanation: √[n(n+2)] = √[5×7] = √35 ≈ 5.92]

Q: The correct order of decreasing basic character of lanthanoid hydroxides is
