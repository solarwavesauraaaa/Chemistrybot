# 18_Moving_Charges_and_Magnetism

Q: The magnetic field produced by a moving charge is:
A) Perpendicular to both velocity and position vector
B) Parallel to velocity
C) Parallel to position vector
D) Zero
Answer: A [B = (mu0/4pi) q (v x r) / r^3. B is perpendicular to both v and r.]

Q: The SI unit of magnetic field (B) is:
A) Tesla
B) Gauss
C) Weber
D) Henry
Answer: A [Tesla (T) is SI unit of magnetic field.]

Q: 1 Tesla is equal to:
A) 10^4 Gauss
B) 10^3 Gauss
C) 10^5 Gauss
D) 10^6 Gauss
Answer: A [1 T = 10^4 G.]

Q: The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop carrying current I is:
A) B = mu0 I / 2R
B) B = mu0 I / R
C) B = mu0 I / 4R
D) B = 2 mu0 I / R
Answer: A [B_center = mu0 I / 2R.]

Q: The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop of radius R carrying current I is:
A) mu0 I / 2R
B) mu0 I / 4R
C) mu0 I / R
D) 2 mu0 I / R
Answer: A [B = mu0 I / 2R.]

Q: The magnetic field on the axis of a circular loop at a distance x from the center is:
A) B = (mu0 I R^2) / 2(R^2 + x^2)^1.5
B) B = mu0 I / 2R
C) B = mu0 I / 4R
D) B = mu0 I / R
Answer: A [B_axis = mu0 I R^2 / 2(R^2 + x^2)^(3/2).]

Q: The magnetic field due to a straight current-carrying wire at a distance r is:
A) B = mu0 I / (2pi r)
B) B = mu0 I / (4pi r)
C) B = 2 mu0 I / r
D) B = mu0 I / r
Answer: A [B = mu0 I / 2pi r.]

Q: The direction of the magnetic field due to a current-carrying wire is given by:
A) Right-hand thumb rule
B) Left-hand rule
C) Fleming's rule
D) Cork screw rule
Answer: A [Right-hand thumb rule gives direction of B around current.]

Q: The force on a charged particle moving in a magnetic field is:
A) F = q (v x B)
B) F = q (B x v)
C) F = q v B
D) F = q v / B
Answer: A [F = q (v x B).]

Q: The magnetic force on a charged particle is maximum when:
A) v is perpendicular to B
B) v is parallel to B
C) v is antiparallel to B
D) v is zero
Answer: A [F = q v B sin(theta). Max when theta = 90°.]

Q: The magnetic force on a charged particle is zero when:
A) v is parallel to B
B) v is perpendicular to B
C) v is antiparallel to B
D) Both A and C
Answer: D [Force = q v B sin(theta). Zero when theta = 0° or 180° (parallel/antiparallel).]

Q: A charged particle moves in a circular path in a magnetic field. The radius of the path is:
A) r = mv / qB
B) r = qB / mv
C) r = mvB / q
D) r = q / mvB
Answer: A [r = mv / qB.]

Q: The time period of a charged particle in a magnetic field is:
A) T = 2pi m / qB
B) T = qB / 2pi m
C) T = 2pi qB / m
D) T = m / 2pi qB
Answer: A [T = 2pi m / qB.]

Q: The frequency of a charged particle moving in a magnetic field is:
A) f = qB / 2pi m
B) f = 2pi m / qB
C) f = qB / m
D) f = m / qB
Answer: A [Cyclotron frequency: f = qB / 2pi m.]

Q: A charged particle moves in a magnetic field. The work done by the magnetic field is:
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Infinite
Answer: A [Magnetic force is perpendicular to velocity, so W = 0.]

Q: The magnetic field inside a long solenoid is:
A) Uniform and proportional to nI
B) Zero
C) Non-uniform
D) Proportional to 1/n
Answer: A [B_inside = mu0 n I.]

Q: The magnetic field outside a long solenoid is:
A) Zero
B) Uniform
C) Proportional to I
D) Proportional to n
Answer: A [Outside a solenoid, B ≈ 0.]

Q: The magnetic field at the ends of a long solenoid is:
A) mu0 n I / 2
B) mu0 n I
C) 2 mu0 n I
D) mu0 n I / 4
Answer: A [At ends, B = mu0 n I / 2.]

Q: The force between two parallel current-carrying conductors is:
A) F = mu0 I1 I2 L / (2pi d)
B) F = mu0 I1 I2 / (2pi d)
C) F = mu0 I1 I2 L / (4pi d)
D) F = mu0 I1 I2 / (4pi d)
Answer: A [F/L = mu0 I1 I2 / (2pi d).]

Q: The force between two parallel currents is attractive if:
A) Currents are in the same direction
B) Currents are in opposite directions
C) Currents are equal
D) Currents are zero
Answer: A [Parallel currents attract.]

Q: The force between two parallel currents is repulsive if:
A) Currents are in opposite directions
B) Currents are in the same direction
C) Currents are equal
D) Currents are zero
Answer: A [Anti-parallel currents repel.]

Q: The torque on a current loop in a magnetic field is:
A) tau = m x B
B) tau = m . B
C) tau = m / B
D) tau = B / m
Answer: A [Torque = m x B.]

Q: The magnetic dipole moment of a current loop is:
A) m = N I A
B) m = I A / N
C) m = N I / A
D) m = N A / I
Answer: A [m = N I A.]

Q: The current loop behaves like a:
A) Magnetic dipole
B) Electric dipole
C) Point charge
D) Capacitor
Answer: A [A current loop has a magnetic dipole moment.]

Q: The potential energy of a magnetic dipole in a magnetic field is:
A) U = -m . B
B) U = m . B
C) U = m x B
D) U = m / B
Answer: A [U = -m . B = -mB cos(theta).]

Q: A galvanometer can be converted into an ammeter by connecting:
A) A low resistance in parallel
B) A high resistance in parallel
C) A low resistance in series
D) A high resistance in series
Answer: A [Ammeter: shunt (low resistance) in parallel.]

Q: A galvanometer can be converted into a voltmeter by connecting:
A) A high resistance in series
B) A low resistance in series
C) A high resistance in parallel
D) A low resistance in parallel
Answer: A [Voltmeter: high resistance in series.]

Q: The current sensitivity of a galvanometer is:
A) S = N B A / C
B) S = C / N B A
C) S = N B / C A
D) S = N A / B C
Answer: A [S = N B A / C.]

Q: The voltage sensitivity of a galvanometer is:
A) S_v = N B A / C R
B) S_v = N B A / C
C) S_v = C / N B A R
D) S_v = N B A R / C
Answer: A [S_v = S / R.]

Q: The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop is B. If the radius is doubled, the field becomes:
A) B/2
B) 2B
C) B/4
D) 4B
Answer: A [B ∝ 1/R. If R doubles, B halves.]

Q: The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop is B. If the current is doubled, the field becomes:
A) 2B
B) B/2
C) 4B
D) B/4
Answer: A [B ∝ I. If I doubles, B doubles.]

Q: The magnetic field due to a circular loop at its center is B. If the number of turns is doubled, the field becomes:
A) 2B
B) B/2
C) 4B
D) B/4
Answer: A [B ∝ N. If N doubles, B doubles.]

Q: A charge q moves with velocity v in a magnetic field B. The force is F. If v is doubled, the force becomes:
A) 2F
B) F/2
C) 4F
D) F/4
Answer: A [F = q v B sin(theta). If v doubles, F doubles.]

Q: A proton and an electron have the same speed in a magnetic field. The radius of the proton's path is:
A) Greater than that of electron
B) Less than that of electron
C) Same as electron
D) Zero
Answer: A [r = mv/qB. Proton has more mass, so larger radius.]

Q: A particle of charge q and mass m is accelerated through a potential V. The radius of its path in a magnetic field B is:
A) r = sqrt(2mV / q) / B
B) r = sqrt(qV / m) / B
C) r = sqrt(2m / qV) / B
D) r = sqrt(q / 2mV) / B
Answer: A [v = sqrt(2qV/m). r = mv/qB = m sqrt(2qV/m)/qB = sqrt(2mV/q)/B.]

Q: The magnetic field produced by a current element is given by:
A) Biot-Savart law
B) Ampere's law
C) Faraday's law
D) Lenz's law
Answer: A [Biot-Savart law gives B due to current element.]

Q: Ampere's circuital law states that:
A) ∮ B . dl = mu0 I_enclosed
B) ∮ B . dl = 0
C) ∮ E . dl = 0
D) ∮ E . dl = Q / epsilon0

Q: The magnetic field inside a toroid is:
A) B = mu0 N I / 2pi r

Q: The cyclotron is used to:
A) Accelerate charged particles
B) Deflect charged particles

Q: The condition for resonance in a cyclotron is:
A) f = f_c (cyclotron frequency)

Q: The moving coil galvanometer works on the principle of:
A) Torque on a current loop in a magnetic field

Answer: A [Galvanometer works on torque on current loop.]

Q: A galvanometer of resistance G gives full-scale deflection for current I_g. The shunt required to convert it to an ammeter of range I is:
A) S = G I_g / (I - I_g)
B) S = G I / I_g

Q: A galvanometer of resistance G gives full-scale deflection for voltage V_g. The series resistance required to convert it to a voltmeter of range V is:

Q: A current-carrying coil in a magnetic field experiences a torque. The torque is maximum when:

Q: The Hall effect is used to determine:
A) Type of charge carriers and their density
B) Resistance
C) Capacitance

Answer: A [Hall effect gives sign and density of charge carriers.]

Q: A current I flows in a circular loop of radius R. The magnetic field at its center is B. If the loop is flattened to a straight wire of length 2pi R, the field at a distance R from the wire is:
A) B / pi
B) B / 2pi
C) 2B / pi
D) B / 4pi
