# 23_Ray_Optics_and_Optical_Instruments

Q: The law of reflection states that:
A) Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection
B) Angle of incidence is greater than angle of reflection
C) Angle of incidence is less than angle of reflection
D) Incident ray and reflected ray are parallel
Answer: A [Law of reflection: i = r.]

Q: The angle of incidence is 30°. The angle between the incident ray and the reflected ray is:
A) 60°
B) 30°
C) 90°
D) 120°
Answer: A [Angle between incident and reflected = 2i = 60°.]

Q: The image formed by a plane mirror is:
A) Virtual, erect, and same size
B) Real, inverted, and same size
C) Virtual, inverted, and magnified
D) Real, erect, and diminished
Answer: A [Plane mirror forms a virtual, erect, same-size image.]

Q: The focal length of a plane mirror is:
A) Infinity
B) Zero
C) 1 m
D) 2 m
Answer: A [Plane mirror has R = ∞, so f = ∞.]

Q: The mirror formula is:
A) 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
B) 1/f = 1/v - 1/u
C) f = v + u
D) f = v - u
Answer: A [1/f = 1/v + 1/u.]

Q: The magnification of a mirror is given by:
A) m = -v/u
B) m = v/u
C) m = -u/v
D) m = u/v
Answer: A [m = -v/u.]

Q: A concave mirror forms a real image when the object is placed:
A) Beyond the focus
B) Between the pole and focus
C) At the focus
D) At infinity
Answer: A [Concave mirror forms a real image for object beyond focus.]

Q: A concave mirror forms a virtual image when the object is placed:
A) Between the pole and focus
B) Beyond the focus
C) At the focus
D) At infinity
Answer: A [Object between pole and focus gives virtual, erect, magnified image.]

Q: The refractive index of a medium is defined as:
A) n = c / v
B) n = v / c
C) n = c / v^2
D) n = v / c^2
Answer: A [n = c / v.]

Q: Snell's law of refraction is:
A) n1 sin(i) = n2 sin(r)
B) n1 cos(i) = n2 cos(r)
C) n1 sin(r) = n2 sin(i)
D) n1 sin(i) = n2 cos(r)
Answer: A [Snell's law: n1 sin i = n2 sin r.]

Q: When light travels from a rarer to a denser medium, it:
A) Bends towards the normal
B) Bends away from the normal
C) Does not bend
D) Reflects completely
Answer: A [From rarer to denser, light bends towards the normal.]

Q: When light travels from a denser to a rarer medium, it:
A) Bends away from the normal
B) Bends towards the normal
C) Does not bend
D) Reflects completely
Answer: A [From denser to rarer, light bends away from the normal.]

Q: The critical angle is defined as:
A) The angle of incidence for which angle of refraction is 90°
B) The angle of incidence for which angle of refraction is 0°
C) The angle of refraction for which angle of incidence is 90°
D) The angle of incidence for which total internal reflection occurs
Answer: A [Critical angle: sin(c) = n2/n1.]

Q: Total internal reflection occurs when:
A) Light travels from denser to rarer medium and i > c
B) Light travels from rarer to denser medium and i > c
C) Light travels from denser to rarer medium and i < c
D) Light travels from rarer to denser medium and i < c
Answer: A [TIR occurs when i > c in denser-to-rarer medium.]

Q: Optical fibres work on the principle of:
A) Total internal reflection
B) Refraction
C) Reflection
D) Diffraction
Answer: A [Optical fibres use TIR to transmit light.]

Q: The lens formula is:
A) 1/f = 1/v - 1/u
B) 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
C) f = v - u
D) f = v + u
Answer: A [Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u.]

Q: The magnification of a lens is:
A) m = v / u
B) m = -v / u
C) m = -u / v
D) m = u / v
Answer: A [m = v/u.]

Q: A convex lens forms a real image when the object is placed:
A) Beyond the focus
B) Between the focus and lens
C) At the focus
D) At infinity
Answer: A [Convex lens forms real image for object beyond focus.]

Q: A convex lens forms a virtual image when the object is placed:
A) Between the focus and the lens
B) Beyond the focus
C) At the focus
D) At infinity
Answer: A [Object between focus and lens gives virtual, erect, magnified image.]

Q: The power of a lens is given by:
A) P = 1 / f (in meters)
B) P = f (in meters)
C) P = f / 1
D) P = 1 / f^2
Answer: A [Power P = 1/f (f in meters).]

Q: The SI unit of power of a lens is:
A) Dioptre
B) Metre
C) Dioptre / metre
D) 1 / metre
Answer: A [Dioptre (D) is SI unit of lens power.]

Q: A convex lens has:
A) Positive power
B) Negative power
C) Zero power
D) Infinite power
Answer: A [Convex lens has positive focal length, so positive power.]

Q: A concave lens has:
A) Negative power
B) Positive power
C) Zero power
D) Infinite power
Answer: A [Concave lens has negative focal length, so negative power.]

Q: A lens of power +2 D has focal length:
A) 50 cm
B) 25 cm
C) 100 cm
D) 40 cm
Answer: A [f = 1/P = 1/2 = 0.5 m = 50 cm.]

Q: A lens of power -4 D has focal length:
A) -25 cm
B) -50 cm
C) -100 cm
D) -40 cm
Answer: A [f = 1/(-4) = -0.25 m = -25 cm.]

Q: The combination of lenses in contact has power:
A) P = P1 + P2
B) P = P1 - P2
C) P = P1 * P2
D) P = 1/P1 + 1/P2
Answer: A [Power adds for lenses in contact.]

Q: The equivalent focal length of two lenses in contact is:
A) 1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2
B) f = f1 + f2
C) f = f1 * f2 / (f1 + f2)
D) f = f1 / f2
Answer: A [1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2.]

Q: The magnification of a compound microscope is:
A) m = m_o × m_e
B) m = m_o + m_e
C) m = m_o / m_e
D) m = m_e / m_o
Answer: A [Total magnification = product of objective and eyepiece magnifications.]

Q: The magnifying power of a simple microscope is:
A) m = 1 + D / f
B) m = D / f
C) m = f / D
D) m = 1 + f / D
Answer: A [For final image at near point: m = 1 + D/f.]

Q: The magnifying power of a simple microscope when image is at infinity is:
A) m = D / f
B) m = 1 + D / f
C) m = f / D
D) m = 1 + f / D
Answer: A [At infinity: m = D/f.]

Q: The near point of a normal human eye is:
A) 25 cm
B) 10 cm
C) 50 cm
D) Infinity
Answer: A [Near point (least distance of distinct vision) = 25 cm.]

Q: The far point of a normal human eye is:
A) Infinity
B) 25 cm
C) 1 m
D) 50 cm
Answer: A [Far point of normal eye is infinity.]

Q: Myopia (nearsightedness) is corrected using:
A) Concave lens
B) Convex lens
C) Bifocal lens
D) Cylindrical lens
Answer: A [Myopia is corrected using a concave lens.]

Q: Hypermetropia (farsightedness) is corrected using:
A) Convex lens
B) Concave lens
C) Bifocal lens
D) Cylindrical lens
Answer: A [Hypermetropia is corrected using a convex lens.]

Q: Astigmatism is corrected using:
A) Cylindrical lens
B) Concave lens
C) Convex lens
D) Bifocal lens
Answer: A [Astigmatism is corrected using a cylindrical lens.]

Q: The resolving power of a microscope is:
A) Directly proportional to the numerical aperture
B) Inversely proportional to wavelength
C) Both A and B
D) Independent of wavelength
Answer: C [Resolving power ∝ NA / lambda.]

Q: The resolving power of a telescope is:
A) Directly proportional to the diameter of the objective
B) Inversely proportional to wavelength
C) Both A and B
D) Independent of diameter
Answer: C [RP ∝ D / lambda.]

Q: The prism formula is:
A) delta = i + e - A
B) delta = i - e + A
C) delta = i + e + A
D) delta = i - e - A
Answer: A [Angle of deviation = i + e - A.]

Q: The angle of minimum deviation is related to refractive index by:
A) n = sin((A + delta_m)/2) / sin(A/2)
B) n = sin(A/2) / sin((A + delta_m)/2)
C) n = sin(A/2) / sin(delta_m/2)
D) n = sin(delta_m/2) / sin(A/2)
Answer: A [Prism formula: n = sin((A+delta_m)/2) / sin(A/2).]

Q: A ray of light is incident normally on a glass slab. The angle of refraction is:
A) 0°
B) 90°
C) 45°
D) 30°
Answer: A [Normally incident means i = 0°, so r = 0°.]

Q: A glass slab of refractive index 1.5 has thickness 10 cm. The lateral shift for a ray incident at 60° is approximately:
A) 5.8 cm
B) 10 cm
C) 3 cm
D) 15 cm
Answer: A [Lateral shift = t sin(i-r) / cos(r). Approximate value ~ 5.8 cm.]

Q: A convex lens of focal length 20 cm forms a real image twice the size of the object. The object distance is:
A) 30 cm
B) 40 cm
C) 15 cm
D) 10 cm
Answer: A [m = -2 = -v/u -> v = 2u. 1/20 = 1/2u + 1/u = 3/2u -> u = 30 cm.]

Q: The angle of deviation produced by a prism depends on:
A) The angle of incidence and prism angle
B) Only the angle of incidence
C) Only the prism angle
D) Only the refractive index
Answer: A [delta depends on i, A, and n.]

Q: The human eye lens is:
A) Convex
B) Concave
C) Bifocal
D) Cylindrical
Answer: A [Eye lens is a convex lens.]

Q: The ciliary muscles of the eye help in:
A) Changing the focal length of the eye lens
B) Changing the size of the pupil
C) Protecting the eye
D) Producing vitreous humor
Answer: A [Ciliary muscles change the curvature of the lens for accommodation.]

Q: The diameter of the pupil is controlled by:
A) Iris
B) Retina
C) Cornea
D) Ciliary muscles
Answer: A [Iris controls the size of the pupil.]

Q: The image formed on the retina is:
A) Real and inverted
B) Virtual and erect
C) Real and erect
D) Virtual and inverted
Answer: A [The image on retina is real and inverted.]

Q: The least distance of distinct vision for a normal eye is:
A) 25 cm
B) 10 cm
C) 50 cm
D) 100 cm
Answer: A [Near point = 25 cm.]

Q: The angle of deviation produced by a prism is minimum when:
A) i = e
B) i = e/2
C) i = 2e
D) i = 0
Answer: A [At minimum deviation, i = e.]

Q: The refractive index of a prism is 1.5 and the angle of prism is 60°. The angle of minimum deviation is:
A) 37.2°
B) 30°
C) 45°
D) 60°
Answer: A [Using n = sin((A+delta_m)/2)/sin(A/2). 1.5 = sin(30 + delta_m/2)/sin(30). sin(30+delta/2) = 0.75 -> 30+delta/2 = 48.6° -> delta = 37.2°.]

Q: The magnifying power of a telescope is given by:
A) m = f_o / f_e
B) m = f_e / f_o
C) m = f_o + f_e
D) m = f_o * f_e
Answer: A [Magnification of telescope = f_o / f_e.]

Q: A simple microscope has a magnifying power of 10. The focal length is (D = 25 cm):
A) 2.5 cm
B) 5 cm
C) 10 cm
D) 2 cm
Answer: A [m = 1 + D/f = 10 -> 25/f = 9 -> f = 2.78 cm ≈ 2.5 cm.]
