Q: The correct order of bond angle in the hydrides of Group 15 is
A) NH₃ > PH₃ > AsH₃ > SbH₃
B) PH₃ > AsH₃ > SbH₃ > NH₃
C) NH₃ > AsH₃ > PH₃ > SbH₃
D) SbH₃ > AsH₃ > PH₃ > NH₃
Answer: A [Explanation: Bond angle decreases down the group due to decreasing electronegativity and increasing size, leading to poorer orbital overlap and more p-character in bonding orbitals.]

Q: Which of the following statements about the anomalous behaviour of nitrogen is incorrect?
A) Nitrogen does not form pentahalides
B) Nitrogen shows catenation tendency higher than phosphorus
C) N≡N bond energy is very high
D) Nitrogen forms only one oxoacid of the type HNO₃ among strong acids
Answer: B [Explanation: Nitrogen has very weak catenation tendency compared to phosphorus because of the high N-N single bond strength relative to multiple bonds and small size.]

Q: The hybridisation of the central atom in XeF₄ and the shape of the molecule are respectively
A) sp³d², square planar
B) sp³d, trigonal bipyramidal
C) sp³d², octahedral
D) sp³d³, pentagonal planar
Answer: A [Explanation: Xe has 8 valence electrons + 4 from F = 12 electrons → 6 pairs (4 bp + 2 lp). Hybridisation is sp³d²; lone pairs occupy axial positions, giving square planar geometry.]

Q: Among the following, the strongest oxidising agent in aqueous solution is

Q: The correct order of acidic strength of the oxoacids of chlorine is

Q: Assertion (A): PF₅ exists but NF₅ does not.
