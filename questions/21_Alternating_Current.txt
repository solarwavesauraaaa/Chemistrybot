Q: An alternating current varies with time as:
A) I = I0 sin(omega t)
B) I = I0 cos(omega t)
C) Both A and B
D) I = I0 tan(omega t)
Answer: C [AC can be represented by sine or cosine functions.]

Q: The peak value of an AC voltage is 200 V. Its RMS value is:
A) 141.4 V
B) 200 V
C) 100 V
D) 282.8 V
Answer: A [V_rms = V0 / sqrt(2) = 200 / 1.414 ≈ 141.4 V.]

Q: The RMS value of an AC current is:
A) I_rms = I0 / sqrt(2)
B) I_rms = I0 * sqrt(2)
C) I_rms = I0 / 2
D) I_rms = I0 * 2
Answer: A [I_rms = I0 / sqrt(2).]

Q: The average value of a full-wave rectified AC is:
A) 2 V0 / pi
B) V0 / pi
C) V0 / 2
D) 2 V0
Answer: A [Average value = 2 V0 / pi.]

Q: The average value of a half-wave rectified AC is:
A) V0 / pi
B) 2 V0 / pi
C) V0 / 2
D) V0
Answer: A [Half-wave average = V0 / pi.]

Q: The frequency of AC in India is:
A) 50 Hz
B) 60 Hz
C) 100 Hz
D) 220 Hz
Answer: A [Indian AC frequency is 50 Hz.]

Q: The voltage in India is:
A) 220 V
B) 110 V
C) 230 V

Answer: A [Indian domestic supply is 220 V RMS.]

Q: In a purely resistive AC circuit, the phase difference between voltage and current is:
A) 0°
B) 90°
C) 180°
D) 45°
Answer: A [In resistor, V and I are in phase.]

Q: In a purely inductive AC circuit, the phase difference between voltage and current is:
A) 90°
B) 0°
C) 180°
D) 45°
Answer: A [Voltage leads current by 90° in inductor.]

Q: In a purely capacitive AC circuit, the phase difference between voltage and current is:
A) 90°
B) 0°
C) 180°
D) 45°
Answer: A [Current leads voltage by 90° in capacitor.]

Q: The inductive reactance is:
A) X_L = omega L
B) X_L = 1 / omega L
C) X_L = omega / L
D) X_L = L / omega
Answer: A [X_L = omega L.]

Q: The capacitive reactance is:
A) X_C = 1 / (omega C)
B) X_C = omega C
C) X_C = C / omega
D) X_C = omega / C
Answer: A [X_C = 1 / (omega C).]

Q: The impedance of an AC circuit is:
A) Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2)
B) Z = R + X_L + X_C
C) Z = sqrt(R^2 + X_L^2 + X_C^2)
D) Z = R + X_L - X_C
Answer: A [Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2).]

Q: The power factor of an AC circuit is:
A) cos(phi) = R / Z
B) cos(phi) = Z / R
C) cos(phi) = X_L / Z
D) cos(phi) = X_C / Z
Answer: A [Power factor = R / Z.]

Q: The average power in an AC circuit is:
A) P = V_rms I_rms cos(phi)
B) P = V_rms I_rms
C) P = V_rms I_rms sin(phi)
D) P = V0 I0
Answer: A [P = V_rms I_rms cos(phi).]

Q: The power factor of a purely resistive circuit is:
A) 1
B) 0
C) 0.5
D) 0.707
Answer: A [cos(phi) = 1.]

Q: The power factor of a purely inductive circuit is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) 0.5
D) 0.707
Answer: A [cos(phi) = 0.]

Q: The power factor of a purely capacitive circuit is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) 0.5
D) 0.707
Answer: A [cos(phi) = 0.]

Q: The resonance in an AC circuit occurs when:
A) X_L = X_C
B) X_L > X_C
C) X_L < X_C
D) R = 0
Answer: A [At resonance, X_L = X_C.]

Q: The resonant frequency of an LCR circuit is:
A) f = 1 / (2pi sqrt(LC))
B) f = 2pi sqrt(LC)
C) f = sqrt(LC) / 2pi
D) f = 2pi / sqrt(LC)
Answer: A [f = 1 / (2pi sqrt(LC)).]

Q: At resonance, the impedance of an LCR circuit is:
A) Minimum and equal to R
B) Maximum
C) Zero
D) Infinite
Answer: A [Z = R at resonance (minimum).]

Q: At resonance, the current in an LCR circuit is:
A) Maximum
B) Minimum
C) Zero
D) Infinite
Answer: A [Current is maximum at resonance.]

Q: The quality factor (Q-factor) of a series LCR circuit is:
A) Q = (1/R) sqrt(L/C)
B) Q = R sqrt(C/L)
C) Q = (1/R) sqrt(C/L)

Q: The bandwidth of an LCR circuit is:

Q: In an AC circuit, the current is maximum when:
A) Frequency is resonant frequency
B) Frequency is very high
C) Frequency is very low

Answer: A [At resonance, current is maximum.]

Q: A series LCR circuit has R = 10 Ω, L = 0.1 H, C = 10 µF. The resonant frequency is:
A) 159 Hz
B) 100 Hz
C) 50 Hz
D) 200 Hz
Answer: A [f = 1 / (2pi sqrt(LC)) = 1 / (2pi sqrt(0.1 * 10e-6)) = 1 / (2pi * 0.001) ≈ 159 Hz.]

Q: An AC source of 220 V RMS is connected to a resistor of 100 Ω. The RMS current is:
A) 2.2 A
B) 1.1 A
C) 3.3 A
D) 4.4 A
Answer: A [I_rms = V_rms / R = 220 / 100 = 2.2 A.]

Q: The peak current in the above circuit is:
A) 3.11 A
B) 2.2 A
C) 1.1 A
D) 4.4 A
Answer: A [I0 = I_rms * sqrt(2) = 2.2 * 1.414 ≈ 3.11 A.]

Q: A coil has resistance 10 Ω and inductance 0.2 H. Its impedance at 50 Hz is:
A) 63.1 Ω
B) 50 Ω
C) 100 Ω
D) 31.6 Ω
Answer: A [X_L = 2pi * 50 * 0.2 = 62.8 Ω. Z = sqrt(100 + 3943) = 63.1 Ω.]

Q: The phase angle in the above circuit is:
A) 80.9°
B) 45°
C) 60°
D) 90°
Answer: A [phi = tan^-1(X_L/R) = tan^-1(6.28) ≈ 80.9°.]

Q: The power factor in the above circuit is:
A) 0.158
B) 0.5
C) 0.707
D) 0.866
Answer: A [cos(phi) = R/Z = 10/63.1 ≈ 0.158.]

A) 318 Ω
B) 3183 Ω

Q: A choke coil is used to:

Q: The wattless current in an AC circuit occurs when:
A) The circuit is purely reactive

Q: The DC current through an inductor in an AC circuit is:
