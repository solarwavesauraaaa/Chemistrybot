# 04_Laws_of_Motion

Q: Newton's first law of motion defines:
A) Inertia
B) Acceleration
C) Force
D) Momentum
Answer: A [First law introduces the concept of inertia; a body resists change in its state of motion.]

Q: Which law explains the action and reaction principle?
A) First law
B) Second law
C) Third law
D) Law of gravitation
Answer: C [Newton's third law states that for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.]

Q: A body of mass 2 kg is acted upon by a force of 10 N. The acceleration produced is:
A) 2 m/s^2
B) 5 m/s^2
C) 10 m/s^2
D) 20 m/s^2
Answer: B [a = F/m = 10/2 = 5 m/s^2.]

Q: In the second law of motion, the rate of change of momentum is directly proportional to:
A) Impulse
B) Time
C) Applied force
D) Velocity
Answer: C [Second law states that net force equals rate of change of momentum.]

Q: Which of the following is a conservative force?
A) Gravitational force
B) Friction
C) Air resistance
D) Viscous force
Answer: A [Gravitational force is conservative; work done is path-independent.]

Q: A block of mass 5 kg is placed on a horizontal surface. The coefficient of static friction is 0.4. The maximum static frictional force is (g=10):
A) 20 N
B) 10 N
C) 40 N
D) 15 N
Answer: A [f_max = mu_s * N = 0.4 * 5 * 10 = 20 N.]

Q: The normal reaction on a body of mass m placed on a horizontal surface is:
A) mg
B) m
C) mg sin(theta)
D) mg cos(theta)
Answer: A [On a horizontal surface, normal force equals weight = mg.]

Q: A ball is dropped from a height. It bounces back to 1/4 of its original height. The coefficient of restitution is:
A) 0.5
B) 0.25
C) 0.75
D) 1.0
Answer: A [e = sqrt(h'/h) = sqrt(1/4) = 0.5.]

Q: For a body moving with constant velocity, the net external force is:
A) Zero
B) Constant non-zero
C) Increasing
D) Decreasing
Answer: A [From Newton's first law, if velocity is constant, net force is zero.]

Q: A block of mass 2 kg is pushed with a force of 20 N. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.2. The net acceleration is (g=10):
A) 8 m/s^2
B) 6 m/s^2
C) 4 m/s^2
D) 10 m/s^2
Answer: A [Friction = 0.2 * 2 * 10 = 4 N. Net force = 20 - 4 = 16 N. a = 16/2 = 8 m/s^2.]

Q: The impulse of a force is equal to the change in:
A) Velocity
B) Momentum
C) Kinetic energy
D) Potential energy
Answer: B [Impulse = F * delta_t = delta_p (change in momentum).]

Q: A body of mass 1 kg is moving with velocity 10 m/s. A force of 5 N acts on it for 2 seconds. The final velocity is:
A) 20 m/s
B) 15 m/s
C) 10 m/s
D) 0 m/s
Answer: A [Impulse = 5*2 = 10 kg m/s. delta_p = 10. p_initial = 10. p_final = 20. v = 20 m/s.]

Q: A car of mass 1000 kg moving at 20 m/s is stopped in 4 seconds. The force applied is:
A) 5000 N
B) 4000 N
C) 2000 N
D) 8000 N
Answer: A [a = (0-20)/4 = -5 m/s^2. F = ma = 1000*5 = 5000 N.]

Q: In a lift accelerating upward with acceleration 'a', the apparent weight of a person of mass m is:
A) m(g-a)
B) m(g+a)
C) mg
D) ma
Answer: B [Apparent weight = m(g+a) when accelerating upward.]

Q: In a lift accelerating downward with acceleration 'a' (a < g), the apparent weight is:
A) m(g+a)
B) m(g-a)
C) mg
D) m(a-g)
Answer: B [Apparent weight = m(g-a) when accelerating downward.]

Q: A trolley of mass 20 kg is pulled with a force of 60 N. The acceleration is 2 m/s^2. The frictional force is:
A) 40 N
B) 20 N
C) 10 N
D) 30 N
Answer: B [Net force = 20*2 = 40 N. F_friction = 60 - 40 = 20 N.]

Q: In a uniform circular motion, the force responsible for circular motion is called:
A) Centripetal force
B) Centrifugal force
C) Gravitational force
D) Frictional force
Answer: A [Centripetal force acts toward the center, keeping the body in circular path.]

Q: A car of mass 500 kg moves on a circular track of radius 50 m with speed 10 m/s. The centripetal force is:
A) 1000 N
B) 500 N
C) 2000 N
D) 100 N
Answer: A [F_c = m v^2 / R = 500 * 100 / 50 = 1000 N.]

Q: Which of the following is a pseudo force?
A) Centripetal force
B) Centrifugal force
C) Gravitational force
D) Electrostatic force
Answer: B [Centrifugal force is a pseudo force, appearing only in a rotating reference frame.]

Q: A particle is moving with constant speed in a circle. The net force acting on it is:
A) Zero
B) Directed towards center
C) Directed away from center
D) Directed along tangent
Answer: B [The net force is centripetal, directed towards the center.]

Q: Two blocks of masses 2 kg and 3 kg are connected by a string and pulled with a force of 50 N. The acceleration of the system is:
A) 10 m/s^2
B) 15 m/s^2
C) 6 m/s^2
D) 5 m/s^2
Answer: A [a = F / (m1 + m2) = 50 / 5 = 10 m/s^2.]

Q: The coefficient of friction is the ratio of:
A) Normal force to frictional force
B) Frictional force to normal force
C) Weight to mass
D) Force to acceleration
Answer: B [Coefficient of friction = f / N.]

Q: A block of mass 2 kg is placed on a rough inclined plane of angle 30 degrees. The coefficient of friction is 0.5. The frictional force is (g=10):
A) 10 N
B) 8.66 N
C) 5 N
D) 20 N
Answer: B [N = mg cos(30) = 20 * 0.866 = 17.32 N. f = mu * N = 0.5 * 17.32 = 8.66 N.]

Q: A force of 20 N acts on a body for 0.5 seconds. The impulse is:
A) 20 Ns
B) 10 Ns
C) 5 Ns
D) 40 Ns
Answer: B [Impulse = F * t = 20 * 0.5 = 10 Ns.]

Q: A body of mass 10 kg is lifted up with acceleration 2 m/s^2. The tension in the string is (g=10):
A) 120 N
B) 100 N
C) 80 N
D) 20 N
Answer: A [T = m(g+a) = 10(10+2) = 120 N.]

Q: In a system of two connected masses, tension is maximum:
A) At the center of the string
B) At the ends
C) Uniform throughout
D) Zero at the center
Answer: B [Tension is uniform throughout an ideal massless string.]

Q: A block is moving on a rough surface. The work done by friction is always:
A) Positive
B) Negative
C) Zero
D) Infinite
Answer: B [Friction always opposes motion, so work done by friction is negative.]

Q: The force required to maintain a body in uniform motion is:
A) Equal to weight
B) Equal to frictional force
C) Zero
D) Equal to mass times acceleration
Answer: B [In uniform motion, net force is zero. Applied force equals opposing friction.]

Q: A particle of mass m is rotated in a vertical circle. The tension is maximum at:
A) The lowest point
B) The highest point
C) The horizontal point
D) Uniform throughout
Answer: A [At the lowest point, tension = mg + mv^2/R, which is maximum.]

Q: The ratio of inertia of two bodies of masses 3 kg and 5 kg is:
A) 3:5
B) 5:3
C) 1:1
D) 3:8
Answer: A [Inertia is directly proportional to mass. Ratio = 3:5.]

Q: A force F = 5i + 3j acts on a particle. The displacement is d = 2i + 4j. The work done is:
A) 22 J
B) 20 J
C) 18 J
D) 10 J
Answer: A [W = F.d = 5*2 + 3*4 = 10 + 12 = 22 J.]

Q: A 2 kg ball is thrown up with velocity 10 m/s. The force of gravity acting on it is:
A) 10 N
B) 20 N
C) 2 N
D) 5 N
Answer: B [F = mg = 2*10 = 20 N.]

Q: Newton's second law is valid for:
A) Inertial frames
B) Non-inertial frames
C) Both inertial and non-inertial frames
D) Only for circular motion
Answer: A [Newton's laws are valid only in inertial reference frames.]

Q: A car travels on a curved road of radius 100 m with speed 20 m/s. The coefficient of friction required to prevent skidding is:
A) 0.2
B) 0.4
C) 0.5
D) 0.6
Answer: B [mu = v^2 / (R * g) = 400 / (100*10) = 0.4.]

Q: A person standing in a lift feels weightless when:
A) Lift goes up with constant velocity
B) Lift goes down with acceleration equal to g
C) Lift is at rest
D) Lift moves with constant speed
Answer: B [When lift accelerates downward with a = g, apparent weight becomes zero.]

Q: A block of mass 1 kg is suspended by a string. The tension in the string when the block is at rest is:
A) 10 N
B) 5 N
C) 0 N
D) 20 N
Answer: A [T = mg = 10 N.]

Q: In the equation F = ma, the 'm' represents:
A) Inertial mass
B) Gravitational mass
C) Both are equal
D) Rest mass
Answer: A [In Newton's second law, 'm' is inertial mass, a measure of inertia.]

Q: A force of 12 N acts on a body of mass 3 kg for 5 seconds. The change in velocity is:
A) 20 m/s
B) 10 m/s
C) 15 m/s
D) 5 m/s
Answer: A [Impulse = 12*5 = 60 Ns. delta_p = 60. m = 3 -> delta_v = 60/3 = 20 m/s.]

Q: A particle is projected horizontally. The vertical component of its velocity:
A) Remains constant
B) Increases linearly
C) Decreases
D) Is zero always
Answer: B [Vertical velocity increases linearly with time due to gravity (vy = gt).]

Q: A block of mass 4 kg is pulled along a horizontal surface with a force of 20 N. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.1. The acceleration is (g=10):
A) 5 m/s^2
B) 4 m/s^2
C) 3 m/s^2
D) 2 m/s^2
Answer: B [F_fric = 0.1 * 4 * 10 = 4 N. Net force = 20 - 4 = 16 N. a = 16/4 = 4 m/s^2.]

Q: The law of conservation of linear momentum is derived from:
A) Newton's first law
B) Newton's second law
C) Newton's third law
D) Law of gravitation
Answer: C [Conservation of momentum is a consequence of Newton's third law.]

Q: Two bodies collide elastically. The total kinetic energy after collision:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains the same
D) Becomes zero
Answer: C [In elastic collision, kinetic energy is conserved.]

Q: In an inelastic collision, the kinetic energy:
A) Is conserved
B) Is not conserved
C) Increases
D) Changes to potential energy
Answer: B [In inelastic collision, some kinetic energy is lost as heat/sound.]

Q: A particle of mass m moves in a circular path of radius r with speed v. The centripetal force is F. If speed is doubled, the new force is:
A) 2F
B) 4F
C) F/2
D) F/4
Answer: B [F = mv^2/r. If v is doubled, F becomes 4 times.]

Q: The direction of friction on a block sliding down an inclined plane is:
A) Up the incline
B) Down the incline
C) Horizontal
D) Vertical
Answer: A [Friction opposes motion; if sliding down, friction acts up the incline.]
