Q: Which of the following is a tertiary alkyl halide?
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂Cl
B) (CH₃)₂CHCl
C) (CH₃)₃CCl
D) CH₃CHClCH₂CH₃
Answer: C [Explanation: Halogen attached to a tertiary carbon.]

Q: The correct IUPAC name of CH₂=CH–CH₂Cl is
A) 1-chloropropene
B) 3-chloropropene
C) 3-chloroprop-1-ene
D) allyl chloride
Answer: C [Explanation: Preferred IUPAC name is 3-chloroprop-1-ene.]

Q: Which of the following compounds will undergo SN1 reaction most readily?
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂Cl
B) (CH₃)₂CHCl
C) (CH₃)₃CCl
D) CH₃Cl
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary alkyl halide forms most stable carbocation.]

Q: The order of reactivity of alkyl halides towards SN2 reaction is
A) 3° > 2° > 1°
B) 1° > 2° > 3°
C) 2° > 1° > 3°
D) 3° > 1° > 2°
Answer: B [Explanation: Steric hindrance increases from 1° to 3°, decreasing SN2 rate.]

Q: Which of the following is least reactive towards nucleophilic substitution?
A) CH₃Cl
B) CH₂=CH–Cl
C) CH₃CH₂Cl
D) (CH₃)₃CCl
Answer: B [Explanation: Vinyl halide has C–Cl bond with partial double bond character due to resonance.]

Q: The major product obtained when 2-bromobutane is treated with alcoholic KOH is
A) but-1-ene
B) but-2-ene
C) butane
D) 2-butanol
Answer: B [Explanation: Saytzeff's rule – more substituted alkene is major product.]

Q: Which of the following reagents is used for the preparation of alkyl halide from alcohol by SN2 mechanism?
A) HCl + ZnCl₂
B) PCl₅
C) SOCl₂ in presence of pyridine
D) HBr
Answer: C [Explanation: SOCl₂/pyridine follows SN2 pathway with inversion.]

Q: The compound which will not give iodoform test is
A) CH₃CH₂Cl
B) CH₃CHClCH₃
C) CH₃CH₂CH₂Cl
D) (CH₃)₂CHCl
Answer: C [Explanation: Only compounds with CH₃CH(X)– or CH₃CO– group give iodoform test.]

Q: In the reaction of (CH₃)₃CBr with aqueous NaOH, the mechanism is
A) SN1
B) SN2
C) E1
D) E2
Answer: A [Explanation: Tertiary halide prefers SN1 in aqueous medium.]

Q: Which of the following is most reactive towards electrophilic aromatic substitution?
A) Chlorobenzene
B) Nitrobenzene
C) Benzene
D) Toluene
Answer: D [Explanation: –CH₃ is activating and ortho-para directing; –Cl is deactivating.]

Q: The correct order of boiling points of the following is
A) CH₃F < CH₃Cl < CH₃Br < CH₃I
B) CH₃I < CH₃Br < CH₃Cl < CH₃F
C) CH₃Cl < CH₃F < CH₃Br < CH₃I
D) CH₃Br < CH₃Cl < CH₃F < CH₃I
Answer: A [Explanation: Boiling point increases with increase in molecular mass and van der Waals forces.]

Q: Which of the following compounds undergoes nucleophilic aromatic substitution most readily?
A) Chlorobenzene
B) p-Nitrochlorobenzene
C) 2,4-Dinitrochlorobenzene
D) 2,4,6-Trinitrochlorobenzene
Answer: D [Explanation: More nitro groups at ortho/para positions increase reactivity towards addition-elimination.]

Q: The product formed when chlorobenzene is treated with sodium in dry ether is
A) biphenyl
B) toluene
C) benzene
D) phenol
Answer: A [Explanation: Fittig reaction gives biphenyl.]

Q: Which of the following is used as a refrigerant?
A) CCl₄
B) CHCl₃
C) Freon-12
D) CHI₃
Answer: C [Explanation: Dichlorodifluoromethane (Freon-12) is used as refrigerant.]

Q: Assertion (A): Allyl chloride is more reactive than n-propyl chloride towards nucleophilic substitution.
Reason (R): Allyl carbocation is stabilised by resonance.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Resonance stabilisation of allyl carbocation makes SN1 faster.]

Q: The major product of the reaction of 1-bromopropane with silver nitrite is
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂NO₂
B) CH₃CH₂CH₂ONO
C) both A and B
D) CH₃CH₂CH₂OH
Answer: C [Explanation: Ambident nitrite ion gives both nitro and nitrite products.]

Q: Which of the following alkyl halides will give the same product with aqueous KOH and alcoholic KOH?
A) CH₃Cl
B) CH₃CH₂Cl
C) (CH₃)₃CCl
D) CH₃CHClCH₃
Answer: A [Explanation: Methyl halide cannot undergo elimination; only substitution occurs.]

Q: The compound used in the detection of unsaturation is
A) Br₂ in CCl₄
B) Bayer's reagent
C) both A and B
D) Tollens' reagent
Answer: C [Explanation: Both decolourise on reaction with alkenes/alkynes.]

Q: In the preparation of chlorobenzene from aniline, the intermediate formed is
A) diazonium salt
B) phenol
C) nitrobenzene
D) benzene
Answer: A [Explanation: Sandmeyer reaction proceeds via diazonium salt.]

Q: Which of the following has the highest dipole moment?
A) CH₃F
B) CH₃Cl
C) CH₃Br
D) CH₃I
Answer: B [Explanation: Although F is most electronegative, C–Cl bond length and polarity combination gives highest dipole moment for CH₃Cl.]

Q: The reaction of tertiary butyl chloride with sodium ethoxide mainly gives
A) (CH₃)₃COC₂H₅
B) (CH₃)₂C=CH₂
C) (CH₃)₃COH
D) CH₃CH₂OCH₂CH₃
Answer: B [Explanation: Strong base + tertiary halide → elimination (E2) is preferred.]

Q: Which of the following is not a polyhalogen compound?
A) Chloroform
B) Carbon tetrachloride
C) Freon
D) Chlorobenzene
Answer: D [Explanation: Chlorobenzene has only one halogen.]

Q: The correct order of bond dissociation enthalpy of C–X bond is
A) C–F > C–Cl > C–Br > C–I
B) C–I > C–Br > C–Cl > C–F
C) C–Cl > C–F > C–Br > C–I
D) C–Br > C–Cl > C–F > C–I
Answer: A [Explanation: Bond strength decreases down the group.]

Q: Which of the following compounds will give a racemic mixture on reaction with aqueous KOH?
A) CH₃CH₂CH₂Cl
B) CH₃CHClCH₃
C) (CH₃)₃CCl
D) CH₃Cl
Answer: B [Explanation: Secondary alkyl halide can follow both SN1 and SN2; SN1 leads to racemisation.]

Q: The reagent used for the conversion of alcohol to alkyl chloride with inversion of configuration is

Q: Which of the following is most reactive towards SN1 reaction?

Q: The product of the reaction of CH₃CH₂Br with sodium acetylide is
