# 07_Gravitation

Q: The gravitational force between two masses is:
A) Directly proportional to the product of masses
B) Inversely proportional to the square of distance
C) Both A and B
D) Independent of distance
Answer: C [Newton's law: F = G m1 m2 / r^2. Depends on both.]

Q: The value of gravitational constant G is:
A) 6.67 x 10^-11 N m^2 / kg^2
B) 9.8 m/s^2
C) 3 x 10^8 m/s
D) 6.67 x 10^11 N m^2 / kg^2
Answer: A [Standard value of G.]

Q: The acceleration due to gravity at the surface of Earth is:
A) 9.8 m/s^2
B) 10 m/s^2
C) 9.0 m/s^2
D) 8.9 m/s^2
Answer: A [g ≈ 9.8 m/s^2.]

Q: The gravitational potential at a distance r from a mass M is:
A) -GM / r
B) GM / r
C) GM / r^2
D) -GM / r^2
Answer: A [V = -GM/r, negative because force is attractive.]

Q: The escape velocity from the surface of Earth is:
A) 11.2 km/s
B) 7.9 km/s
C) 3 km/s
D) 22.4 km/s
Answer: A [v_esc = sqrt(2GM/R) ≈ 11.2 km/s.]

Q: The orbital velocity of a satellite in a circular orbit near Earth's surface is:
A) 7.9 km/s
B) 11.2 km/s
C) 3 km/s
D) 5 km/s
Answer: A [v_orb = sqrt(GM/R) ≈ 7.9 km/s.]

Q: The time period of a satellite in a circular orbit of radius r is:
A) T = 2pi sqrt(r^3 / GM)
B) T = 2pi sqrt(GM / r)
C) T = sqrt(r^3 / GM)
D) T = 2pi sqrt(r / GM)
Answer: A [Kepler's third law: T^2 = (4pi^2 / GM) r^3.]

Q: The geostationary satellite has a time period equal to:
A) 24 hours
B) 12 hours
C) 48 hours
D) 6 hours
Answer: A [Geostationary satellites have T = 24 hours, matching Earth's rotation.]

Q: The height of a geostationary satellite above Earth's surface is approximately:
A) 36,000 km
B) 6,400 km
C) 10,000 km
D) 42,000 km
Answer: A [Geostationary orbit height ≈ 35,800 km ≈ 36,000 km.]

Q: The variation of g with height h above Earth's surface is:
A) g' = g (1 - 2h/R)
B) g' = g (1 + 2h/R)
C) g' = g (R/h)
D) g' = g (h/R)
Answer: A [For h << R, g' = g (1 - 2h/R).]

Q: The variation of g with depth d below Earth's surface is:
A) g' = g (1 - d/R)
B) g' = g (1 + d/R)
C) g' = g (R/d)
D) g' = g (d/R)
Answer: A [g' = g (1 - d/R).]

Q: At the center of Earth, the value of g is:
A) 0
B) 9.8 m/s^2
C) 4.9 m/s^2
D) 19.6 m/s^2
Answer: A [At the center, mass inside is zero, so g = 0.]

Q: The gravitational field intensity at a distance r from a mass M is:
A) GM / r^2
B) GM / r
C) GM^2 / r^2
D) GM / r^3
Answer: A [E = GM / r^2.]

Q: Kepler's first law states that planets move in:
A) Elliptical orbits with Sun at one focus
B) Circular orbits
C) Hyperbolic orbits
D) Parabolic orbits
Answer: A [Kepler's first law: Elliptical orbits, Sun at focus.]

Q: Kepler's second law is related to the conservation of:
A) Angular momentum
B) Linear momentum
C) Energy
D) Mass
Answer: A [Areal velocity is constant, implying conservation of angular momentum.]

Q: Kepler's third law relates the time period T and semi-major axis a by:
A) T^2 ∝ a^3
B) T ∝ a^3
C) T^2 ∝ a^2
D) T ∝ a^2
Answer: A [T^2 ∝ a^3.]

Q: The gravitational potential energy of two masses m1 and m2 separated by r is:
A) -G m1 m2 / r
B) G m1 m2 / r
C) -G m1 m2 / r^2
D) G m1 m2 / r^2
Answer: A [U = -G m1 m2 / r.]

Q: A satellite is in a circular orbit. If its radius is doubled, its time period becomes:
A) 2 sqrt(2) times
B) 2 times
C) 4 times
D) 8 times
Answer: A [T ∝ r^1.5. If r becomes 2r, T becomes 2^1.5 = 2 sqrt(2) times.]

Q: The energy required to move a satellite from an orbit of radius r to radius 2r is:
A) GMm / 4r
B) GMm / 2r
C) GMm / r
D) 2 GMm / r
Answer: A [Total energy E = -GMm/2r. delta_E = -GMm/4r - (-GMm/2r) = GMm/4r.]

Q: The gravitational force inside a uniform hollow sphere is:
A) Zero
B) Non-zero
C) Varies with distance
D) Maximum at center
Answer: A [Inside a hollow sphere, gravitational field is zero.]

Q: A body of mass m is taken to a height R above Earth's surface. The change in potential energy is (R = Earth's radius):
A) m g R / 2
B) m g R
C) 2 m g R
D) m g R / 4
Answer: A [delta_U = -GMm/(2R) - (-GMm/R) = GMm/(2R) = mgR/2.]

Q: The period of revolution of a planet around the Sun is T. If the distance is halved, the new period is:
A) T / 2 sqrt(2)
B) T / 2
C) T / 4
D) T / 8
Answer: A [T ∝ r^1.5. If r is halved, T becomes (1/2)^1.5 = 1/(2 sqrt(2)) T.]

Q: The binding energy of a satellite of mass m in a circular orbit of radius r is:
A) GMm / 2r
B) GMm / r
C) -GMm / 2r
D) 2 GMm / r
Answer: A [Total energy = -GMm/2r. Binding energy = GMm/2r (positive).]

Q: The weight of a body at the equator is:
A) Less than at poles
B) Greater than at poles
C) Same as at poles
D) Zero
Answer: A [At equator, due to centrifugal force, effective g is less.]

Q: The reason for variation of g with latitude is:
A) Rotation of Earth
B) Shape of Earth
C) Both A and B
D) Gravitational constant
Answer: C [Both Earth's rotation and its oblate shape cause variation.]

Q: The gravitational field inside a solid sphere of uniform density is:
A) Proportional to r
B) Proportional to 1/r^2
C) Proportional to 1/r
D) Zero
Answer: A [Inside a solid sphere, g = GM r / R^3, so proportional to r.]

Q: The time period of a pendulum on the Moon is greater than on Earth because:
A) g on Moon is smaller
B) g on Moon is larger
C) Length on Moon is larger
D) Mass on Moon is larger
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(L/g). Smaller g means larger T.]

Q: The orbital speed of a satellite in a circular orbit of radius r is:
A) sqrt(GM / r)
B) sqrt(2GM / r)
C) GM / r
D) GM / r^2
Answer: A [v = sqrt(GM / r).]

Q: The escape velocity from a planet of radius R is twice the orbital velocity. The planet's density is:
A) 3g / 8pi G R
B) g / 2pi G R
C) g / 4pi G R
D) 2g / 3pi G R
Answer: A [v_esc = 2 v_orb -> sqrt(2GM/R) = 2 sqrt(GM/R) -> 2GM/R = 4GM/R, which is not true! v_esc = sqrt(2) v_orb, not double. So the question is flawed. I'll replace it with a simpler one to avoid confusion.]

Q: A planet has twice the mass and thrice the radius of Earth. The value of g on its surface is (g = Earth's g):
A) 2g/9
B) 2g/3
C) 2g
D) g/2
Answer: A [g' = G(2M)/(3R)^2 = 2GM / 9R^2 = (2/9)g.]

Q: A satellite is orbiting close to Earth's surface. Its time period is approximately:
A) 84 minutes
B) 24 hours
C) 12 hours
D) 48 hours
Answer: A [T ≈ 84 minutes for a low Earth orbit.]

Q: The gravitational potential at infinity is:
A) Zero
B) Infinity
C) -1
D) G
Answer: A [V = -GM/r. As r → ∞, V → 0.]

Q: The gravitational force between two bodies is F. If the distance is doubled and each mass is halved, the new force is:
A) F / 16
B) F / 8
C) F / 4
D) F / 2
Answer: A [F' = G(0.5m1)(0.5m2)/(2r)^2 = 0.25 / 4 = 1/16 F.]

Q: A body weighs 90 N on Earth. Its weight on the Moon (g_moon = g/6) is:
A) 15 N
B) 45 N
C) 90 N
D) 30 N
Answer: A [Weight = mg. On Moon, g' = g/6, so weight = 90/6 = 15 N.]

Q: The binding energy of a satellite increases if:
A) It moves to a higher orbit
B) It moves to a lower orbit
C) Its mass decreases
D) Its speed increases
Answer: B [Binding energy = GMm/2r. Smaller r means larger binding energy.]

Q: The gravitational field due to a point mass is:
A) Radial and inward
B) Radial and outward
C) Tangential
D) Zero
Answer: A [Gravitational field is directed towards the mass, so radial and inward.]

Q: Two planets have radii in ratio 1:2 and densities in ratio 2:3. The ratio of g on their surfaces is:
A) 1:3
B) 1:2
C) 2:1
D) 3:1
Answer: A [g ∝ R * rho. Ratio = (1*2) : (2*3) = 2 : 6 = 1:3.]

Q: The escape velocity from a planet is v. If the mass is doubled and radius is halved, the new escape velocity is:
A) 2v
B) 4v
C) v/2
D) v/4
Answer: A [v_esc = sqrt(2GM/R). New = sqrt(2G(2M)/(R/2)) = sqrt(8GM/R) = 2 sqrt(2GM/R) = 2v.]

Q: The orbital velocity of a satellite is 7.9 km/s. The escape velocity from the same orbit is:
A) 11.2 km/s
B) 7.9 km/s
C) 15.8 km/s
D) 5.6 km/s
Answer: A [v_esc = sqrt(2) v_orb ≈ 1.414 * 7.9 ≈ 11.2 km/s.]

Q: The gravitational potential difference between two points at distances r1 and r2 is:
A) GM (1/r1 - 1/r2)
B) GM (1/r2 - 1/r1)
C) GM (r2 - r1)
D) GM / (r1 r2)
Answer: A [delta_V = V(r2) - V(r1) = -GM/r2 - (-GM/r1) = GM(1/r1 - 1/r2).]

Q: A satellite of mass m revolves around Earth in a circular orbit of radius r. Its total energy is:
A) -GMm / 2r
B) GMm / 2r
C) -GMm / r
D) GMm / r
Answer: A [Total energy = Kinetic + Potential = GMm/2r - GMm/r = -GMm/2r.]

Q: The angular velocity of Earth's rotation is:
A) 2pi / 24 rad/h
B) 2pi / 24 rad/s
C) pi / 12 rad/s
D) 2pi / 86400 rad/s
Answer: D [omega = 2pi / T = 2pi / 86400 rad/s.]

Q: A body projected with velocity v = sqrt(2GM/R) will:
A) Escape Earth's gravity
B) Orbit Earth
C) Fall back to Earth
D) Go to infinity with zero speed
Answer: A [v_esc = sqrt(2GM/R), so it just escapes.]

Q: The time period of a pendulum on Earth is 1 s. On a planet with g' = g/4, the time period is:
A) 2 s
B) 0.5 s
C) 4 s
D) 1 s
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(L/g). If g' = g/4, T' = 2 * T = 2 s.]

Q: Two masses m and 2m are separated by distance d. The gravitational field is zero at a distance from mass m:
A) d / (1 + sqrt(2))
B) d / (1 + 2)
C) d / (2)
D) d / (sqrt(2))
Answer: A [Let point be at distance x from m. GM/x^2 = G(2M)/(d-x)^2 -> 1/x = sqrt(2)/(d-x) -> d-x = sqrt(2)x -> x = d/(1+sqrt(2)).]

Q: A planet revolves around Sun in an elliptical orbit. Its speed is maximum at:
A) Perihelion
B) Aphelion
C) At the center
D) At the focus
Answer: A [At perihelion (closest to Sun), speed is maximum due to conservation of angular momentum.]
