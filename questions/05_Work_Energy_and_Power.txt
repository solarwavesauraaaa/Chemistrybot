# 05_Work_Energy_and_Power

Q: Work done by a force is zero if the angle between force and displacement is:
A) 0 degrees
B) 90 degrees
C) 45 degrees
D) 60 degrees
Answer: B [W = F s cos(theta). cos(90) = 0, so work done is zero.]

Q: The SI unit of work is:
A) Newton
B) Joule
C) Watt
D) Pascal
Answer: B [Joule is the SI unit of work and energy.]

Q: A force of 10 N acts on a body and displaces it by 5 m along the direction of force. The work done is:
A) 50 J
B) 10 J
C) 25 J
D) 100 J
Answer: A [W = F * s = 10 * 5 = 50 J.]

Q: Which of the following is a scalar quantity?
A) Force
B) Work
C) Momentum
D) Velocity
Answer: B [Work is a scalar quantity; it has magnitude only.]

Q: If the angle between force and displacement is 60 degrees, and F = 20 N, s = 5 m, the work done is:
A) 50 J
B) 86.6 J
C) 100 J
D) 0 J
Answer: A [W = 20 * 5 * cos(60) = 100 * 0.5 = 50 J.]

Q: The work done by gravity when a body of mass m falls through height h is:
A) mgh
B) -mgh
C) 0
D) mg/h
Answer: A [Gravity and displacement are in same direction, so work done is +mgh.]

Q: The kinetic energy of a body of mass m moving with velocity v is:
A) mv
B) 0.5 mv^2
C) mv^2
D) m v^2 / g
Answer: B [K = 0.5 m v^2.]

Q: If the velocity of a body is doubled, its kinetic energy becomes:
A) 2 times
B) 4 times
C) 8 times
D) 16 times
Answer: B [K = 0.5 m v^2. If v is doubled, K becomes 4 times.]

Q: The work-energy theorem states that work done by net force equals change in:
A) Potential energy
B) Kinetic energy
C) Total energy
D) Momentum
Answer: B [Work-Energy Theorem: W_net = delta_K.]

Q: A body of mass 2 kg is moving with velocity 3 m/s. Its kinetic energy is:
A) 9 J
B) 6 J
C) 18 J
D) 3 J
Answer: A [K = 0.5 * 2 * 9 = 9 J.]

Q: The potential energy of a body of mass m at height h above ground is:
A) mgh
B) 0.5 mgh
C) mg/h
D) mh/g
Answer: A [U = mgh.]

Q: A spring of spring constant k is compressed by x. The potential energy stored is:
A) 0.5 k x^2
B) k x
C) k x^2
D) 0.5 k x
Answer: A [U = 0.5 k x^2.]

Q: The law of conservation of energy states that energy can be:
A) Created
B) Destroyed
C) Converted from one form to another
D) Neither created nor destroyed
Answer: C [Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted.]

Q: A stone of mass 1 kg is thrown up with velocity 20 m/s. The kinetic energy at the topmost point is:
A) 200 J
B) 0 J
C) 100 J
D) 50 J
Answer: B [At highest point, velocity is zero, so kinetic energy = 0.]

Q: A ball of mass 0.5 kg is dropped from height 20 m. The velocity just before hitting ground is (g=10):
A) 10 m/s
B) 20 m/s
C) 15 m/s
D) 25 m/s
Answer: B [v = sqrt(2gh) = sqrt(2*10*20) = sqrt(400) = 20 m/s.]

Q: Power is defined as:
A) Work done per unit time
B) Force per unit area
C) Energy per unit volume
D) Momentum per unit time
Answer: A [Power = Work / Time.]

Q: The SI unit of power is:
A) Joule
B) Newton
C) Watt
D) Pascal
Answer: C [Watt (W) is the SI unit of power.]

Q: A machine does 500 J of work in 10 seconds. The power is:
A) 50 W
B) 500 W
C) 10 W
D) 5 W
Answer: A [P = W / t = 500 / 10 = 50 W.]

Q: 1 horsepower (HP) is equal to:
A) 746 W
B) 100 W
C) 1000 W
D) 7460 W
Answer: A [1 HP = 746 W.]

Q: A body of mass 2 kg is lifted to a height of 5 m. The increase in potential energy is (g=10):
A) 100 J
B) 50 J
C) 20 J
D) 10 J
Answer: A [delta_U = mgh = 2*10*5 = 100 J.]

Q: A car of mass 1000 kg accelerates from rest to 20 m/s in 10 seconds. The average power is:
A) 20 kW
B) 10 kW
C) 40 kW
D) 100 kW
Answer: A [K = 0.5 * 1000 * 400 = 200,000 J. P = W/t = 200000/10 = 20000 W = 20 kW.]

Q: Which of the following is a non-conservative force?
A) Gravitational force
B) Electrostatic force
C) Frictional force
D) Elastic force
Answer: C [Friction is a non-conservative force; work depends on path.]

Q: A body is moved from A to B along two different paths. The work done by a conservative force is:
A) Different
B) Same
C) Zero
D) Infinite
Answer: B [For conservative forces, work done is path-independent.]

Q: A particle of mass m moves with velocity v. Its momentum is:
A) mv
B) 0.5 mv^2
C) m/v
D) mv^2
Answer: A [Momentum p = mv.]

Q: A force F = 2i + 3j acts on a particle displaced by s = 4i + 5j. The work done is:
A) 23 J
B) 8 J
C) 15 J
D) 30 J
Answer: A [W = F.s = 2*4 + 3*5 = 8 + 15 = 23 J.]

Q: A spring is stretched by 0.1 m with a force of 5 N. The spring constant is:
A) 50 N/m
B) 5 N/m
C) 0.5 N/m
D) 500 N/m
Answer: A [k = F / x = 5 / 0.1 = 50 N/m.]

Q: A 2 kg object is moving with speed 4 m/s. Its kinetic energy is:
A) 32 J
B) 16 J
C) 8 J
D) 4 J
Answer: B [K = 0.5 * 2 * 16 = 16 J.]

Q: If the kinetic energy of a body is doubled, its momentum becomes:
A) sqrt(2) times
B) 2 times
C) 4 times
D) 8 times
Answer: A [p = sqrt(2mK). If K is doubled, p becomes sqrt(2) times.]

Q: A ball of mass 0.2 kg is thrown up with velocity 10 m/s. The maximum height reached is (g=10):
A) 5 m
B) 2.5 m
C) 10 m
D) 1 m
Answer: A [h = u^2 / 2g = 100 / 20 = 5 m.]

Q: A 5 kg block slides down a frictionless incline of height 10 m. The velocity at the bottom is (g=10):
A) 10 m/s
B) 14.14 m/s
C) 20 m/s
D) 5 m/s
Answer: B [v = sqrt(2gh) = sqrt(200) = 14.14 m/s.]

Q: Which of the following is not a form of energy?
A) Kinetic energy
B) Potential energy
C) Momentum
D) Thermal energy
Answer: C [Momentum is not a form of energy; it is a vector quantity.]

Q: The power of a pump which lifts 100 kg of water to a height of 10 m in 20 seconds is (g=10):
A) 500 W
B) 1000 W
C) 2000 W
D) 100 W
Answer: A [Work = mgh = 100*10*10 = 10000 J. P = W/t = 10000/20 = 500 W.]

Q: A body of mass 1 kg is dropped from height 5 m. The kinetic energy just before hitting ground is (g=10):
A) 50 J
B) 25 J
C) 100 J
D) 10 J
Answer: A [K = mgh = 1*10*5 = 50 J.]

Q: Which of the following is a conservative force?
A) Spring force
B) Air resistance
C) Friction
D) Viscous drag
Answer: A [Spring force is conservative because it depends only on displacement.]

Q: A particle moves under a force F = -kx. The potential energy function is:
A) 0.5 k x^2
B) k x
C) k x^2
D) -0.5 k x^2
Answer: A [U = 0.5 k x^2 for a spring.]

Q: A bullet of mass 0.01 kg moving with 400 m/s penetrates a wooden block and stops. The work done by the block is:
A) 800 J
B) 400 J
C) 200 J
D) 100 J
Answer: A [Work done = change in K = 0.5 * 0.01 * 400^2 = 0.005 * 160000 = 800 J.]

Q: A 2 kg block is moving with velocity 6 m/s. The force required to stop it in 0.5 seconds is:
A) 24 N
B) 12 N
C) 6 N
D) 48 N
Answer: A [Impulse = change in momentum = 2*6 = 12 Ns. F = 12 / 0.5 = 24 N.]

Q: The efficiency of a machine is the ratio of:
A) Output power to input power
B) Input power to output power
C) Work to time
D) Force to distance
Answer: A [Efficiency = (Output power / Input power) * 100%.]

Q: A body is moved by a constant force. The work done is maximum when the angle is:
A) 0 degrees
B) 90 degrees
C) 180 degrees
D) 60 degrees
Answer: A [Work is maximum when force and displacement are parallel (theta = 0).]

Q: A pendulum of mass 2 kg is raised to a height 5 m. The potential energy at that point is (g=10):
A) 100 J
B) 50 J
C) 20 J
D) 10 J
Answer: A [U = mgh = 2*10*5 = 100 J.]

Q: The mechanical energy of a system is conserved if:
A) Only conservative forces act
B) Non-conservative forces act
C) Net force is zero
D) Acceleration is zero
Answer: A [Mechanical energy is conserved in the presence of only conservative forces.]

Q: The average power of an engine that does 2000 J of work in 5 seconds is:
A) 400 W
B) 500 W
C) 1000 W
D) 200 W
Answer: A [P = 2000 / 5 = 400 W.]

Q: A particle's kinetic energy is 100 J. Its momentum is 20 kg m/s. The mass of the particle is:
A) 2 kg
B) 4 kg
C) 5 kg
D) 10 kg
Answer: A [K = p^2 / 2m. 100 = 400 / 2m -> 200m = 400 -> m = 2 kg.]

Q: A spring of force constant 100 N/m is stretched by 0.2 m. The work done is:
A) 2 J
B) 4 J
C) 1 J
D) 10 J
Answer: A [W = 0.5 k x^2 = 0.5 * 100 * 0.04 = 2 J.]

Q: Which of the following is a unit of energy?
A) Joule
B) Watt
C) Pascal
D) Newton
Answer: A [Joule is the SI unit of energy. Watt is power.]
