# 15_Electric_Charges_and_Fields

Q: The SI unit of electric charge is:
A) Coulomb
B) Ampere
C) Volt
D) Ohm
Answer: A [Coulomb (C) is the SI unit of electric charge.]

Q: The charge on an electron is:
A) -1.6 x 10^-19 C
B) +1.6 x 10^-19 C
C) -1.6 x 10^19 C
D) +1.6 x 10^19 C
Answer: A [Electron charge e = -1.6 x 10^-19 C.]

Q: The electrostatic force between two charges is:
A) Directly proportional to the product of charges
B) Inversely proportional to the square of distance
C) Both A and B
D) Independent of distance
Answer: C [Coulomb's law: F = k q1 q2 / r^2.]

Q: The value of Coulomb's constant (k) in SI units is:
A) 9 x 10^9 N m^2/C^2
B) 8.987 x 10^9 N m^2/C^2
C) 9 x 10^9 C^2/N m^2
D) 8.987 x 10^9 C^2/N m^2
Answer: A [k = 1/(4pi epsilon0) ≈ 9 x 10^9 N m^2/C^2.]

Q: The permittivity of free space (epsilon0) is:
A) 8.854 x 10^-12 C^2/N m^2
B) 9 x 10^9 C^2/N m^2
C) 8.854 x 10^12 C^2/N m^2
D) 1.6 x 10^-19 C^2/N m^2
Answer: A [epsilon0 ≈ 8.854 x 10^-12 C^2/N m^2 (F/m).]

Q: The electric field at a distance r from a point charge q is:
A) E = k q / r^2
B) E = k q / r
C) E = k q r
D) E = k q / r^3
Answer: A [E = k q / r^2 (Coulomb's law for electric field).]

Q: The direction of the electric field due to a positive charge is:
A) Away from the charge
B) Towards the charge
C) Perpendicular to the charge
D) Zero
Answer: A [Electric field lines point away from positive charges.]

Q: The direction of the electric field due to a negative charge is:
A) Towards the charge
B) Away from the charge
C) Perpendicular to the charge
D) Zero
Answer: A [Electric field lines point towards negative charges.]

Q: The number of electric field lines passing through a surface is called:
A) Electric flux
B) Electric field
C) Electric potential
D) Electric dipole moment
Answer: A [Electric flux is the number of field lines passing through a surface.]

Q: The SI unit of electric flux is:
A) N m^2/C
B) N/C
C) V/m
D) C/N m^2
Answer: A [Electric flux = E * A * cos(theta). Unit = N m^2/C.]

Q: Gauss's law relates the electric flux through a closed surface to:
A) The enclosed charge
B) The surface area
C) The electric field
D) The distance
Answer: A [Gauss's law: flux = q_enclosed / epsilon0.]

Q: The electric field inside a uniformly charged spherical shell is:
A) Zero
B) Constant
C) Proportional to r
D) Proportional to 1/r^2
Answer: A [Inside a charged shell, E = 0.]

Q: The electric field outside a uniformly charged spherical shell is:
A) Proportional to 1/r^2
B) Proportional to r
C) Zero
D) Constant
Answer: A [Outside the shell, E = k Q / r^2.]

Q: The electric field at the surface of a charged conductor is:
A) Perpendicular to the surface
B) Parallel to the surface
C) Zero
D) At 45 degrees to the surface
Answer: A [Electric field is always perpendicular to the surface of a conductor.]

Q: The electric field inside a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium is:
A) Zero
B) Constant
C) Maximum
D) Variable
Answer: A [Inside a conductor, field is zero.]

Q: The electric potential at a distance r from a point charge q is:
A) V = k q / r
B) V = k q / r^2
C) V = k q r
D) V = k q / r^3
Answer: A [V = k q / r.]

Q: The work done in moving a charge q through a potential difference V is:
A) W = q V
B) W = q / V
C) W = V / q
D) W = q V^2
Answer: A [W = q V.]

Q: The equipotential surfaces for a point charge are:
A) Spheres centered at the charge
B) Planes parallel to the charge
C) Cylinders
D) Ellipsoids
Answer: A [Equipotential surfaces are spheres for a point charge.]

Q: The dipole moment vector points from:
A) Negative charge to positive charge
B) Positive charge to negative charge
C) It is zero
D) It is perpendicular to the line joining charges
Answer: A [Dipole moment vector points from -q to +q.]

Q: The electric field at the axial point of an electric dipole is:
A) Proportional to 1/r^3
B) Proportional to 1/r^2
C) Proportional to 1/r
D) Proportional to r
Answer: A [E_axial = 2 k p / r^3.]

Q: The electric field at the equatorial point of an electric dipole is:
A) Proportional to 1/r^3
B) Proportional to 1/r^2
C) Proportional to 1/r
D) Proportional to r
Answer: A [E_equatorial = k p / r^3.]

Q: The electric potential at the axial point of an electric dipole is:
A) Proportional to 1/r^2
B) Proportional to 1/r
C) Proportional to 1/r^3
D) Constant
Answer: A [V_axial = k p / r^2.]

Q: The electric potential at the equatorial point of an electric dipole is:
A) Zero
B) Proportional to 1/r
C) Proportional to 1/r^2
D) Proportional to r
Answer: A [V_equatorial = 0.]

Q: The torque on an electric dipole placed in a uniform electric field is:
A) tau = p E sin(theta)
B) tau = p E cos(theta)
C) tau = p E
D) tau = p / E
Answer: A [torque = p x E = p E sin(theta).]

Q: The potential energy of an electric dipole in a uniform electric field is:
A) U = -p E cos(theta)
B) U = p E cos(theta)
C) U = p E sin(theta)
D) U = -p E sin(theta)
Answer: A [U = -p.E = -pE cos(theta).]

Q: The electric field at a point due to a uniformly charged infinite plane sheet is:
A) sigma / (2 epsilon0)
B) sigma / epsilon0
C) 2 sigma / epsilon0
D) sigma / (4 epsilon0)
Answer: A [E = sigma / (2 epsilon0).]

Q: The electric field due to two parallel infinite charged sheets with opposite charges is:
A) sigma / epsilon0
B) sigma / (2 epsilon0)
C) 2 sigma / epsilon0
D) 0
Answer: A [Between plates, E = sigma / epsilon0.]

Q: The electric field due to two parallel infinite charged sheets with same charge is:
A) Zero in between
B) sigma / epsilon0
C) sigma / (2 epsilon0)
D) 2 sigma / epsilon0
Answer: A [Between plates, E = 0.]

Q: A charge q is placed at the center of a cube. The electric flux through one face is:
A) q / (6 epsilon0)
B) q / epsilon0
C) q / (4 epsilon0)
D) q / (2 epsilon0)
Answer: A [By Gauss's law, total flux = q/epsilon0. Divided among 6 faces = q/(6 epsilon0).]

Q: A charge q is placed at the center of a sphere. The electric flux through the sphere is:
A) q / epsilon0
B) q / (4 epsilon0)
C) q / (2 epsilon0)
D) q / (6 epsilon0)
Answer: A [Total flux = q / epsilon0.]

Q: The electric field due to a point charge at a distance r is E. If the distance is doubled, the field becomes:
A) E / 4
B) E / 2
C) 2E
D) 4E
Answer: A [E ∝ 1/r^2. So E' = E/4.]

Q: The electric field at a point is 10 N/C. The force on a charge of 2 C placed at that point is:
A) 20 N
B) 5 N
C) 10 N
D) 2 N
Answer: A [F = qE = 2 * 10 = 20 N.]

Q: The number of electrons in 1 C of charge is:
A) 6.25 x 10^18
B) 1.6 x 10^19
C) 6.25 x 10^16
D) 1.6 x 10^18
Answer: A [1 C = 1 / (1.6 x 10^-19) = 6.25 x 10^18 electrons.]

Q: The electric field lines for a positive charge are:
A) Radial and outward
B) Radial and inward
C) Tangential
D) Circular
Answer: A [Field lines are radial and outward for positive charge.]

Q: The electric field lines for a negative charge are:
A) Radial and inward
B) Radial and outward
C) Tangential
D) Circular
Answer: A [Field lines are radial and inward for negative charge.]

Q: The electric field lines from a dipole start from:
A) Positive charge
B) Negative charge
C) Both charges
D) Midpoint
Answer: A [Field lines originate from positive charge and terminate on negative.]

Q: The electric field lines are denser where the field is:
A) Stronger
B) Weaker
C) Zero
D) Constant
Answer: A [Density of field lines is proportional to field strength.]

Q: The electric field inside a charged conductor is:
A) Zero
B) Non-zero
C) Infinite
D) Variable
Answer: A [Inside a conductor, E = 0.]

Q: The force between two charges in a medium of dielectric constant K is:
A) F / K
B) F * K
C) F / K^2
D) F * K^2
Answer: A [Force in medium = F / K.]

Q: The electric field due to a short electric dipole at a point on its axis is:
A) E = 2 k p / r^3
B) E = k p / r^3
C) E = k p / r^2
D) E = 2 k p / r^2
Answer: A [E_axis = 2 k p / r^3.]

Q: A charge q is placed at a distance x from a charge Q. The force is F. If the distance is doubled, the new force is:
A) F / 4
B) F / 2
C) 2F
D) 4F
Answer: A [F ∝ 1/r^2. F' = F/4.]

Q: The electric field at a point due to a uniform spherical shell is zero:
A) Inside the shell
B) Outside the shell
C) On the surface
D) Everywhere
Answer: A [Inside a spherical shell, field is zero.]

Q: The electric field outside a uniformly charged sphere is:
A) Same as that of a point charge at the center
B) Zero
C) Uniform
D) Infinite
Answer: A [For r > R, field is same as if all charge were at the center.]

Q: The electric flux through a closed surface depends only on:
A) The enclosed charge
B) The surface area
C) The electric field
D) The shape of the surface
Answer: A [Gauss's law: flux depends only on enclosed charge.]

Q: The field at the center of a uniformly charged ring is:
A) Zero
B) Maximum
C) Constant
D) Infinite
Answer: A [By symmetry, field at center is zero.]

Q: The electric field at the center of a uniformly charged circular arc subtending angle theta is:
A) (2 k lambda / R) sin(theta/2)
B) (k lambda / R) sin(theta)
C) (2 k lambda / R) cos(theta/2)
D) (k lambda / R) cos(theta)
Answer: A [E = (2 k lambda / R) sin(theta/2).]

Q: A positively charged body attracts a neutral body due to:
A) Induction
B) Conduction
C) Friction
D) Radiation
Answer: A [Charged body induces opposite charge on neutral body, causing attraction.]
