# 12_Kinetic_Theory

Q: The kinetic theory of gases assumes that gas molecules:
A) Move randomly with varying speeds
B) Are stationary
C) Move in a single direction
D) Move in circular paths
Answer: A [Gas molecules move randomly in all directions with different speeds.]

Q: The average kinetic energy of a gas molecule is proportional to:
A) Absolute temperature
B) Pressure
C) Volume
D) Density
Answer: A [K_avg = (3/2) k_B T. So, it is proportional to absolute temperature.]

Q: The root mean square (RMS) speed of gas molecules is:
A) sqrt(3RT/M)
B) sqrt(2RT/M)
C) sqrt(RT/M)
D) sqrt(3M/RT)
Answer: A [v_rms = sqrt(3RT/M).]

Q: The most probable speed of gas molecules is:
A) sqrt(2RT/M)
B) sqrt(3RT/M)
C) sqrt(RT/M)
D) sqrt(3M/RT)
Answer: A [v_mp = sqrt(2RT/M).]

Q: The average speed of gas molecules is:
A) sqrt(8RT/pi M)
B) sqrt(3RT/M)
C) sqrt(2RT/M)
D) sqrt(RT/M)
Answer: A [v_avg = sqrt(8RT/pi M).]

Q: The relation between RMS speed (v_rms) and most probable speed (v_mp) is:
A) v_rms > v_mp
B) v_rms < v_mp
C) v_rms = v_mp
D) v_rms = 0
Answer: A [v_rms = sqrt(3RT/M) > v_mp = sqrt(2RT/M).]

Q: The ideal gas equation is:
A) PV = nRT
B) PV = nR/T
C) P = nRTV
D) V = nRTP
Answer: A [Ideal gas equation: PV = nRT.]

Q: The universal gas constant (R) has value:
A) 8.314 J/mol K
B) 1.38e-23 J/K
C) 6.67e-11 N m^2/kg^2
D) 3e8 m/s
Answer: A [R = 8.314 J mol^-1 K^-1.]

Q: The Boltzmann constant (k_B) has value:
A) 1.38e-23 J/K
B) 8.314 J/mol K
C) 6.67e-11 N m^2/kg^2
D) 3e8 m/s
Answer: A [k_B = R / N_A = 1.38e-23 J/K.]

Q: The degree of freedom for a monoatomic gas molecule is:
A) 3
B) 5
C) 6
D) 2
Answer: A [Monoatomic gas has 3 translational degrees of freedom.]

Q: The degree of freedom for a diatomic gas molecule at room temperature is:
A) 5
B) 3
C) 6
D) 2
Answer: A [Diatomic gas has 3 translational + 2 rotational = 5 degrees of freedom.]

Q: The degree of freedom for a polyatomic gas molecule is:
A) 6
B) 3
C) 5
D) 2
Answer: A [Polyatomic gas has 3 translational + 3 rotational = 6 degrees of freedom.]

Q: The molar specific heat at constant volume (C_v) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 1.5 R
B) 2.5 R
C) 3.5 R
D) 0.5 R
Answer: A [C_v = (f/2) R = (3/2) R = 1.5 R.]

Q: The molar specific heat at constant pressure (C_p) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 2.5 R
B) 1.5 R
C) 3.5 R
D) 0.5 R
Answer: A [C_p = C_v + R = 1.5 R + R = 2.5 R.]

Q: The ratio of specific heats (gamma) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 5/3
B) 7/5
C) 3/2
D) 4/3
Answer: A [gamma = C_p/C_v = 2.5/1.5 = 5/3.]

Q: The molar specific heat at constant volume (C_v) for a diatomic gas is:
A) 2.5 R
B) 1.5 R
C) 3.5 R
D) 0.5 R
Answer: A [C_v = (5/2) R = 2.5 R.]

Q: The molar specific heat at constant pressure (C_p) for a diatomic gas is:
A) 3.5 R
B) 2.5 R
C) 1.5 R
D) 4.5 R
Answer: A [C_p = C_v + R = 2.5 R + R = 3.5 R.]

Q: The ratio of specific heats (gamma) for a diatomic gas is:
A) 7/5
B) 5/3
C) 3/2
D) 4/3
Answer: A [gamma = C_p/C_v = 3.5/2.5 = 7/5.]

Q: The mean free path of a gas molecule is:
A) The average distance between collisions
B) The average speed of molecules
C) The average kinetic energy
D) The total distance traveled
Answer: A [Mean free path is average distance a molecule travels between collisions.]

Q: The mean free path (lambda) is inversely proportional to:
A) Pressure
B) Volume
C) Temperature
D) Density
Answer: A [lambda = 1/(sqrt(2) pi d^2 n). n ∝ P, so lambda ∝ 1/P.]

Q: The mean free path increases with:
A) Increase in temperature at constant pressure
B) Increase in pressure
C) Increase in density
D) Decrease in volume
Answer: A [At constant pressure, T increases -> n decreases -> lambda increases.]

Q: The transport of momentum in a gas is related to:
A) Viscosity
B) Thermal conductivity
C) Diffusion
D) All of these
Answer: D [Transport phenomena in gases: viscosity (momentum), conduction (energy), diffusion (mass).]

Q: The law of equipartition of energy states that each degree of freedom contributes:
A) 0.5 k_B T energy per molecule
B) k_B T energy per molecule
C) 2 k_B T energy per molecule
D) 0.5 R T energy per mole
Answer: A [Equipartition theorem: energy per degree of freedom = 0.5 k_B T.]

Q: The internal energy of one mole of a monoatomic gas is:
A) 1.5 RT
B) 2.5 RT
C) 3.5 RT
D) 0.5 RT
Answer: A [U = (f/2) RT = 1.5 RT for monoatomic.]

Q: The internal energy of one mole of a diatomic gas is:
A) 2.5 RT
B) 1.5 RT
C) 3.5 RT
D) 0.5 RT
Answer: A [U = (f/2) RT = 2.5 RT for diatomic.]

Q: The pressure exerted by an ideal gas is given by:
A) P = (1/3) rho v_rms^2
B) P = rho v_rms^2
C) P = (1/2) rho v_rms^2
D) P = (2/3) rho v_rms^2
Answer: A [P = (1/3) rho v_rms^2.]

Q: The average kinetic energy of a gas molecule at temperature T is:
A) (3/2) k_B T
B) (5/2) k_B T
C) k_B T
D) (1/2) k_B T
Answer: A [K_avg = (3/2) k_B T.]

Q: The translational kinetic energy of one mole of a gas is:
A) (3/2) RT
B) (5/2) RT
C) RT
D) (1/2) RT
Answer: A [Translational K = (3/2) RT.]

Q: The total kinetic energy of one mole of a diatomic gas is:
A) (5/2) RT
B) (3/2) RT
C) RT
D) (1/2) RT
Answer: A [Total K = (f/2) RT = (5/2) RT.]

Q: The root mean square speed of oxygen molecules at 300 K is (M = 32 g/mol, R = 8.314):
A) 483 m/s
B) 500 m/s
C) 400 m/s
D) 600 m/s
Answer: A [v_rms = sqrt(3RT/M) = sqrt(3*8.314*300/0.032) ≈ 483 m/s.]

Q: The average speed of molecules is related to RMS speed by:
A) v_avg = 0.921 v_rms
B) v_avg = v_rms
C) v_avg = 2 v_rms
D) v_avg = 0.5 v_rms
Answer: A [v_avg = sqrt(8/3pi) v_rms ≈ 0.921 v_rms.]

Q: The most probable speed is related to RMS speed by:
A) v_mp = sqrt(2/3) v_rms
B) v_mp = v_rms
C) v_mp = 2 v_rms
D) v_mp = 0.5 v_rms
Answer: A [v_mp = sqrt(2/3) v_rms ≈ 0.816 v_rms.]

Q: The Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution gives the:
A) Distribution of molecular speeds
B) Distribution of molecular energies
C) Average kinetic energy
D) RMS speed
Answer: A [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution describes the distribution of speeds.]

Q: The fraction of molecules having speed greater than a certain value increases with:
A) Increase in temperature
B) Decrease in temperature
C) Increase in pressure
D) Decrease in volume
Answer: A [Higher temperature means more molecules have high speeds.]

Q: The mean free path (lambda) is given by:
A) lambda = 1 / (sqrt(2) pi d^2 n)
B) lambda = 1 / (pi d^2 n)
C) lambda = sqrt(2) / (pi d^2 n)
D) lambda = 1 / (2 pi d^2 n)
Answer: A [Standard formula for mean free path.]

Q: The number of collisions per second of a molecule is:
A) v / lambda
B) lambda / v
C) v * lambda
D) v + lambda
Answer: A [Collision frequency = v_avg / lambda.]

Q: The viscosity of a gas increases with:
A) Increase in temperature
B) Decrease in temperature
C) Increase in pressure
D) Decrease in volume
Answer: A [Viscosity of gases increases with temperature.]

Q: The thermal conductivity of a gas increases with:
A) Increase in temperature
B) Decrease in temperature
C) Increase in pressure
D) Decrease in volume
Answer: A [Thermal conductivity of gases increases with temperature.]

Q: The diffusion coefficient of a gas is inversely proportional to:
A) Pressure
B) Temperature
C) Volume
D) Density
Answer: A [D ∝ 1/P.]

Q: In an ideal gas, the internal energy depends only on:
A) Temperature
B) Pressure
C) Volume
D) Density
Answer: A [U = f(T) for ideal gas.]

Q: The equation of state for an ideal gas is:
A) PV = nRT
B) PV = constant
C) P = nRT/V
D) V = nRT/P
Answer: A [Ideal gas equation.]

Q: The number of molecules in 1 mole of a gas is:
A) 6.022e23
B) 6.022e22
C) 6.022e24
D) 6.022e25
Answer: A [Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022e23.]

Q: The value of Boltzmann constant k_B in terms of R and N_A is:
A) R / N_A
B) R * N_A
C) N_A / R
D) R / 2 N_A
Answer: A [k_B = R / N_A.]

Q: For a gas, the relation between pressure and kinetic energy density is:
A) P = (2/3) K/V
B) P = K/V
C) P = (1/3) K/V
D) P = (1/2) K/V
Answer: A [P = (2/3) (K/V).]

Q: The RMS speed of gas molecules is directly proportional to:
A) sqrt(T)
B) T
C) T^2
D) 1/T
Answer: A [v_rms ∝ sqrt(T).]

Q: The gas constant R has the same dimensions as:
A) Specific heat
B) Energy per mole per kelvin
C) Force
D) Pressure
Answer: B [R = energy / (mole * K).]
