Q: The C–N–C bond angle in trimethylamine is
A) 109.5°
B) 108°
C) 120°
D) 180°
Answer: B [Explanation: Due to lone pair-bond pair repulsion, the angle is slightly less than tetrahedral.]

Q: Which of the following is a tertiary amine?
A) CH₃NH₂
B) (CH₃)₂NH
C) (CH₃)₃N
D) C₆H₅NH₂
Answer: C [Explanation: Nitrogen attached to three alkyl groups.]

Q: The correct IUPAC name of CH₃NHCH₂CH₃ is
A) N-methylethanamine
B) N-ethylmethanamine
C) ethylmethylamine
D) both A and B
Answer: A [Explanation: Preferred IUPAC name is N-methylethanamine.]

Q: Which of the following amines has the highest boiling point?
A) CH₃NH₂
B) (CH₃)₂NH
C) (CH₃)₃N
D) CH₃CH₂NH₂
Answer: D [Explanation: Primary amines have strongest intermolecular H-bonding; higher molecular mass further increases b.p.]

Q: The correct order of basic strength in aqueous solution is
A) (CH₃)₂NH > CH₃NH₂ > (CH₃)₃N > NH₃
B) (CH₃)₃N > (CH₃)₂NH > CH₃NH₂ > NH₃
C) CH₃NH₂ > (CH₃)₂NH > (CH₃)₃N > NH₃
D) (CH₃)₂NH > (CH₃)₃N > CH₃NH₂ > NH₃
Answer: A [Explanation: Combined effect of inductive effect and solvation.]

Q: Which of the following is the strongest base in aqueous medium?
A) C₆H₅NH₂
B) C₆H₅NHCH₃
C) C₆H₅N(CH₃)₂
D) CH₃NH₂
Answer: D [Explanation: Aliphatic amines are stronger bases than aromatic amines due to resonance in aniline derivatives.]

Q: Aniline is less basic than ethylamine because
A) of +R effect of phenyl group
B) of –R effect of phenyl group
C) of +I effect of phenyl group
D) of –I effect of phenyl group
Answer: B [Explanation: Lone pair of nitrogen is delocalised into the benzene ring, decreasing availability for protonation.]

Q: Which of the following reagents is used for the carbylamine test?
A) CHCl₃ + KOH
B) CHCl₃ + NaOH
C) both A and B
D) CCl₄ + KOH
Answer: C [Explanation: Primary amines give foul-smelling isocyanides with CHCl₃/KOH or NaOH.]

Q: The product formed when aniline is treated with bromine water is
A) o-bromoaniline
B) p-bromoaniline
C) 2,4,6-tribromoaniline
D) m-bromoaniline
Answer: C [Explanation: –NH₂ is strongly activating; polysubstitution occurs.]

Q: Which of the following amines will not give Hinsberg test?
A) CH₃NH₂
B) (CH₃)₂NH
C) (CH₃)₃N
D) C₆H₅NH₂
Answer: C [Explanation: Tertiary amines do not react with Hinsberg reagent (benzenesulphonyl chloride).]

Q: Assertion (A): Aniline does not undergo Friedel-Crafts reaction.

A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true

Q: Which of the following is the most basic in gas phase?
A) NH₃
B) CH₃NH₂
C) (CH₃)₂NH
D) (CH₃)₃N
Answer: D [Explanation: In gas phase, only inductive effect operates; tertiary amine is strongest.]

Q: The product obtained when acetamide is reduced with LiAlH₄ is

B) CH₃CH₂NH₂
C) (CH₃)₂NH
