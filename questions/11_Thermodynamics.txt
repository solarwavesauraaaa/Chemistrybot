# 11_Thermodynamics

Q: Thermodynamics is the study of:
A) Heat and its conversion into work
B) Motion of bodies
C) Electric current
D) Light propagation
Answer: A [Thermodynamics deals with heat, work, and energy transformations.]

Q: The state of a thermodynamic system is defined by:
A) Pressure, volume, and temperature
B) Only pressure
C) Only volume
D) Only temperature
Answer: A [State variables: P, V, T determine the state of a system.]

Q: Which of the following is an extensive property?
A) Volume
B) Pressure
C) Temperature
D) Density
Answer: A [Extensive properties depend on the size of the system. Volume is extensive.]

Q: Which of the following is an intensive property?
A) Temperature
B) Volume
C) Mass
D) Internal energy
Answer: A [Intensive properties do not depend on size. Temperature is intensive.]

Q: The first law of thermodynamics is a statement of:
A) Conservation of energy
B) Conservation of mass
C) Conservation of momentum
D) Conservation of charge
Answer: A [First law: Energy is conserved. delta_U = Q - W.]

Q: In the first law of thermodynamics, the internal energy of a system depends on:
A) The path taken
B) Only the initial and final states
C) The work done
D) The heat supplied
Answer: B [Internal energy is a state function; it depends only on initial and final states.]

Q: The work done by a gas in an isothermal expansion is:
A) nRT ln(V2/V1)
B) nRT (V2 - V1)
C) nRT ln(V1/V2)
D) nRT (V1 - V2)
Answer: A [W = nRT ln(V2/V1) for isothermal process.]

Q: In an adiabatic process, there is:
A) No exchange of heat
B) No change in temperature
C) No change in volume
D) No change in pressure
Answer: A [Adiabatic means no heat exchange (Q = 0).]

Q: In an isochoric process, the volume:
A) Remains constant
B) Increases
C) Decreases
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [Isochoric means constant volume.]

Q: In an isobaric process, the pressure:
A) Remains constant
B) Increases
C) Decreases
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [Isobaric means constant pressure.]

Q: The efficiency of a heat engine is defined as:
A) Work output / Heat input
B) Heat input / Work output
C) Heat rejected / Heat input
D) Work output / Heat rejected
Answer: A [Efficiency = W / Q_in.]

Q: The Carnot engine is a theoretical engine with:
A) Maximum possible efficiency
B) Minimum efficiency
C) Zero efficiency
D) Negative efficiency
Answer: A [Carnot engine has maximum efficiency for given temperatures.]

Q: The efficiency of a Carnot engine depends on:
A) Temperatures of source and sink
B) Nature of working substance
C) Volume of the engine
D) Pressure of the engine
Answer: A [Efficiency = 1 - T_sink / T_source.]

Q: The second law of thermodynamics states that:
A) Heat cannot flow from a colder body to a hotter body spontaneously
B) Energy is conserved
C) Mass is conserved
D) Momentum is conserved
Answer: A [Second law: Spontaneous flow of heat is from hot to cold.]

Q: The change in entropy (delta_S) of a system is given by:
A) delta_S = Q / T
B) delta_S = Q * T
C) delta_S = T / Q
D) delta_S = Q - T
Answer: A [Entropy change = Heat / Temperature.]

Q: For a reversible process, the total entropy change of the universe is:
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Infinite
Answer: A [For reversible process, delta_S_universe = 0.]

Q: For an irreversible process, the entropy of the universe:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [For irreversible process, entropy of universe increases.]

Q: A refrigerator works on the principle of:
A) Second law of thermodynamics
B) First law of thermodynamics
C) Law of conservation of mass
D) Law of conservation of momentum
Answer: A [Refrigerator transfers heat from cold to hot by doing work, following second law.]

Q: The coefficient of performance of a refrigerator is:
A) Q_c / W
B) W / Q_c
C) Q_h / W
D) W / Q_h
Answer: A [COP = Heat removed from cold reservoir / Work input.]

Q: In a cyclic process, the net work done is equal to:
A) Area enclosed by the cycle on P-V diagram
B) Zero
C) Heat supplied
D) Change in internal energy
Answer: A [Area of P-V cycle = net work done.]

Q: The internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on:
A) Temperature
B) Pressure
C) Volume
D) Both B and C
Answer: A [For ideal gas, U depends only on T.]

Q: In a free expansion of a gas, the work done by the gas is:
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Infinite
Answer: A [In free expansion (into vacuum), W = 0.]

Q: In a free expansion of an ideal gas, the temperature:
A) Remains constant
B) Increases
C) Decreases
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [For ideal gas, free expansion is isothermal (T constant).]

Q: The molar heat capacity at constant volume (C_v) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 1.5 R
B) 2.5 R
C) 3.5 R
D) 0.5 R
Answer: A [C_v = (3/2) R for monoatomic gas.]

Q: The molar heat capacity at constant pressure (C_p) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 2.5 R
B) 1.5 R
C) 3.5 R
D) 0.5 R
Answer: A [C_p = C_v + R = 2.5 R.]

Q: The ratio of specific heats (gamma) for a monoatomic gas is:
A) 5/3
B) 7/5
C) 3/2
D) 4/3
Answer: A [gamma = C_p/C_v = 2.5R/1.5R = 5/3.]

Q: In an adiabatic process, the relation between P and V is:
A) P V^gamma = constant
B) P V = constant
C) P V^2 = constant
D) P / V = constant
Answer: A [Adiabatic relation: P V^gamma = constant.]

Q: The work done in an adiabatic process is equal to:
A) Change in internal energy
B) Change in enthalpy
C) Heat supplied
D) Zero
Answer: A [W = -delta_U (since Q = 0).]

Q: The heat supplied to a system at constant volume goes entirely into:
A) Increasing internal energy
B) Doing work
C) Increasing temperature and doing work
D) Increasing pressure
Answer: A [At constant volume, W = 0, so Q = delta_U.]

Q: The heat supplied to a system at constant pressure goes into:
A) Increasing internal energy and doing work
B) Only increasing internal energy
C) Only doing work
D) Increasing pressure
Answer: A [Q = delta_U + W.]

Q: The efficiency of a Carnot engine operating between 500 K and 300 K is:
A) 40%
B) 60%
C) 50%
D) 80%
Answer: A [Efficiency = 1 - 300/500 = 1 - 0.6 = 0.4 = 40%.]

Q: A heat engine operates between 600 K and 300 K. The maximum possible efficiency is:
A) 50%
B) 25%
C) 75%
D) 100%
Answer: A [Max efficiency = 1 - 300/600 = 0.5 = 50%.]

Q: The COP of a refrigerator operating between 300 K and 200 K is:
A) 2
B) 1
C) 0.5
D) 3
Answer: A [COP = T_c / (T_h - T_c) = 200 / (300 - 200) = 2.]

Q: The process in which no heat enters or leaves the system is:
A) Adiabatic
B) Isothermal
C) Isochoric
D) Isobaric
Answer: A [Adiabatic: Q = 0.]

Q: The process in which temperature remains constant is:
A) Isothermal
B) Adiabatic
C) Isochoric
D) Isobaric
Answer: A [Isothermal: T = constant.]

Q: In a P-V diagram, the work done by a gas in a cyclic process is:
A) The area inside the cycle
B) Zero
C) The perimeter
D) The volume
Answer: A [Area of P-V cycle = net work.]

Q: The first law of thermodynamics can be written as:
A) delta_U = Q - W
B) delta_U = Q + W
C) delta_U = Q * W
D) delta_U = Q / W
Answer: A [First law: delta_U = Q - W.]

Q: The specific heat of a gas at constant pressure is greater than at constant volume because:
A) Work is done in expansion
B) No work is done
C) Temperature rises more
D) Pressure rises more
Answer: A [At constant pressure, gas expands and does work, so more heat is needed.]

Q: The thermodynamic state of a system is specified by:
A) Pressure, volume, and temperature
B) Mass only
C) Velocity only
D) Acceleration only
Answer: A [State variables: P, V, T.]

Q: In an isothermal process, the internal energy of an ideal gas:
A) Remains constant
B) Increases
C) Decreases
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [For ideal gas, U depends only on T, so in isothermal U is constant.]

Q: A gas does 100 J of work and absorbs 150 J of heat. The change in internal energy is:
A) 50 J
B) 250 J
C) -50 J
D) 100 J
Answer: A [delta_U = Q - W = 150 - 100 = 50 J.]

Q: A gas does 200 J of work and rejects 50 J of heat. The change in internal energy is:
A) -250 J
B) 250 J
C) 150 J
D) -150 J
Answer: A [Q = -50 J (rejected). delta_U = Q - W = -50 - 200 = -250 J.]

Q: In a cyclic process, the change in internal energy is:
A) Zero
B) Positive
C) Negative
D) Maximum
Answer: A [In a cyclic process, the system returns to its initial state, so delta_U = 0.]

Q: The efficiency of a heat engine is maximum when:
A) The sink temperature is as low as possible
B) The source temperature is as low as possible
C) The sink temperature is as high as possible
D) Both source and sink are at same temperature
Answer: A [Efficiency = 1 - T_sink/T_source. Lower T_sink gives higher efficiency.]
