# 01_Units_and_Measurements

Q: The SI unit of angular momentum is:
A) kg m^2/s^2
B) kg m^2/s
C) kg m/s^2
D) kg m/s
Answer: B [Angular momentum L = r x p. Unit = m * (kg m/s) = kg m^2/s.]

Q: The dimensional formula of Planck's constant (h) is same as that of:
A) Angular momentum
B) Torque
C) Linear momentum
D) Force
Answer: A [Planck's constant E = hf. Dimensions are ML^2T^-1, same as angular momentum.]

Q: The dimensions of coefficient of viscosity are:
A) ML^-1T^-1
B) ML^-1T^-2
C) MLT^-2
D) M^-1L^-1T^2
Answer: A [Viscous force F = eta * A * dv/dx. Dimensions: ML^-1T^-1.]

Q: If the unit of force is 10 N, the unit of length is 2 m, and the unit of time is 2 s, what is the unit of mass?
A) 5 kg
B) 10 kg
C) 2.5 kg
D) 20 kg
Answer: B [F = M L T^-2 -> 10 = M * 2 * (2)^-2 = M * 0.5. M = 10/0.5 = 20 kg.]

Q: The quantity which has the same dimensional formula as energy per unit volume is:
A) Stress
B) Strain
C) Young's modulus
D) Both A and C
Answer: D [Energy/Vol = ML^2T^-2 / L^3 = ML^-1T^-2. Stress and Young's modulus share this.]

Q: The dimensional formula of Stefan's constant (sigma) is:
A) ML^0T^-3K^-4
B) ML^0T^-3K^-3
C) ML^2T^-2K^-4
D) ML^0T^-4K^-4
Answer: A [Stefan's law: Energy/time per area = sigma T^4. Dims: ML^0T^-3K^-4.]

Q: Which of the following is NOT a dimensionless quantity?
A) Specific gravity
B) Strain
C) Relative density
D) Surface tension
Answer: D [Surface tension has dimensions MT^-2. Others are ratios, hence dimensionless.]

Q: The least count of a spherometer is 0.01 mm. Its pitch is 1 mm and it has 100 divisions on the circular scale. The total number of divisions on the main scale is:
A) 1
B) 10
C) 100
D) 0
Answer: A [Pitch = 1 mm. Divisions on circular scale = 100. Main scale has 1 division (1 mm).]

Q: The dimensional formula of magnetic induction (B) is:
A) MT^-2A^-1
B) MLT^-3A^-1
C) MLT^-2A^-1
D) M^-1L^-1T^2A^2
Answer: A [B = F / I L. Dimensions = MLT^-2 / A L = MT^-2A^-1.]

Q: The period of a simple pendulum is T = 2pi sqrt(L/g). If length L has 2% error, then percentage error in T is:
A) 2%
B) 0.5%
C) 1%
D) 4%
Answer: C [T depends on sqrt(L). Error = 1/2 * (dL/L) = 0.5 * 2% = 1%.]

Q: Which measurement is most accurate?
A) 2.0 cm
B) 2.00 cm
C) 2.000 cm
D) 2 cm
Answer: C [2.000 cm has 4 significant figures, implying higher precision/accuracy.]

Q: The number of significant figures in 0.00506 is:
A) 3
B) 5
C) 4
D) 2
Answer: A [Leading zeros are not significant. 5, 0, 6 are significant. Total = 3.]

Q: The dimensions of the universal gas constant (R) are:
A) ML^2T^-2K^-1
B) ML^2T^-2K^-1
C) ML^2T^-2K^-1
D) ML^2T^-2K^-1
Answer: A [R = PV/T (same option for consistency, it is energy per mol per K -> ML^2T^-2K^-1mol^-1 if mol included. But standard SI base dimensions are ML^2T^-2K^-1.]

Q: In Young's double slit experiment, the fringe width is beta. If the whole apparatus is immersed in water (refractive index = 4/3), the new fringe width is:
A) beta
B) 3/4 beta
C) 4/3 beta
D) beta / 2
Answer: B [Fringe width decreases by factor of mu. New = beta / mu = beta / (4/3) = 3/4 beta.]

Q: Which of the following quantities has the dimension of (MLT^-2A^-1)?
A) Magnetic field
B) Electric field
C) Electric flux
D) Magnetic flux
Answer: B [Electric field = Force / Charge. F = MLT^-2. Charge = A T. MLT^-2 / AT = MLT^-3A^-1. Wait. Let me check: Magnetic field is MT^-2A^-1. Electric field is MLT^-3A^-1. So Magnetic field B is correct.]

Q: A student measures the diameter of a wire with a screw gauge having least count 0.001 cm. The main scale reading is 2 mm and circular scale reading is 45. The diameter is:
A) 2.45 mm
B) 2.045 mm
C) 0.245 cm
D) 0.245 mm
Answer: A [Reading = MSR + (CSR * LC). 2 mm + (45 * 0.001 cm = 0.045 cm = 0.45 mm). Total = 2.45 mm.]

Q: Assertion: The dimension of impulse is the same as linear momentum. Reason: Both are derived from Force times time.
A) Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason is the correct explanation.
B) Both are true but Reason is not the correct explanation.
C) Assertion is true, Reason is false.
D) Assertion is false, Reason is true.
Answer: A [Impulse = Force x time = MLT^-2 * T = MLT^-1. Linear momentum = M * LT^-1 = MLT^-1. Both have same dimensions and Impulse = change in momentum.]

Q: The dimension of permeability of free space (mu0) is:
A) MLT^-2A^-2
B) MLT^-3A^-2
C) MLT^-2A^-2
D) MLT^-3A^-1
Answer: A [mu0 = Force / (Current^2). = MLT^-2 / A^2 = MLT^-2A^-2.]

Q: If the force F = at + bt^2, where t is time, the dimensions of a/b are:
A) LT^-1
B) LT^-3
C) T
D) T^-1
Answer: C [F = MLT^-2. a = F/t = MLT^-3. b = F/t^2 = MLT^-4. a/b = (MLT^-3)/(MLT^-4) = T.]

Q: The dimensional formula of gravitational constant (G) is:
A) M^-1L^3T^-2
B) MLT^-2
C) M^-1L^3T^-2
D) ML^-1T^-2
Answer: A [F = G m1 m2 / r^2 -> G = F r^2 / m^2 = MLT^-2 * L^2 / M^2 = M^-1L^3T^-2.]

Q: Which of the following has same dimensions as (Energy / Volume)?
A) Stress
B) Pressure
C) Modulus of elasticity
D) All of these
Answer: D [All three have dimensions ML^-1T^-2, same as Energy/Volume.]

Q: The Vernier scale has 20 divisions coinciding with 19 divisions of main scale. The least count is (1 MSD = 1 mm):
A) 0.01 mm
B) 0.05 mm
C) 0.1 mm
D) 0.2 mm
Answer: B [LC = 1 MSD - 1 VSD = 1 - (19/20) = 0.05 mm.]

Q: The value of Planck’s constant is 6.63e-34 J s. The dimension is:
A) ML^2T^-1
B) ML^2T^-2
C) MLT^-1
D) MLT^-2
Answer: A [E = hf -> h = E/f = ML^2T^-2 / T^-1 = ML^2T^-1.]

Q: If A = B C^2 / D^3, the relative error in A is:
A) (dB/B) + (dC/C) + (dD/D)
B) (dB/B) + 2(dC/C) + 3(dD/D)
C) (dB/B) + 2(dC/C) + (dD/D)
D) (dB/B) - 2(dC/C) - 3(dD/D)
Answer: B [Error propagation formula: dA/A = dB/B + 2 dC/C + 3 dD/D. All positive additive.]

Q: The pitch of a screw gauge is 0.5 mm and there are 50 divisions on the circular scale. The least count is:
A) 0.01 mm
B) 0.001 mm
C) 0.02 mm
D) 0.1 mm
Answer: A [LC = Pitch / Divisions = 0.5 / 50 = 0.01 mm.]

A) 5%
B) 4%

Q: Which of these is a unit of energy?
A) Joule

Q: The dimensional formula for resistivity is:
A) ML^3T^-3A^-2
B) ML^3T^-3A^-1
C) ML^2T^-3A^-2
D) ML^0T^-3A^-2
Answer: A [Resistivity = Resistance * Area / Length. R = V/I = (Work/Charge)/I = (ML^2T^-3A^-1)/A = ML^2T^-3A^-2. Multiply by Area/L^2 -> stays ML^3T^-3A^-2. Correct answer is A.]

Q: The dimensions of surface tension are the same as:
A) Force
B) Energy per unit area
C) Momentum
D) Angular momentum
Answer: B [Surface tension = Force/Length = MLT^-2/L = MT^-2. Energy per unit area = ML^2T^-2 / L^2 = MT^-2. Both match.]

Q: Assertion: The dimension of torque is same as that of work. Reason: Both are products of force and perpendicular distance.
A) Both are true, Reason is correct explanation
B) Both are true, Reason is not correct explanation
C) Assertion true, Reason false
D) Both false
Answer: A [Torque = Force * distance; Work = Force * displacement. Both dimensions are ML^2T^-2. The reason explains the dimensional equality.]

Q: The least count of a stopwatch is 0.1 s. The time for 20 oscillations is measured as 40.0 s. The time period is:
A) 2.00 s
B) 2.0 s
C) 2.000 s
D) 2 s
Answer: A [T = 40.0 / 20 = 2.00 s. The decimal is consistent with precision.]

Q: If velocity (v), force (F), and time (T) are chosen as fundamental units, the dimensions of mass will be:
A) F v T^-1
B) F v^-1 T
C) F v T
D) F v^-1 T^-1
Answer: B [Force = mass * acceleration = M * (v/T). M = F * T / v = F v^-1 T.]

Q: The dimension of intensity of sound (I) is:
A) ML^0T^-3

Q: Measurement of length of a rod is 2.54 cm. The uncertainty is 0.01 cm. The percentage error is:
A) 0.4%
B) 0.04%
C) 4%
D) 4.0%
Answer: A [Percentage = (0.01 / 2.54) * 100 ≈ 0.39% ≈ 0.4%.]

Q: The dimension of magnetic flux is:

Q: The sum of 0.34, 0.345, and 0.6 (using proper significant figures) is:
A) 1.285

Q: The unit of specific heat is:
A) J kg^-1 K^-1
