# 09_Mechanical_Properties_of_Fluids

Q: The SI unit of pressure is:
A) Pascal
B) Newton
C) Joule
D) Watt
Answer: A [Pascal (Pa) is the SI unit of pressure, equal to 1 N/m^2.]

Q: The pressure at a depth h in a liquid of density rho is:
A) rho g h
B) rho / g h
C) g h / rho
D) rho g / h
Answer: A [Hydrostatic pressure: P = rho g h.]

Q: Pascal's law states that pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted:
A) Undiminished in all directions
B) Only in the direction of force
C) Only to the walls
D) In proportion to the area
Answer: A [Pascal's law: Pressure is transmitted undiminished throughout the fluid.]

Q: The buoyant force on a body immersed in a fluid is equal to:
A) Weight of the displaced fluid
B) Weight of the body
C) Volume of the body
D) Density of the body
Answer: A [Archimedes' principle: Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid.]

Q: A body floats in a liquid if:
A) Its density is less than that of the liquid
B) Its density is greater than that of the liquid
C) Its density is equal to that of the liquid
D) Its volume is large
Answer: A [For floating, density of body < density of liquid.]

Q: The Bernoulli's principle is a consequence of:
A) Conservation of energy
B) Conservation of momentum
C) Conservation of mass
D) Newton's laws
Answer: A [Bernoulli's principle is derived from the conservation of energy.]

Q: The velocity of efflux of a liquid from a hole in a tank is given by:
A) sqrt(2gh)
B) sqrt(gh)
C) 2gh
D) gh
Answer: A [Torricelli's theorem: v = sqrt(2gh).]

Q: The rise of liquid in a capillary tube is due to:
A) Surface tension
B) Viscosity
C) Pressure
D) Gravity
Answer: A [Capillary action is due to surface tension.]

Q: The surface tension of a liquid is the force per unit:
A) Length
B) Area
C) Volume
D) Mass
Answer: A [Surface tension = Force / Length (N/m).]

Q: The excess pressure inside a soap bubble of radius R is:
A) 4T / R
B) 2T / R
C) T / R
D) T / 2R
Answer: A [Excess pressure in soap bubble = 4T/R (two surfaces).]

Q: The excess pressure inside a liquid drop of radius R is:
A) 2T / R
B) 4T / R
C) T / R
D) T / 2R
Answer: A [Excess pressure in liquid drop = 2T/R (one surface).]

Q: The rise of liquid in a capillary tube of radius r is given by:
A) h = 2T cos(theta) / (rho g r)
B) h = T cos(theta) / (rho g r)
C) h = 2T / (rho g r)
D) h = T / (rho g r)
Answer: A [Capillary rise: h = 2T cos(theta) / (rho g r).]

Q: The coefficient of viscosity has SI unit:
A) Pa s
B) N/m^2
C) N s / m^2
D) Both A and C
Answer: D [Viscosity unit: Pa s = N s / m^2.]

Q: The viscous force between two layers of a fluid is proportional to:
A) Velocity gradient
B) Area
C) Both A and B
D) Temperature
Answer: C [Viscous force F = eta A (dv/dx).]

Q: The terminal velocity of a small sphere falling in a viscous liquid is:
A) Proportional to r^2
B) Proportional to r
C) Proportional to 1/r
D) Proportional to 1/r^2
Answer: A [v_t = (2/9) (r^2 g (rho - sigma) / eta).]

Q: The Reynolds number is a dimensionless quantity used to determine:
A) Nature of flow (laminar/turbulent)
B) Viscosity
C) Surface tension
D) Density
Answer: A [Reynolds number determines whether flow is laminar or turbulent.]

Q: The critical velocity for a fluid flow is given by:
A) v_c = Re * eta / (rho D)
B) v_c = Re * rho / (eta D)
C) v_c = Re * D / (rho eta)
D) v_c = Re * eta * D / rho
Answer: A [v_c = Re * eta / (rho D).]

Q: The normal force exerted by a fluid on a surface per unit area is called:
A) Pressure
B) Stress
C) Strain
D) Buoyancy
Answer: A [Pressure is normal force per unit area.]

Q: The law of floatation states that a body floats when:
A) Weight of body equals weight of displaced fluid
B) Weight of body is less than buoyant force
C) Weight of body is greater than buoyant force
D) Density of body equals density of fluid
Answer: A [For floating, weight = buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid.]

Q: The height of a liquid column in a barometer depends on:
A) Atmospheric pressure
B) Density of liquid
C) Both A and B
D) Diameter of tube
Answer: C [h = P_atm / (rho g), so it depends on both atmospheric pressure and density.]

Q: The gauge pressure at a depth h in a liquid is:
A) rho g h
B) P_atm + rho g h
C) P_atm - rho g h
D) P_atm
Answer: A [Gauge pressure = absolute pressure - atmospheric pressure = rho g h.]

Q: A hydraulic lift works on the principle of:
A) Pascal's law
B) Archimedes' principle
C) Bernoulli's principle
D) Torricelli's theorem
Answer: A [Hydraulic lift is based on Pascal's law.]

Q: The Venturi meter works on the principle of:
A) Bernoulli's principle
B) Pascal's law
C) Archimedes' principle
D) Torricelli's theorem
Answer: A [Venturi meter measures flow rate using Bernoulli's principle.]

Q: The speed of liquid flowing out of a hole at depth h is:
A) sqrt(2gh)
B) 2gh
C) sqrt(gh)
D) gh
Answer: A [Torricelli's theorem: v = sqrt(2gh).]

Q: A liquid flows through a pipe of varying cross-section. At a constriction, the pressure:
A) Decreases
B) Increases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [From Bernoulli's principle, at constriction, velocity increases, pressure decreases.]

Q: The capillary rise in a tube is h. If the tube radius is doubled, the rise is:
A) h/2
B) 2h
C) h
D) h/4
Answer: A [h = 2T cos(theta) / (rho g r). If r is doubled, h becomes half.]

Q: The surface tension of a liquid decreases with:
A) Increase in temperature
B) Decrease in temperature
C) Increase in pressure
D) Decrease in pressure
Answer: A [Surface tension decreases as temperature increases.]

Q: A drop of liquid is spherical because:
A) Surface tension tries to minimize surface area
B) Gravity is negligible
C) Viscosity is zero
D) Density is uniform
Answer: A [Surface tension makes drops spherical to minimize surface area.]

Q: The angle of contact for pure water and glass is:
A) 0 degrees
B) 90 degrees
C) 180 degrees
D) 45 degrees
Answer: A [Water wets glass, so contact angle is 0 degrees.]

Q: The angle of contact for mercury and glass is:
A) 135 degrees
B) 0 degrees
C) 90 degrees
D) 45 degrees
Answer: A [Mercury does not wet glass. Contact angle is about 135 degrees.]

Q: A sphere of radius r falls through a viscous liquid. The viscous force is proportional to:
A) r
B) r^2
C) r^3
D) 1/r
Answer: A [Stokes' law: F = 6 pi eta r v.]

Q: The terminal velocity of a falling sphere depends on:
A) Radius of sphere
B) Density difference
C) Viscosity of liquid
D) All of these
Answer: D [v_t = (2/9) r^2 g (rho - sigma) / eta.]

Q: A fluid is said to be ideal if:
A) It is incompressible and non-viscous
B) It is compressible and viscous
C) It has high density
D) It has low density
Answer: A [Ideal fluid: incompressible and non-viscous.]

Q: The equation of continuity is based on:
A) Conservation of mass
B) Conservation of energy
C) Conservation of momentum
D) Bernoulli's principle
Answer: A [Equation of continuity: A1 v1 = A2 v2, from mass conservation.]

Q: In a streamline flow, the velocity of a fluid particle:
A) Is constant at a given point
B) Changes randomly
C) Is zero
D) Depends on time
Answer: A [In steady streamline flow, velocity at a point is constant with time.]

Q: The pressure in a fluid at rest is:
A) Same in all directions
B) Different in different directions
C) Maximum horizontally
D) Minimum vertically
Answer: A [In a fluid at rest, pressure is isotropic (same in all directions).]

Q: The buoyant force on a body in a fluid depends on:
A) Volume of the body
B) Density of the fluid
C) Both A and B
D) Mass of the body
Answer: C [Buoyant force = rho_fluid * V * g.]

Q: A ship floats because:
A) Its average density is less than water
B) Its mass is less than water
C) Its volume is large
D) Its shape is streamlined
Answer: A [A ship floats because its average density (including air) is less than water.]

Q: The rise of water in a capillary tube is h. If the tube is tilted, the rise:
A) Remains same
B) Increases
C) Decreases
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [The vertical rise remains the same; the length of liquid column increases.]

Q: A drop of water is placed on a waxed surface. The angle of contact is:
A) Obtuse
B) Acute
C) Zero
D) 90 degrees
Answer: A [Water does not wet wax, so contact angle is obtuse (> 90).]

Q: The work done in blowing a soap bubble of radius R is:
A) 8 pi R^2 T
B) 4 pi R^2 T
C) 2 pi R^2 T
D) 6 pi R^2 T
Answer: A [Work done = surface energy = 2 * 4 pi R^2 * T = 8 pi R^2 T.]

Q: The excess pressure in a bubble of radius R in air is:
A) 4T / R
B) 2T / R
C) T / R
D) 8T / R
Answer: A [Excess pressure in air bubble = 4T/R (two surfaces).]

Q: The excess pressure in a liquid drop of radius R in air is:
A) 2T / R
B) 4T / R
C) T / R
D) 8T / R
Answer: A [Excess pressure in liquid drop = 2T/R (one surface).]

Q: The velocity of water in a river is greater at:
A) The center
B) The banks
C) The bottom
D) The surface
Answer: A [Velocity is maximum at the center due to minimum friction.]

Q: A fluid flows in a pipe of varying cross-section. At a wider section, the velocity is:
A) Less
B) Greater
C) Same
D) Zero
Answer: A [From continuity equation, velocity is less at wider section.]

Q: The energy per unit volume of a fluid in streamline flow is:
A) P + 0.5 rho v^2 + rho g h
B) P + rho v^2 + rho g h
C) P + 0.5 rho v^2
D) rho g h
Answer: A [Bernoulli's equation: P + 0.5 rho v^2 + rho g h = constant.]

Q: A liquid is flowing through a horizontal pipe. At a point where pressure is less, the velocity is:
A) Greater
B) Less
C) Same
D) Zero
Answer: A [From Bernoulli's principle, lower pressure means higher velocity.]
