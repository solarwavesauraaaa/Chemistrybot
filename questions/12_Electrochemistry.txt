Q: In a Daniell cell, the standard electrode potential is 1.1 V when concentration of Zn²⁺ and Cu²⁺ is 1 M. If the concentration of Zn²⁺ is increased, the cell potential
A) increases
B) decreases
C) remains same
D) becomes zero
Answer: B [Explanation: From Nernst equation, higher [Zn²⁺] decreases E_cell.]

Q: The standard reduction potential of Zn²⁺/Zn and Cu²⁺/Cu are –0.76 V and +0.34 V respectively. The standard emf of the cell Zn|Zn²⁺||Cu²⁺|Cu is
A) 1.10 V
B) –1.10 V
C) 0.42 V
D) –0.42 V
Answer: A [Explanation: E°_cell = E°_cathode – E°_anode = 0.34 – (–0.76) = 1.10 V]

Q: For the cell reaction Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s), the Nernst equation is
A) E = E° – (0.059/2) log ([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺])
B) E = E° – (0.059/2) log ([Cu²⁺]/[Zn²⁺])
C) E = E° + (0.059/2) log ([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺])
D) E = E° – 0.059 log ([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺])
Answer: A [Explanation: Standard Nernst form for n=2.]

Q: The relationship between standard free energy change and equilibrium constant is
A) ΔG° = –nFE°
B) ΔG° = nFE°
C) ΔG° = –RT ln K
D) Both A and C
Answer: D [Explanation: Both relations are valid; ΔG° = –nFE° = –RT ln K.]

Q: The unit of conductivity is
A) ohm cm
B) ohm⁻¹ cm
C) ohm⁻¹ cm⁻¹
D) ohm cm⁻¹
Answer: C [Explanation: κ = 1/ρ; unit is S cm⁻¹ or ohm⁻¹ cm⁻¹.]

Q: Molar conductivity of a solution increases with
A) increase in concentration
B) decrease in concentration
C) increase in temperature only
D) remains constant
Answer: B [Explanation: Λ_m increases with dilution due to increased degree of dissociation and mobility.]

Q: Kohlrausch's law is used to calculate
A) Λ_m° of weak electrolytes
B) degree of dissociation
C) solubility of sparingly soluble salts
D) All of these
Answer: D [Explanation: All are important applications of Kohlrausch's law.]

Q: The molar conductivity of 0.1 M acetic acid is 5.0 S cm² mol⁻¹. If Λ_m° is 400 S cm² mol⁻¹, the degree of dissociation is
A) 0.0125
B) 0.025
C) 0.05
D) 0.10
Answer: A [Explanation: α = Λ_m / Λ_m° = 5/400 = 0.0125]

Q: In electrolysis of molten NaCl, the product at cathode is
A) Cl₂
B) Na
C) H₂
D) O₂
Answer: B [Explanation: Na⁺ is reduced at cathode to sodium metal.]

Q: Faraday's first law states that the mass of substance deposited is proportional to
A) time
B) current
C) quantity of electricity
D) concentration
Answer: C [Explanation: m ∝ Q (charge).]

Q: The charge required to deposit 1 mole of Al from Al³⁺ is
A) 1 F
B) 2 F
C) 3 F
D) 4 F
Answer: C [Explanation: Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al; 3 faradays required.]

Q: Which of the following is a primary battery?
A) Lead storage battery
B) Nickel-cadmium battery
C) Dry cell
D) Fuel cell
Answer: C [Explanation: Dry cell (Leclanché cell) is a primary battery (non-rechargeable).]

Q: In a lead storage battery, during discharging, the electrolyte becomes
A) more concentrated H₂SO₄
B) dilute H₂SO₄
C) pure water
D) NaOH
Answer: B [Explanation: H₂SO₄ is consumed and water is produced, diluting the acid.]

Q: Corrosion of iron is an electrochemical process. The anode reaction is

B) Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Fe
C) O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H₂O
D) 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻
Answer: A [Explanation: Iron is oxidised at anodic sites.]

Q: Assertion (A): Molar conductivity of strong electrolytes increases slowly with dilution.
Reason (R): For strong electrolytes, degree of dissociation is already nearly 1.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Increase is mainly due to increase in ionic mobility, not dissociation.]

Q: The standard electrode potential of a half-cell is measured by coupling it with
A) hydrogen electrode
B) calomel electrode
C) silver-silver chloride electrode
D) any of these
Answer: A [Explanation: Standard hydrogen electrode is the primary reference.]

Q: For the cell Pt, H₂ (1 atm)|H⁺ (a=1)||Cu²⁺ (1 M)|Cu, E°_cell is 0.34 V. The value of ΔG° is
A) –65.6 kJ
B) –32.8 kJ
C) +65.6 kJ
D) +32.8 kJ
Answer: A [Explanation: ΔG° = –nFE° = –2 × 96500 × 0.34 ≈ –65.6 kJ]

Q: The conductivity of 0.001 M acetic acid is 5 × 10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹. The molar conductivity is
A) 5 S cm² mol⁻¹
B) 50 S cm² mol⁻¹
C) 0.5 S cm² mol⁻¹
D) 500 S cm² mol⁻¹
Answer: B [Explanation: Λ_m = (κ × 1000)/C = (5×10⁻⁵ × 1000)/0.001 = 50]

Q: Which of the following increases with dilution for a strong electrolyte?
