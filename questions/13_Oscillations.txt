# 13_Oscillations

Q: A motion that repeats itself after a fixed interval of time is called:
A) Periodic motion
B) Linear motion
C) Circular motion
D) Random motion
Answer: A [Periodic motion repeats after a fixed time interval.]

Q: The time taken for one complete oscillation is called:
A) Time period
B) Frequency
C) Amplitude
D) Wavelength
Answer: A [Time period (T) is the time for one complete cycle.]

Q: The number of oscillations per second is called:
A) Frequency
B) Time period
C) Amplitude
D) Phase
Answer: A [Frequency (f) = 1/T.]

Q: The maximum displacement from the mean position is called:
A) Amplitude
B) Frequency
C) Time period
D) Phase
Answer: A [Amplitude is the maximum displacement from equilibrium.]

Q: In SHM, the acceleration of a particle is:
A) Directly proportional to displacement
B) Inversely proportional to displacement
C) Independent of displacement
D) Zero at the mean position
Answer: A [a = -omega^2 x. So a ∝ x.]

Q: In SHM, the restoring force is:
A) Directly proportional to displacement
B) Inversely proportional to displacement
C) Constant
D) Zero
Answer: A [F = -kx (Hooke's law).]

Q: The time period of a simple pendulum is:
A) T = 2pi sqrt(L/g)
B) T = 2pi sqrt(g/L)
C) T = 2pi sqrt(m/k)
D) T = 2pi sqrt(k/m)
Answer: A [T_pendulum = 2pi sqrt(L/g).]

Q: The time period of a spring-mass system is:
A) T = 2pi sqrt(m/k)
B) T = 2pi sqrt(k/m)
C) T = 2pi sqrt(L/g)
D) T = 2pi sqrt(g/L)
Answer: A [T_spring = 2pi sqrt(m/k).]

Q: The frequency of SHM is given by:
A) f = 1 / (2pi) sqrt(k/m)
B) f = 2pi sqrt(k/m)
C) f = sqrt(k/m)
D) f = 2pi sqrt(m/k)
Answer: A [f = (1/2pi) sqrt(k/m).]

Q: The total energy of a particle in SHM is:
A) 0.5 k A^2
B) k A
C) 0.5 k A
D) k A^2
Answer: A [Total energy = 0.5 k A^2.]

Q: In SHM, the kinetic energy is maximum at:
A) Mean position
B) Extreme position
C) Midway between mean and extreme
D) All positions
Answer: A [At mean position, speed is maximum, so KE is maximum.]

Q: In SHM, the potential energy is maximum at:
A) Extreme position
B) Mean position
C) Midway between mean and extreme
D) All positions
Answer: A [At extreme position, displacement is maximum, so PE is maximum.]

Q: The phase of a particle in SHM is given by:
A) omega t + phi
B) omega t
C) phi
D) A sin(omega t)
Answer: A [Phase = omega t + phi.]

Q: The displacement in SHM is given by:
A) x = A sin(omega t + phi)
B) x = A cos(omega t + phi)
C) Both A and B
D) x = A tan(omega t)
Answer: C [Both sine and cosine are valid for SHM depending on initial conditions.]

Q: The velocity of a particle in SHM is:
A) v = omega sqrt(A^2 - x^2)
B) v = omega x
C) v = omega^2 sqrt(A^2 - x^2)
D) v = sqrt(A^2 - x^2)
Answer: A [v = omega sqrt(A^2 - x^2).]

Q: The acceleration of a particle in SHM is:
A) a = -omega^2 x
B) a = omega^2 x
C) a = -omega x
D) a = omega x
Answer: A [a = -omega^2 x.]

Q: The time period of a simple pendulum depends on:
A) Length of the pendulum
B) Mass of the bob
C) Amplitude of oscillation
D) Both B and C
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(L/g). It depends only on length and g.]

Q: The time period of a spring-mass system depends on:
A) Mass and spring constant
B) Amplitude
C) Length of spring
D) Gravity
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(m/k).]

Q: For a spring-mass system, if the mass is doubled, the time period becomes:
A) sqrt(2) times
B) 2 times
C) 4 times
D) 1/sqrt(2) times
Answer: A [T ∝ sqrt(m). So T' = sqrt(2) T.]

Q: For a spring-mass system, if the spring constant is doubled, the time period becomes:
A) 1/sqrt(2) times
B) 2 times
C) sqrt(2) times
D) 4 times
Answer: A [T ∝ 1/sqrt(k). So T' = T/sqrt(2).]

Q: The acceleration due to gravity at a place is determined using:
A) Simple pendulum
B) Spring-mass system
C) Both A and B
D) None of these
Answer: A [Simple pendulum: g = 4pi^2 L / T^2.]

Q: In SHM, the phase difference between displacement and velocity is:
A) pi/2
B) pi
C) 2pi
D) 0
Answer: A [Velocity leads displacement by pi/2.]

Q: In SHM, the phase difference between displacement and acceleration is:
A) pi
B) pi/2
C) 2pi
D) 0
Answer: A [Acceleration and displacement are in opposite direction (phase diff = pi).]

Q: A particle executes SHM with amplitude A. The displacement at which kinetic energy equals potential energy is:
A) A / sqrt(2)
B) A / 2
C) A sqrt(2)
D) A / 4
Answer: A [0.5 k (A^2 - x^2) = 0.5 k x^2 -> A^2 = 2 x^2 -> x = A/sqrt(2).]

Q: A particle executes SHM with period T. The time taken to move from mean position to half the amplitude is:
A) T/12
B) T/6
C) T/4
D) T/8
Answer: A [x = A sin(omega t). A/2 = A sin(omega t) -> sin(omega t) = 0.5 -> omega t = pi/6 -> t = pi/(6*2pi/T) = T/12.]

Q: The motion of a particle in a circular path with uniform speed projected on a diameter gives:
A) SHM
B) Circular motion
C) Random motion
D) Linear motion
Answer: A [The projection of uniform circular motion on a diameter is SHM.]

Q: The restoring force in SHM is proportional to:
A) Displacement
B) Velocity
C) Acceleration
D) Frequency
Answer: A [F = -kx.]

Q: The velocity of a particle in SHM is zero at:
A) Extreme position
B) Mean position
C) Midway
D) All positions
Answer: A [At extreme position, x = A, velocity v = 0.]

Q: The velocity of a particle in SHM is maximum at:
A) Mean position
B) Extreme position
C) Midway
D) All positions
Answer: A [At mean position, x = 0, velocity v = omega A (maximum).]

Q: The acceleration of a particle in SHM is maximum at:
A) Extreme position
B) Mean position
C) Midway
D) All positions
Answer: A [At extreme position, a = -omega^2 A (maximum magnitude).]

Q: The acceleration of a particle in SHM is zero at:
A) Mean position
B) Extreme position
C) Midway
D) All positions
Answer: A [At mean position, x = 0, a = 0.]

Q: The total energy of a particle in SHM is proportional to:
A) A^2
B) A
C) A^3
D) 1/A
Answer: A [E = 0.5 k A^2.]

Q: The energy of a particle in SHM is:
A) Conserved
B) Dissipated
C) Increases with time
D) Decreases with time
Answer: A [In ideal SHM, total mechanical energy is conserved.]

Q: The time period of a simple pendulum of length 1 m is approximately:
A) 2 s
B) 1 s
C) 3 s
D) 0.5 s
Answer: A [T ≈ 2pi sqrt(1/10) ≈ 2s.]

Q: The time period of a spring-mass system on the moon is:
A) Same as on Earth
B) Greater than on Earth
C) Less than on Earth
D) Zero
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(m/k), independent of g.]

Q: The time period of a simple pendulum on the moon is:
A) Greater than on Earth
B) Same as on Earth
C) Less than on Earth
D) Zero
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(L/g). g_moon < g_earth, so T is greater.]

Q: A particle executes SHM with amplitude A and angular frequency omega. The maximum velocity is:
A) omega A
B) omega^2 A
C) A / omega
D) A / omega^2
Answer: A [v_max = omega A.]

Q: A particle executes SHM with amplitude A and angular frequency omega. The maximum acceleration is:
A) omega^2 A
B) omega A
C) A / omega
D) A / omega^2
Answer: A [a_max = omega^2 A.]

Q: The displacement of a particle in SHM is x = 0.5 sin(2t). The amplitude is:
A) 0.5
B) 2
C) 1
D) 4
Answer: A [Amplitude A = 0.5.]

Q: The angular frequency of SHM is:
A) 2pi / T
B) 2pi f
C) Both A and B
D) T / 2pi
Answer: C [omega = 2pi/T = 2pi f.]

Q: The phase constant in SHM determines:
A) The initial position and velocity
B) The amplitude
C) The frequency
D) The time period
Answer: A [Phase constant (phi) determines the initial state.]

Q: A pendulum clock runs fast. To correct it, the length of the pendulum should be:
A) Increased
B) Decreased
C) Kept same
D) Changed to zero
Answer: A [If clock runs fast, T is less than required. To increase T, length should be increased.]

Q: In SHM, the graph between velocity and displacement is:
A) An ellipse
B) A straight line
C) A parabola
D) A circle
Answer: A [v^2 = omega^2(A^2 - x^2) -> x^2/A^2 + v^2/(omega^2 A^2) = 1 (ellipse).]

Q: A body oscillates with SHM. At displacement x, the kinetic energy is K. The potential energy is:
A) 0.5 k A^2 - K
B) K
C) 0.5 k x^2
D) 0.5 k A^2 + K
Answer: A [Total energy = K + U. U = Total - K = 0.5 k A^2 - K.]

Q: The time period of oscillation of a liquid column in a U-tube is:
A) 2pi sqrt(h/g)
B) 2pi sqrt(L/g)
C) 2pi sqrt(m/k)
D) 2pi sqrt(g/h)
Answer: A [T = 2pi sqrt(h/g), where h is height of liquid column.]

Q: The frequency of a particle in SHM is 5 Hz. The time period is:
A) 0.2 s
B) 0.5 s
C) 2 s
D) 5 s
Answer: A [T = 1/f = 1/5 = 0.2 s.]
