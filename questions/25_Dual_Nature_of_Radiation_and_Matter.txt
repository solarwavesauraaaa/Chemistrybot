# 25_Dual_Nature_of_Radiation_and_Matter

Q: The photoelectric effect demonstrates the:
A) Particle nature of light
B) Wave nature of light
C) Dual nature of light
D) Quantum nature of light
Answer: A [Photoelectric effect shows light behaves as particles (photons).]

Q: The energy of a photon is given by:
A) E = h f
B) E = h / f
C) E = f / h
D) E = h c f
Answer: A [E = h f = h c / lambda.]

Q: The momentum of a photon is:
A) p = h / lambda
B) p = h lambda
C) p = h / c
D) p = c / h
Answer: A [p = h / lambda = h f / c.]

Q: The work function of a metal is:
A) The minimum energy required to eject an electron
B) The maximum energy of an ejected electron
C) The kinetic energy of an electron
D) The potential energy of an electron
Answer: A [Work function (phi) is the minimum energy to remove an electron.]

Q: The threshold frequency is:
A) The minimum frequency of light required for photoelectric emission
B) The maximum frequency of light required for photoelectric emission
C) The frequency of the incident light
D) The frequency of the emitted electron
Answer: A [Threshold frequency f0 is the minimum frequency to cause emission.]

Q: The photoelectric equation is given by:
A) K_max = h f - phi
B) K_max = phi - h f
C) K_max = h f + phi
D) K_max = h f / phi
Answer: A [Einstein's photoelectric equation: K_max = h f - phi.]

Q: The stopping potential is:
A) The potential required to stop the most energetic photoelectrons
B) The potential required to accelerate photoelectrons
C) The potential of the anode
D) The potential of the cathode
Answer: A [Stopping potential (V0) stops electrons with maximum kinetic energy.]

Q: The relation between stopping potential and maximum kinetic energy is:
A) K_max = e V0
B) K_max = V0 / e
C) K_max = e / V0
D) K_max = e V0^2
Answer: A [K_max = e V0.]

Q: If the intensity of incident light is increased, the photoelectric current:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [Photoelectric current is directly proportional to intensity.]

Q: If the frequency of incident light is increased, the maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons:
A) Increases
B) Decreases
C) Remains constant
D) Becomes zero
Answer: A [K_max = h f - phi. Increasing f increases K_max.]

Q: The photoelectric effect is instantaneous because:
A) Energy transfer is a quantum process
B) Light travels fast
C) Electrons are loosely bound
D) The metal is a good conductor
Answer: A [The emission is instantaneous due to photon-electron interaction.]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of a particle is:
A) lambda = h / p
B) lambda = p / h
C) lambda = h p
D) lambda = h / (m v^2)
Answer: A [lambda = h / p = h / mv.]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated through a potential V is:
A) lambda = h / sqrt(2 m e V)
B) lambda = sqrt(2 m e V) / h
C) lambda = h / (2 m e V)
D) lambda = 2 m e V / h
Answer: A [lambda = h / sqrt(2meV).]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of a particle of mass m and kinetic energy K is:
A) lambda = h / sqrt(2 m K)
B) lambda = sqrt(2 m K) / h
C) lambda = h / (2 m K)
D) lambda = 2 m K / h
Answer: A [lambda = h / sqrt(2mK).]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of a particle is inversely proportional to:
A) Momentum
B) Energy
C) Velocity
D) Mass
Answer: A [lambda = h / p, so inversely proportional to momentum.]

Q: The dual nature of matter was proposed by:
A) de Broglie
B) Einstein
C) Planck
D) Compton
Answer: A [de Broglie proposed the wave nature of matter.]

Q: The Davisson-Germer experiment confirmed:
A) The wave nature of electrons
B) The particle nature of electrons
C) The wave nature of light
D) The particle nature of light
Answer: A [Davisson-Germer experiment showed electron diffraction, confirming wave nature of electrons.]

Q: The energy of an electron in the nth orbit of a hydrogen atom is:
A) E_n = -13.6 / n^2 eV
B) E_n = 13.6 / n^2 eV
C) E_n = -13.6 n^2 eV
D) E_n = 13.6 n^2 eV
Answer: A [E_n = -13.6 / n^2 eV.]

Q: The radius of the nth orbit of a hydrogen atom is:
A) r_n = 0.529 n^2 A
B) r_n = 0.529 n A
C) r_n = 0.529 / n^2 A
D) r_n = 0.529 / n A
Answer: A [r_n = 0.529 n^2 A (Bohr radius).]

Q: The velocity of an electron in the nth orbit of a hydrogen atom is:
A) v_n = 2.18 × 10^6 / n m/s
B) v_n = 2.18 × 10^6 n m/s
C) v_n = 2.18 × 10^6 / n^2 m/s
D) v_n = 2.18 × 10^6 n^2 m/s
Answer: A [v_n = 2.18 × 10^6 / n m/s.]

Q: The angular momentum of an electron in the nth orbit is:
A) L = n h / 2pi
B) L = h / 2pi n
C) L = n h
D) L = h / n
Answer: A [L = n h / 2pi (Bohr's quantization condition).]

Q: The energy of a photon of wavelength 500 nm is (h = 6.626e-34 J s, c = 3e8 m/s):
A) 3.98 × 10^-19 J
B) 3.98 × 10^-16 J
C) 3.98 × 10^-22 J
D) 3.98 × 10^-25 J
Answer: A [E = h c / lambda = 6.626e-34 * 3e8 / 5e-7 = 3.98e-19 J.]

Q: The momentum of a photon of wavelength 500 nm is:
A) 1.325 × 10^-27 kg m/s
B) 1.325 × 10^-24 kg m/s
C) 1.325 × 10^-30 kg m/s
D) 1.325 × 10^-21 kg m/s
Answer: A [p = h / lambda = 6.626e-34 / 5e-7 = 1.325e-27 kg m/s.]

Q: The number of photons per second emitted by a 100 W source of 500 nm light is:
A) 2.51 × 10^20
B) 2.51 × 10^18
C) 2.51 × 10^22
D) 2.51 × 10^24
Answer: A [n = P / E = 100 / 3.98e-19 = 2.51e20.]

Q: The work function of a metal is 2 eV. The threshold wavelength is (hc = 1240 eV nm):
A) 620 nm
B) 1240 nm
C) 310 nm
D) 2480 nm
Answer: A [lambda0 = hc / phi = 1240 / 2 = 620 nm.]

Q: A photon of energy 4 eV falls on a metal with work function 2.5 eV. The maximum kinetic energy of emitted electrons is:
A) 1.5 eV
B) 2.5 eV
C) 4 eV
D) 6.5 eV
Answer: A [K_max = 4 - 2.5 = 1.5 eV.]

Q: The stopping potential in the above case is:
A) 1.5 V
B) 2.5 V
C) 4 V
D) 6.5 V
Answer: A [V0 = K_max / e = 1.5 V.]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of an electron moving with speed 10^6 m/s is (h = 6.626e-34, m = 9.1e-31 kg):
A) 0.728 nm
B) 7.28 nm
C) 0.0728 nm
D) 72.8 nm
Answer: A [lambda = h / mv = 6.626e-34 / (9.1e-31 * 1e6) = 7.28e-10 m = 0.728 nm.]

Q: The de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated through 100 V is:
A) 0.123 nm
B) 0.0123 nm
C) 1.23 nm
D) 12.3 nm
Answer: A [lambda = h / sqrt(2meV) = 6.626e-34 / sqrt(2 * 9.1e-31 * 1.6e-19 * 100) = 6.626e-34 / sqrt(2.912e-47) = 6.626e-34 / 5.396e-24 = 1.228e-10 m = 0.1228 nm ≈ 0.123 nm.]

Q: The wavelength of a photon and a particle are equal. Their momentum is:
A) Equal
B) Different
C) Zero
D) Infinite
Answer: A [p = h / lambda. If lambda is same, momentum is same.]

Q: The energy of a photon of frequency f is E. If the frequency is doubled, the energy becomes:
A) 2E
B) E/2
C) 4E
D) E/4
Answer: A [E = h f. If f doubles, E doubles.]

Q: The photoelectric current is proportional to:
A) Intensity of incident light
B) Frequency of incident light
C) Wavelength of incident light
D) Velocity of photoelectrons
Answer: A [Current is proportional to intensity (number of photons).]

Q: The kinetic energy of photoelectrons depends on:
A) Frequency of incident light

C) Wavelength of incident light
D) Velocity of incident light
Answer: A [K_max depends on frequency, not intensity.]

Q: The photoelectric effect was explained by:
A) Einstein

Answer: A [Einstein explained photoelectric effect using photon concept.]

Q: The energy of a photon is directly proportional to:

Q: The photon has:
