# 20_Electromagnetic_Induction

Q: The phenomenon of producing an induced emf due to a changing magnetic flux is called:
A) Electromagnetic induction
B) Electrostatic induction
C) Magnetic induction
D) Mutual induction
Answer: A [Electromagnetic induction is the process of producing emf by changing flux.]

Q: The SI unit of magnetic flux is:
A) Weber
B) Tesla
C) Gauss
D) Henry
Answer: A [Weber (Wb) is the SI unit of magnetic flux.]

Q: 1 Weber is equal to:
A) 1 T m^2
B) 1 T / m^2
C) 1 T m
D) 1 T / m
Answer: A [1 Wb = 1 T m^2.]

Q: The magnetic flux through a surface is given by:
A) Phi = B . A = B A cos(theta)
B) Phi = B × A
C) Phi = B / A
D) Phi = B + A
Answer: A [Phi = B A cos(theta).]

Q: Faraday's first law of electromagnetic induction states that:
A) An emf is induced when magnetic flux linked with a coil changes
B) The induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of flux
C) The induced emf opposes the change in flux
D) The induced emf is zero
Answer: A [Faraday's first law: emf is induced when flux changes.]

Q: Faraday's second law of electromagnetic induction states that:
A) The induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of flux
B) An emf is induced when flux changes
C) The induced emf opposes the change in flux
D) The induced emf is zero
Answer: A [Faraday's second law: |E| = dPhi/dt.]

Q: Lenz's law states that:
A) The direction of induced emf is such that it opposes the change in flux
B) The induced emf is proportional to flux
C) The induced emf is proportional to time
D) The induced emf is zero
Answer: A [Lenz's law: induced current opposes the change in magnetic flux.]

Q: The induced emf in a coil is given by:
A) E = -N dPhi/dt
B) E = N dPhi/dt
C) E = -N Phi / dt
D) E = N Phi dt
Answer: A [E = -N dPhi/dt.]

Q: The unit of rate of change of magnetic flux is:
A) Volt
B) Weber
C) Tesla
D) Henry
Answer: A [V = dPhi/dt.]

Q: The motional emf induced in a conductor moving in a magnetic field is:
A) E = B l v
B) E = B l / v
C) E = B v / l
D) E = l v / B
Answer: A [E = B l v.]

Q: The direction of induced current in a conductor moving in a magnetic field can be found using:
A) Fleming's right-hand rule
B) Fleming's left-hand rule
C) Right-hand thumb rule
D) Lenz's law
Answer: A [Fleming's right-hand rule gives direction of induced current.]

Q: The self-inductance of a coil is defined as:
A) L = N Phi / I
B) L = N I / Phi
C) L = Phi / I
D) L = N Phi I
Answer: A [L = N Phi / I.]

Q: The SI unit of self-inductance is:
A) Henry
B) Weber
C) Tesla
D) Gauss
Answer: A [Henry (H) is SI unit of inductance.]

Q: The self-inductance of a solenoid is:
A) L = mu0 N^2 A / l
B) L = mu0 N A / l
C) L = mu0 N^2 / A l
D) L = mu0 N A l
Answer: A [L = mu0 N^2 A / l.]

Q: The self-inductance of a coil depends on:
A) Number of turns, area, length, and permeability
B) Current
C) Voltage
D) Resistance
Answer: A [L depends on geometry and number of turns.]

Q: The energy stored in an inductor is:
A) U = 0.5 L I^2
B) U = L I^2
C) U = 0.5 L I
D) U = L I
Answer: A [U = 0.5 L I^2.]

Q: The mutual inductance between two coils is defined as:
A) M = N2 Phi_2 / I1
B) M = N1 Phi_1 / I2
C) M = L1 L2
D) M = L1 / L2
Answer: A [Mutual inductance M = N2 Phi_2 / I1.]

Q: The SI unit of mutual inductance is:
A) Henry
B) Weber
C) Tesla
D) Gauss
Answer: A [Henry (H) is SI unit of mutual inductance.]

Q: The mutual inductance between two coils depends on:
A) Their geometry, number of turns, and separation
B) The current in the primary coil
C) The voltage across the primary coil
D) The resistance of the secondary coil
Answer: A [M depends on geometry and coupling.]

Q: The coefficient of coupling (k) between two coils is:
A) k = M / sqrt(L1 L2)
B) k = M / (L1 L2)
C) k = sqrt(L1 L2) / M
D) k = M L1 L2
Answer: A [k = M / sqrt(L1 L2).]

Q: The eddy currents are:
A) Induced currents in a bulk conductor
B) Currents in a coil
C) Currents in a wire
D) Currents in a solenoid
Answer: A [Eddy currents are induced loops of current in bulk conductors.]

Q: Eddy currents are minimized by using:
A) Laminated cores
B) Solid cores
C) Air cores
D) Copper cores
Answer: A [Laminations reduce eddy currents.]

Q: Eddy currents are used in:
A) Induction furnaces and speedometers
B) Generators
C) Motors
D) Transformers
Answer: A [Eddy currents have applications in furnaces and speedometers.]

Q: The AC generator works on the principle of:
A) Electromagnetic induction
B) Mutual induction
C) Self-induction
D) Faraday's law
Answer: A [AC generator uses electromagnetic induction.]

Q: The transformer works on the principle of:
A) Mutual induction
B) Self-induction
C) Electromagnetic induction
D) Faraday's law
Answer: A [Transformer works on mutual induction.]

Q: The transformer equation is:
A) V_s / V_p = N_s / N_p
B) V_s / V_p = N_p / N_s
C) V_s * V_p = N_s * N_p
D) V_s / V_p = I_s / I_p
Answer: A [V_s / V_p = N_s / N_p.]

Q: For a step-up transformer:
A) N_s > N_p
B) N_s < N_p
C) N_s = N_p
D) N_s = 0
Answer: A [In step-up transformer, secondary turns > primary turns.]

Q: For a step-down transformer:
A) N_s < N_p
B) N_s > N_p
C) N_s = N_p
D) N_s = 0
Answer: A [In step-down transformer, secondary turns < primary turns.]

Q: The efficiency of a transformer is:
A) Output power / Input power
B) Input power / Output power
C) Output voltage / Input voltage
D) Input voltage / Output voltage
Answer: A [Efficiency = P_out / P_in.]

Q: The self-inductance of a coil is 2 H. The energy stored when a current of 3 A flows is:
A) 9 J
B) 6 J
C) 18 J
D) 3 J
Answer: A [U = 0.5 L I^2 = 0.5 * 2 * 9 = 9 J.]

Q: A coil of 100 turns has a magnetic flux of 0.01 Wb. If the flux changes to 0.05 Wb in 0.1 s, the induced emf is:
A) 40 V
B) 20 V
C) 10 V
D) 5 V
Answer: A [E = -N dPhi/dt = -100 * (0.05 - 0.01)/0.1 = -100 * 0.4 = -40 V. Magnitude = 40 V.]

Q: A conductor of length 2 m moves with a speed of 5 m/s in a magnetic field of 0.2 T. The motional emf is:
A) 2 V
B) 0.5 V
C) 1 V
D) 4 V
Answer: A [E = B l v = 0.2 * 2 * 5 = 2 V.]

Q: The mutual inductance between two coils is 0.5 H. If the current in the primary changes from 2 A to 5 A in 0.3 s, the induced emf in the secondary is:
A) 5 V
B) 3 V
C) 10 V
D) 8 V
Answer: A [E = M dI/dt = 0.5 * (5-2)/0.3 = 0.5 * 3/0.3 = 5 V.]

Q: The self-inductance of a coil is proportional to:
A) N^2
B) N
C) 1/N
D) 1/N^2
Answer: A [L ∝ N^2.]

Q: The mutual inductance between two coils is maximum when:
A) They are close and coaxial
B) They are far apart
C) They are perpendicular
D) They are at 45°
Answer: A [M is maximum when coils are close and coaxially aligned.]

Q: The current in a coil changes from 2 A to 4 A in 0.1 s. The self-inductance is 0.5 H. The induced emf is:
A) 10 V
B) 20 V
C) 5 V
D) 15 V
Answer: A [E = L dI/dt = 0.5 * (4-2)/0.1 = 0.5 * 20 = 10 V.]

Q: The Lenz's law is in accordance with the law of conservation of:
A) Energy
B) Charge
C) Momentum
D) Mass
Answer: A [Lenz's law conserves energy by opposing the change.]

Q: A circular coil of radius 0.1 m and 50 turns is placed in a magnetic field of 0.2 T. The magnetic flux through the coil is:
A) 0.314 Wb
B) 0.628 Wb
C) 0.157 Wb
D) 0.0785 Wb
Answer: A [Phi = N B A cos(0) = 50 * 0.2 * pi * 0.01 = 0.314 Wb.]

Q: In an inductor, the current lags the voltage by:
A) 90°
B) 45°
C) 180°
D) 0°
Answer: A [In inductor, voltage leads current by 90°, so current lags voltage by 90°.]

Q: The power factor of an ideal inductor is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) 0.5
D) 0.707
Answer: A [Power factor = cos(90°) = 0.]

Q: The induced emf in a coil is proportional to:
A) Rate of change of magnetic flux
B) Magnetic flux
C) Area of the coil
D) Number of turns
Answer: A [E ∝ dPhi/dt.]

Q: The back emf in a motor is maximum when:
A) The motor runs at maximum speed
B) The motor starts
C) The motor is stalled
D) The motor is switched off
Answer: A [Back emf is proportional to speed; max at max speed.]

Q: An inductor of 2 H is connected to a 12 V DC source. After a long time, the current is:
A) Limited only by the resistance
B) Infinite
C) Zero
D) E / L
Answer: A [In DC steady state, inductor acts as a short circuit (zero resistance).]

Q: A coil has a resistance of 5 Ω and inductance of 0.2 H. The time constant is:
A) 0.04 s
B) 0.2 s
C) 0.5 s
D) 0.1 s
Answer: A [tau = L / R = 0.2 / 5 = 0.04 s.]

Q: The induced current in a coil is maximum when:

Q: The energy stored in an inductor of 2 H carrying 4 A is:
