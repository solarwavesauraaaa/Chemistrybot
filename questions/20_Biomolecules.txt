Q: Which of the following is not a carbohydrate according to the chemical definition?
A) Glucose
B) Fructose
C) Acetic acid
D) Sucrose
Answer: C [Explanation: Although it fits Cx(H₂O)y, acetic acid is not a polyhydroxy aldehyde/ketone.]

Q: The number of asymmetric carbon atoms in glucose is
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 6
Answer: B [Explanation: C2, C3, C4, C5 are chiral in open-chain form.]

Q: Which of the following is a non-reducing sugar?
A) Maltose
B) Lactose
C) Sucrose
D) Glucose
Answer: C [Explanation: Both anomeric carbons are involved in glycosidic linkage.]

Q: The product formed when glucose is treated with bromine water is
A) gluconic acid
B) saccharic acid
C) glucaric acid
D) fructose
Answer: A [Explanation: Mild oxidation of aldehyde group gives gluconic acid.]

Q: Mutarotation is shown by
A) glucose
B) sucrose
C) both A and B
D) starch
Answer: A [Explanation: Only reducing sugars with free anomeric carbon show mutarotation.]

Q: The number of amino acids obtained on complete hydrolysis of a protein is
A) always 20
B) always less than 20
C) may be more or less than 20
D) always more than 20
Answer: C [Explanation: Proteins may contain fewer or (rarely) modified amino acids beyond the standard 20.]

Q: Which of the following amino acids is optically inactive?
A) Alanine
B) Glycine
C) Serine
D) Phenylalanine
Answer: B [Explanation: Glycine has no chiral carbon (two hydrogens on α-carbon).]

Q: The secondary structure of proteins is stabilised by
A) peptide bonds
B) hydrogen bonds
C) ionic bonds
D) van der Waals forces
Answer: B [Explanation: α-helix and β-sheet are stabilised by intramolecular H-bonding.]

Q: Which of the following is a fibrous protein?
A) Albumin
B) Insulin
C) Keratin
D) Haemoglobin
Answer: C [Explanation: Keratin is a structural fibrous protein.]

Q: Denaturation of proteins involves the destruction of
A) primary structure
B) secondary and tertiary structure
C) only tertiary structure
D) all structures
Answer: B [Explanation: Peptide bonds (primary structure) remain intact; higher structures are disrupted.]

Q: The enzyme that catalyses the hydrolysis of peptide bonds is
A) amylase
B) lipase
C) pepsin
D) maltase
Answer: C [Explanation: Pepsin is a protease.]

Q: Which of the following bases is not present in DNA?
A) Adenine
B) Guanine
C) Uracil
D) Thymine
Answer: C [Explanation: DNA contains thymine; RNA contains uracil.]

Q: The number of hydrogen bonds between adenine and thymine is
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: B [Explanation: A–T pair has two H-bonds; G–C has three.]

Q: Which of the following is a fat-soluble vitamin?
A) Vitamin B₁
B) Vitamin C
C) Vitamin D
D) Vitamin B₁₂
Answer: C [Explanation: A, D, E, K are fat-soluble.]

Q: The deficiency of vitamin C causes
A) night blindness
B) scurvy
C) beriberi
D) rickets
Answer: B [Explanation: Classic deficiency disease of ascorbic acid.]

Q: Assertion (A): Sucrose is a non-reducing sugar.
Reason (R): The glycosidic linkage in sucrose is between C1 of glucose and C2 of fructose.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Both anomeric carbons are locked in the glycosidic bond.]

Q: The anomer of α-D-glucose is
A) β-D-glucose
B) α-L-glucose
C) β-L-glucose
D) α-D-fructose
Answer: A [Explanation: Anomers differ only at the anomeric carbon (C1).]

Q: Which of the following reagents is used for the oxidation of glucose to saccharic acid?
A) Br₂ water
B) HNO₃
C) Tollens' reagent
D) Fehling's solution
Answer: B [Explanation: Strong oxidation with nitric acid oxidises both aldehyde and primary alcohol groups.]

Q: The number of peptide bonds in a tetrapeptide is
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Answer: B [Explanation: n amino acids form (n-1) peptide bonds.]

Q: Which of the following is an example of a conjugated protein?
A) Albumin
B) Globulin
C) Haemoglobin
D) Keratin
Answer: C [Explanation: Haemoglobin has a prosthetic group (haem).]

Q: The secondary structure of DNA is stabilised by
A) peptide bonds
B) hydrogen bonds and hydrophobic interactions
C) only ionic bonds
D) disulphide linkages
Answer: B [Explanation: Base pairing (H-bonds) and base stacking stabilise the double helix.]

Q: Which of the following is a pyrimidine base?
A) Adenine
B) Guanine
C) Cytosine
D) both B and C
Answer: C [Explanation: Cytosine, thymine and uracil are pyrimidines.]

Q: The enzyme invertase hydrolyses
A) maltose
B) sucrose
C) lactose
D) starch
Answer: B [Explanation: Invertase (sucrase) converts sucrose into glucose + fructose.]

Q: Which of the following vitamins is synthesised in the human body by bacteria?
A) Vitamin A
B) Vitamin C
C) Vitamin K
D) Vitamin D
Answer: C [Explanation: Vitamin K is produced by intestinal bacteria.]

Q: The isoelectric point of an amino acid is the pH at which
A) it has maximum solubility
B) it has zero net charge
C) it migrates towards anode
D) it migrates towards cathode
Answer: B [Explanation: Zwitterion has equal positive and negative charges.]

Q: Which of the following is a reducing disaccharide?
A) Sucrose
B) Maltose
C) both A and B
D) none
Answer: B [Explanation: Maltose has a free anomeric carbon.]

Q: The number of chiral carbons in open-chain fructose is
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Answer: B [Explanation: C3, C4, C5 are chiral.]

Q: Which of the following statements is correct about enzymes?
A) They are highly specific
B) They are proteins
C) They lower activation energy
D) All of these
Answer: D [Explanation: All are characteristic properties of enzymes.]

Q: The base pairing in DNA is
A) A with G and T with C
B) A with T and G with C
C) A with C and G with T
D) A with U and G with C
Answer: B [Explanation: Complementary base pairing (Chargaff's rule).]

Q: Which of the following is a water-soluble vitamin?
A) Vitamin A
B) Vitamin D
C) Vitamin E
D) Vitamin B₂
Answer: D [Explanation: B-complex and C are water-soluble.]

Q: The product formed when glucose is treated with phenylhydrazine (excess) is
A) osazone
B) hydrazone
C) both A and B
D) oxime
Answer: A [Explanation: Excess phenylhydrazine gives glucosazone (same as fructosazone).]

Q: Which of the following proteins has a quaternary structure?
A) Myoglobin
B) Haemoglobin
C) Keratin
D) Insulin (monomer)
Answer: B [Explanation: Haemoglobin has four subunits.]

Q: The deficiency of vitamin D causes
A) scurvy
B) rickets
C) beriberi
D) night blindness
Answer: B [Explanation: Rickets in children, osteomalacia in adults.]

Q: Assertion (A): DNA is more stable than RNA.
Reason (R): DNA has deoxyribose and thymine instead of ribose and uracil.
A) Both A and R true and R explains A
B) Both true but R does not explain A
C) A true, R false
D) A false, R true
Answer: A [Explanation: Absence of 2'-OH and presence of thymine make DNA chemically more stable.]

Q: The linkage between two monosaccharide units in a disaccharide is
A) peptide bond
B) glycosidic linkage
C) phosphodiester bond
D) ester linkage
Answer: B [Explanation: Acetal-type bond between anomeric carbon and hydroxyl of another sugar.]

Q: Which of the following amino acids has a basic side chain?
A) Glycine
B) Alanine
C) Lysine
D) Aspartic acid
Answer: C [Explanation: Lysine has –(CH₂)₄NH₂ side chain.]

Q: The number of hydrogen bonds between guanine and cytosine is
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: C [Explanation: G–C pair has three H-bonds.]

Q: Which of the following is an example of a derived protein?
A) Albumin
B) Globulin
C) Peptone
D) Casein
Answer: C [Explanation: Peptones are partial hydrolysis products of proteins.]

Q: The sugar present in RNA is
A) ribose
B) deoxyribose
C) glucose
D) fructose
Answer: A [Explanation: RNA contains β-D-ribose.]

Q: Which of the following vitamins is also known as ascorbic acid?
A) Vitamin A
B) Vitamin B₁
C) Vitamin C
D) Vitamin D
Answer: C [Explanation: Chemical name of vitamin C is ascorbic acid.]

Q: The primary structure of a protein is determined by
A) hydrogen bonding
B) sequence of amino acids
C) disulphide bonds
D) hydrophobic interactions
Answer: B [Explanation: Covalent sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bonds.]

Q: Which of the following is a non-essential amino acid?
A) Valine
B) Leucine
C) Alanine
D) Lysine
Answer: C [Explanation: Alanine can be synthesised in the body.]

Q: The enzyme that converts starch into maltose is
A) maltase
B) amylase
C) invertase
D) lactase
Answer: B [Explanation: Salivary and pancreatic amylase hydrolyse starch.]

Q: Which of the following statements is correct about nucleic acids?
A) DNA is double-stranded, RNA is usually single-stranded
B) DNA contains thymine, RNA contains uracil
C) both A and B
D) none
Answer: C [Explanation: Fundamental differences between DNA and RNA.]

Q: The deficiency of vitamin B₁ (thiamine) causes
A) scurvy
B) beriberi
C) rickets
D) night blindness
Answer: B [Explanation: Classic deficiency disease of thiamine.]
